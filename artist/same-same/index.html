Same Same

Same Same was a pop duo consisting of identical twins Bob and Clint Moffatt, originally members of the Canadian boy band the Moffatts. Same Same was based in Thailand and they sold albums and performed mainly in Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, and the Philippines. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
