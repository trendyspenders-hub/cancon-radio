Shattered Silence

Shattered Silence is a brute force and poetic experience. With soul-cutting vocals and a fusion of rock riffs, emo melancholy, and the intensity of 2000s metal, the band tells the journey of accepting scars and fighting against the passage of time. Their songs are anthems for those who feel strange in their own skin but refuse to give up. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
