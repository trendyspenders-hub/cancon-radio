Stu Brooks

Stu Brooks (also known as Stu "Bassie" Brooks) (born July 7, 1978) is a Canadian bassist, composer, and producer. He is a co-founder of the band Dub Trio. He is known for playing and recording with various artists and music bands, including Saturday Night Live Band, Lady Gaga, Mike Patton, and Nine Inch Nails. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
