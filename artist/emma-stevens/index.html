Emma Stevens

British singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist Emma Stevens was bought her first guitar at the tender age of three after being utterly captivated by her mother’s playing. Sadly Emma’s mother passed away in 2012 and, having been Emma’s inspiration, she is now the driving force behind Emma’s incredible work ethic and unwavering determination. www.emmastevensmusic.com Source: the artist's YouTube channel
