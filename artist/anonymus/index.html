Anonymus is a Canadian thrash metal band from Montreal, Quebec, founded in 1989. Its members are Carlos Araya (drums), Oscar Souto, Daniel Souto (guitar) and Jef Fortin (guitar). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
