Nomadic Massive is an independent Canadian hip-hop collective based in Montreal. The group formed in 2004, and has since performed globally. The fundamentalist ensemble consists of multi-instrumentalists and rotates members depending on the song. The members include rappers, singers, keyboardists, saxophonists, trumpeters, trombonists, guitarists, bassists, and drummers. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
