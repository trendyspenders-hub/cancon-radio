Ariane Mahrÿke Lemire is a Canadian bilingual francophone singer-songwriter from Edmonton, Alberta. Her sound is a mix of folk, jazz, blues, and electroacoustic . She regularly sings in both English and French. Lemire won a Western Canadian Music Award in 2008 and 2012. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
