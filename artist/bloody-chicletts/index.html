Bloody Chicletts

Bloody Chicletts was a Canadian new wave, power pop band based in Vancouver. Band members were guitarist/vocalist Glen Reid, keyboardist Devin Reschny, bassist Chad Reid, and drummer Gabe Tracey. They were signed to BMG Music Canada and released one album, 1996's Presenting...Bloody Chicletts. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
