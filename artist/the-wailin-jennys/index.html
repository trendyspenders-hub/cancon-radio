The Wailin' Jennys

BandcampOfficial site

The Wailin' Jennys are a Canadian music group. A 2011 review says they "sing like siblings and play like seasoned veterans". They have released several albums and received two Juno Awards. The group has been featured several times on the American Public Media program A Prairie Home Companion. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
