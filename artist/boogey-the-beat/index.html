Boogey The Beat looks to bring together all of his “Cousins” for his first official debut project. Mostly reserved for describing someone of blood-relation, the term “cousin” in Indigenous communities can mean anything but ie. It means to be part of an extended family, it means to be on the lookout for one another and to take care of each other – regardless of your colonial surname.
