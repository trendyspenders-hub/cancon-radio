Aliocha Schneider

Aliocha Schneider (French pronunciation: [aljɔʃa ʃnɛdɛʁ]; born 21 September 1993) is a French-Canadian actor and musician who was born in Paris, France, but grew up in Quebec, Canada. He is most noted for his roles in the films Aurelie Laflamme's Diary (Le Journal d'Aurélie Laflamme), Closet Monster and Pompei. He acted in several plays including the role of Momo in the stage adaptation of The Life Before Us with the Théâtre du Rideau Vert. As a musician, he released his debut album, Eleven Songs, in 2017. His second album, Naked, followed in 2020. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
