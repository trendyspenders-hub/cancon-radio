Jann Arden (born Jann Arden Richards; March 27, 1962) is a Canadian singer-songwriter, author and actress. She is best known for her signature ballads, "Could I Be Your Girl" and "Insensitive", which is her biggest hit to date, as well as other ballads, such as "Cherry Popsicle" and "I Would Die for You". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

8 wins, 16 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
