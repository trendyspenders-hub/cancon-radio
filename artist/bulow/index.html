Megan Bülow, known professionally as Bülow, is a German-Canadian pop singer who had success with her debut EP Damaged Vol. 1. She also co-wrote Beyoncé's Billboard Hot 100 number-one single "Texas Hold 'Em", which earned her two nominations at the Grammy Awards, including Song of the Year. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
