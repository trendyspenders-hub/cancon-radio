Ripcordz

Official site

Ripcordz are a Canadian punk rock band, formed in Montreal in 1980. They have released 15 full-length albums and continue to tour extensively as a three-piece band. The band has undergone numerous lineup changes through its history, with vocalist and guitarist Paul Gott as the only remaining original band member. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
