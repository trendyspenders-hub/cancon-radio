Conor Patton better known by his stage name Conro, is a Canadian DJ and record producer, based in Kelowna, British Columbia. He is best known for his song "Therapy" with over 31 million streams on Spotify and as an artist of the Canadian record label Monstercat. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
