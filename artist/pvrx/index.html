Pvrx

Daniel Dove (born October 25, 1994), professionally known as Pvrx ( Pie-Rex), is a Canadian rapper, singer, and songwriter. Pvrx is known for his storytelling about growing up in the rough surroundings in government housing projects. He signed to Def Jam Recordings in 2017. He released his debut EP 3.14 on July 26, 2019.
