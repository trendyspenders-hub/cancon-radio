No More Moments

Bandcamp

No More Moments have been delivering their brand of gritty, anthemic punk since 2009. Known for their raw energy, and honest lyrics, they've toured extensively across Turtle Island, sharing stages with legends like Propagandhi, Agent Orange, Metz, and Cancer Bats, all while building a following along the way. Now armed with a refreshed lineup of Calgary punk veterans, NMM is gearing up to release Then and Now, a bold statement of growth and survival. With lots of shows on the horizon, and no stop in sight, these OG's are ready to shred. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
