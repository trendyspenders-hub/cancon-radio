Martyr

Martyr is a Canadian technical death metal band from Trois-Rivières, Québec. Their style consists of progressive use of melodics and variations of thrash and death metal. In June 2026, Martyr announced the remixed and remastered reissues of their first three studio albums on Relapse Records. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
