Thundermug

Thundermug was a Canadian rock band formed in London, Ontario, in 1970. They were active from 1970 to 1976 and again from 1991 to 2001. They charted three top 50 Canadian singles and released five studio albums. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
