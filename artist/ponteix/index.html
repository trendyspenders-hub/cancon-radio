Ponteix condenses hypnotic grooves influenced by RnB and psychedelic indie pop. Mario Lepage sings brightly above saturated electronic motifs, a warm blanket of synthesizers and buttery bass lines. With nostalgic lyrics sung mostly “en Français”, Ponteix explores language barriers while delivering to its audiences what is undeniably central to music, emotion.
