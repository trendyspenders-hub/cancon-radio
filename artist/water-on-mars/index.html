Water on Mars

Water on Mars is a psychedelic rock and electronic music group from Quebec City, founded in 2004. The music trio is led by Philippe Navarro, guitarist, vocalist, arranger, producer, principal lyricist, and music composer. He is joined by drummer Sylvain Harvey and bassist Benoit L'Allier Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
