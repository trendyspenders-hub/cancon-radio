FM Radio Gods

FM Radio Gods is the live electronica act of two artists: Andrew Hamilton and Tao-Nhan Nguyen, both from Montreal, Quebec. In 2008, their track "See You Later Oscillator", the result of a collaboration between FM Radio Gods and Ali Emad, won the Roland Synthesizer Contest. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
