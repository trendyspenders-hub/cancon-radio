Michael Vincent

Michael Vincent (born 1976) is a music journalist, media executive, and composer, who splits his time between Toronto, Ontario and Moncton, New Brunswick. Since 2014, he has been publisher and editor-in-chief of Ludwig Van (formally Musical Toronto), and CEO of Museland Media Inc. He was a freelance music critic for the Toronto Star, and also a composer of works which combine electronic and traditional instrumentation, as well as his work with spoken word, which includes a full-length opera Generation X with text by the Canadian Author Douglas Coupland.
