Noah

NOAH is an Indonesia Alternative-Rock band from Bandung, West Java. NOAH's current line-up consists of Ariel (Vocal), Lukman (Guitar) and David (Keyboard). NOAH was formed in 2012 after the disbandment of Peterpan. On 15-16 September 2012, NOAH held a concert in five countries in 24-hour span in Melbourne, Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, and Jakarta. With this concert, NOAH broke another Indonesian Wolrd Records Museum (MURI) record. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
