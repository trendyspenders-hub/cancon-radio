Elle Kay

With a folk-rock sound that takes on an extra edge live, Elle Kay is a Nashville-based singer-songwriter from Washington, DC. Her 2022 EP, “hi my name is,” spins musical stories about enduring friendships, a life’s romance lived in one night, and the influence of John Lennon in her own music journey. Elle is a classically trained vocalist majoring in songwriting at Belmont University. She loves and is heavily influenced by Fleetwood Mac and Joni Mitchell. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
