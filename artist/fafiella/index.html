Fafiélla

Fafiélla is an alternative soul songstress from Sint Maarten who transforms life's hardships into healing anthems. Drawing from neo-soul pioneers like Erykah Badu and R&B icons like Aaliyah, she weaves her Caribbean roots and Latin childhood influences into a sound uniquely her own. Her music is an intimate journey through vulnerability, observing growth from pain with unflinching honesty. With vocals shaped by island rhythms and Latin melodies, Fafiélla creates space for others to find strength in their struggles.
