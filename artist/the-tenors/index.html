The Tenors (formerly known as The Canadian Tenors) are a vocal group consisting of Victor Micallef, Clifton Murray, Eduardo Aguirre, and Mark Masri. They perform operatic pop music that is a mixture of classical and pop, featuring songs such as "The Prayer", Panis angelicus, and Leonard Cohen's Hallelujah. Originating from Canada, Micallef and Masri from Toronto, Aguirre from Costa Rica, and Murray from Port McNeill, the Tenors have performed on more than 60 international television programs.
