Fresh Snow are a Canadian instrumental post-rock band from Toronto, Ontario. They are most noted for their 2013 album I, which was a longlisted nominee for the 2014 Polaris Music Prize. Formed in 2010, the band consists of guitarist and keyboardist Tim Condon, bassist and keyboardist Andy Lloyd, guitarist Brad Davis and drummer Jon Maki. They first united to play a show at Duffy's Tavern in Toronto; the following year they released their first recording, a 7" split single with METZ.
