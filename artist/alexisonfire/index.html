Alexisonfire is a Canadian post-hardcore band formed in St. Catharines, Ontario, in 2001. The band's members are Dallas Green, Wade MacNeil, George Pettit (vocals), Chris Steele (bass) and Jordan Hastings. The band has won numerous awards, and in Canada their albums have all been certified either gold or platinum. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
