Perfume Tree

BandcampOfficial site

Perfume Tree is a band from Vancouver, British Columbia that existed from 1991 until December 1999, before becoming active again in November of 2024. Members were Jane Tilley, Bruce Turpin and Peter Lutwyche The band performed mostly psychedelic electronic rock. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
