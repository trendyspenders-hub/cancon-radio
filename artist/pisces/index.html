Pisces

Straight outta Rockford, IL Pisces a cool loose affiliation of musicians who traversed the wild terrain of 60's psychedelic rock. Comprised of dynamic, churning percussion woozy electronic textures. Found sound samples, and a little bit of Joni, Joplin and Slick from vocalist Linda Bruner.
