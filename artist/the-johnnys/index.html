The Johnnys

The Johnnys were an Australian pub rock band from Sydney. The band was formed in 1982 and is still active today. The band combines country and punk musical styles. Members are Graham Hood, Billy Pommer Jr and Slim Doherty and have included founder, Roddy Ray'da and Spencer P. Jones. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
