The Violet Archers

The Violet Archers is a Canadian indie pop band from Toronto. Led by Rheostatics bassist Tim Vesely, the band currently consists of Vesely and singer-songwriter Jane Gowan, with past members including Yawd Sylvester on guitar, drummer Camille Giroux, bassist Scott Remila, Ida Nilsen on piano.
