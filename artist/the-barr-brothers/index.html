The Barr Brothers is an indie folk band founded in Montreal, Quebec in 2006, consisting of two American brothers Andrew and Brad Barr, pedal steel guitarist Brett Lanier, and harpist Eveline Gregoire-Rousseau. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

3 wins, 16 nominations; certified gold in Canada
