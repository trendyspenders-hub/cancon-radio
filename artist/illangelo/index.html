Carlo Montagnese (born July 29, 1987), known professionally as Illangelo, is a Canadian DJ, record producer, songwriter and sound mixer, known for his early work with singer the Weeknd. Having executive produced his 2011 mixtapes compiled in his Trilogy (2012) album, Montagnese has served as a frequent contributor for several of his subsequent releases.
