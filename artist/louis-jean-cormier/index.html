Louis-Jean Cormier is a Canadian indie rock singer and songwriter. Formerly associated with the band Karkwa, since that band went on hiatus in 2012 he has recorded and performed as a solo artist and was a judge on the second season of the television singing competition La Voix. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

3 wins, 4 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
