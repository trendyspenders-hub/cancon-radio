Charlie Major

Charles Bernard Major (born December 31, 1954) is a Canadian country music artist. He has recorded seven studio albums and released more than twenty singles. In 2019 he was inducted into the Canadian Country Music Hall of Fame. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

10 wins, 3 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
