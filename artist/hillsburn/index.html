Hillsburn

Hillsburn was an indie pop band from Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. During their career they were hailed by several media outlets as one of the east coast of Canada's next big acts. Hillsburn released three full-length studio albums, and won a number of national and regional awards. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
