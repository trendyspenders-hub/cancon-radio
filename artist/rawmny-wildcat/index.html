RAWMNY WILDCAT

Rawmny Wildcat is a Toronto-based hip-hop and R&B artist blending soulful storytelling, sharp lyricism, and Afro-influenced sound rooted in his Ethiopian heritage. Known for his emotional authenticity and genre-fluid style, he bridges classic hip-hop with modern global rhythms, creating music that reflects identity, resilience, and lived experience. With a growing independent catalog and a reputation for hands-on artistry, Rawmny continues to build momentum through his own imprint, Wildvision Media. He is currently set to release his new project Habeshinated on May 22, 2026—marking a bold new chapter in his evolution as an artist. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
