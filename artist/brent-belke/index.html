Brent Belke

Brent Belke (born February 1, 1965, in Edmonton, Alberta) is a Canadian guitarist and composer. After playing in the punk and alternative rock bands SNFU and The Wheat Chiefs between 1981 and 1998, Belke began a career composing music for film and television. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
