Foxwarren is a Canadian indie rock band from Regina, Saskatchewan. Formed in 2009, Foxwarren has released three full-length albums. Foxwarren was nominated for the Alternative Album of the Year at the 2020 Juno Awards for their album Foxwarren. Their latest album, 2, was released on 30 May 2025 by Anti-. The current members of Foxwarren are Andy Shauf, brothers Avery and Darryl Kissick, Dallas Bryson, and Colin Nealis. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
