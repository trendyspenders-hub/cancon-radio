Stan Taylor

I'm Stan Taylor - singer, musician, and creator based in California. Music has been the through line of my life. I grew up on soul, R&B, rock and artists who made records feel human, and I've spent the years since putting my own spin on the songs I love while figuring out what I want to say with music of my own. This page is becoming a home for all of it - original music, covers, live sessions, piano, DJ sets, behind the scenes creative experiments and pieces of life along the way. I'm less interested in making everything perfect these days, and more interested in making things that feel honest. Thanks for listening and being here. -Stan Tayl Source: the artist's YouTube channel
