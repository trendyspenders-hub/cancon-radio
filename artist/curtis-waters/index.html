Curtis Waters

Abhinav Bastakoti (born December 20, 1999), known by his stage name Curtis Waters, is a Canadian-American musician, rapper, singer and songwriter. He is known for his 2020 debut single "Stunnin'". He is the first Nepal-born musician to attain international success. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
