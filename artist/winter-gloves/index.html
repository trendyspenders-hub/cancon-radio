Winter Gloves is a Canadian indie rock music group from Montreal. The band consists of Charles F. on vocals, Wurlitzer, organ, Vincent Chalifour on synth bass and keyboards, Patrick Sayers on drums and Nico Ormiston on guitars. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
