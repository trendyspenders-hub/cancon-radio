Andy Warren

Official site

Andy Warren is a Canadian independent musician. He currently resides in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. His music has been characterized as genre-crossing "Vancouver folk soul" and "an eclectic, Ben Harperish amalgam of poly rhythms laid over jazz, funk, soul and folk/roots elements". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
