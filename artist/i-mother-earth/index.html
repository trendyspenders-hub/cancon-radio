I Mother Earth, or IME, is a Canadian rock band. The band formed in 1990, reaching its peak popularity in the latter half of the 1990s. After an eight-year hiatus, it reunited in 2012. Between 1996 and 2016, they were among the top 150 best-selling Canadian artists and top 40 best-selling Canadian bands in Canada. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
