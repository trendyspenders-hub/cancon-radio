Georgia Murray

Georgia Murray is a Canadian singer-songwriter whose 2009 song "We'll Never Know" was plagiarized by composer Bahnus to create the song "Swing" (feat. LeeSang) for Korean pop star Lee Hyori. "Swing" became the number one song in Korea on April 4, 2010. Georgia was later compensated and given a writing credit from MNet Media, the Seoul-based entertainment company that had released the plagiarized song. Murray portrayed herself in an episode of the ABC show Boston Legal in its second season.
