11:30

11:30 was a Canadian dance-pop duo from Montreal that consisted of identical twin sisters Toni and Trish Sherwood (born in 1980; the 11:30 refers to their birthdate, November 30).They are known for their hit single, “Olé Olé”. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
