Sunny Malton

Sandeep Singh Sidhu, known as Sunny Malton, is a Canadian rapper and singer of Punjabi music. He rose to mainstream with his track 'Issa Jatt' with Sidhu Moose Wala. His song Levels with Sidhu charted at 2 on Billboard Hot 100. He is the co-founder of Brown Boys and TPM Records. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
