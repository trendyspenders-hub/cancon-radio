Porkbelly Futures

Porkbelly Futures is a Canadian blues music group based in Toronto, Ontario. The latest lineup of the band included lead singer Paul Quarrington (1953-2010), guitarist/harmonica player Stuart Laughton, bass player Chas Elliott (Toronto Symphony), and vocalist Rebecca Campbell.
