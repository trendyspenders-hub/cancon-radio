Irfan Makki

Irfan Makki (Urdu: عرفان مکی; born 30 October 1975) is a Pakistani-born Canadian singer-songwriter. He is known for his album, “I Believe”, and his collaborations with Maher Zain and Mesut Kurtis. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
