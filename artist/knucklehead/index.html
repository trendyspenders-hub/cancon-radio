Knucklehead

Official site

Knucklehead was a Canadian punk rock group formed in Calgary, Alberta, in 1994. Influenced by such punk groups as Bad Religion, Stiff Little Fingers, Cocksparrer, Billy Bragg, and Social Distortion. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
