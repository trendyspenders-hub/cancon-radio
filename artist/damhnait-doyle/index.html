Damhnait Doyle

Apple MusicOfficial site

Damhnait Doyle is a Canadian singer, composer and radio host. A phonetic spelling of her first name also serves as the title of her 2003 album Davnet. She was a member of Atlantic Canadian band Shaye from 2003–2009 with Kim Stockwood and Tara MacLean (2003–2007). Along with Blake Manning, Stuart Cameron and Peter Fusco, she formed a new band called The Heartbroken; they have been active since 2009. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
