Rebecca Black

Rebecca Renee Black (born June 21, 1997) is an American singer, songwriter, YouTuber, and DJ. She gained extensive media coverage when the music video for her 2011 debut single "Friday" went viral on YouTube and various social media sites. The song peaked at number 58 on the Billboard Hot 100. It received an overwhelmingly negative response from audiences and music critics, many of whom considered it among the worst songs of all time.
