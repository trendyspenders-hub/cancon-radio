Johnny Hollow

Official site

Johnny Hollow is a Canadian music group formed in 2001 in Guelph, Ontario. The band consists of singer Janine White, digital artist Vincent Marcone, cellist Kitty Thompson, and guitarist Steve Hiehn. Johnny Hollow was founded by Janine White & Vincent Marcone and the group often features Kitty Thompson. Their music combines organic instruments and electro-industrial sounds. Its genre is a form of avant-garde pop closely associated with steampunk and goth genres that features dark art. Their fans have supported several successful crowd-sourcing campaigns.
