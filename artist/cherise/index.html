Cherise

Cherise Roberts is an English singer and songwriter who is a member of the hip hop/R&B group Big Brovaz and the dance-pop duo Booty Luv a.k.a. Cherise & Nadia. She has been with both groups since they both started. Roberts released a solo album, Look Inside before the debut album Nu-Flow by Big Brovaz was released. It only spawned one single, "2nd Best", while the album won a MOBO award. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
