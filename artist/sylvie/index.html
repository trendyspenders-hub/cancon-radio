Sylvie is a Canadian indie rock music group from Regina, Saskatchewan. It features singer/guitarist Joel Passmore, formerly of the now-disbanded Despistado, alongside bass player Riva Farrell Racette, guitarist Chris Notenboom, keyboardist Erin Passmore, and drummer Jeff Romanyk. All band members also provide backing vocals. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
