Jonathan-Ismaël Diaby is a Canadian former professional ice hockey player, rapper and actor who was drafted by the Nashville Predators in the 2013 NHL draft, who played in the AHL, ECHL and LNAH. In his post-playing career, he is a rapper under the name JoDolo, and features in the TV show Shoresy, playing a fictionalised version of himself. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
