Dumb Numbers

Dumb Numbers is the musical project of Adam Harding. Harding selects musicians for each Dumb Numbers project from a large group of friends and collaborators.” Lou Barlow, Dale Crover, Bobb Bruno and others contributed to the first two studio albums. Dumb Numbers musical style has been described "swooning feedback pop". Source: the artist's YouTube channel
