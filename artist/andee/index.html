Andee is a Canadian singer-songwriter from Saint-Jean-Chrysostome, Quebec. She rose to fame as a finalist on the Quebecois singing competition Star Académie before pursuing a solo career in English and signing with Universal Music Canada in 2014. Her debut single, "Never Gone", was released May 19, 2014 and entered the Canadian Hot 100 at No. 94. Her debut studio album, Black and White Heart was released on May 26, 2015. Andee's latest EP, One, Plus One Equals Us, was released on March 26, 2021, under independent label Coalition Music Records. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
