Hilotrons is a Canadian indie pop band from Ottawa, Ontario. The band's members include Mike Dubue, Philip Shaw Bova, Pascal OfLaki, Philippe Charbonneau, and Alex Moxon. Adam Saikaley is an occasional member. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
