Robin-Joël Cool

Robin-Joël Cool is a Canadian actor and musician from Tracadie-Sheila, New Brunswick. He is most noted as a frequent collaborator with his wife Viviane Audet, both in the band Mentana and on film and television scores. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
