Lili

Lili is an indie folk/pop singer-songwriter from Metro Manila, Philippines. She launched her solo career this 2021 after several years playing keyboard and back-up vocals for her band, The Ransom Collective. Her upcoming album is a coming-of-age story largely inspired by her stay in Indonesia last year, where she got stuck during lockdown. It is there where she spent her time making new friends, learning to surf, and falling in love. Feeling inspired and finally taking the time to pursue her musical dreams, she came home to the Philippines in December 2020 to record her debut album “Sunchild”, which will be released in August this year.
