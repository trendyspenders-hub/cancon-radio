Tim Tamashiro

Tim Tamashiro is a Canadian jazz singer, radio broadcaster and speaker. Tim was the host of Tonic, a nightly jazz program on CBC Radio 2 from March 2007 until late June 2017. He became the full-time host of Tonic on April 2, 2012, after the retirement of weekday host Katie Malloch. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
