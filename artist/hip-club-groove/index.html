Hip Club Groove

Hip Club Groove was a Canadian hip hop band, active in the 1990s. Originally from Truro, Nova Scotia, and later based in Halifax, the band consisted of rappers Derek "MC" Mackenzie and Cory "Cheklove Shakil" Bowles, and DJ Brian "DJ Moves" Higgins. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
