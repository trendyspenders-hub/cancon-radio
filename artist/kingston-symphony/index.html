Kingston Symphony

Official site

The Kingston Symphony is a Canadian orchestra based in Kingston, Ontario. Since 2014 the principal conductor of the symphony has been Evan Mitchell. The ensemble performs most of its concerts at The Grand Theatre. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
