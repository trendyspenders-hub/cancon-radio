Mike Evin

Mike Evin is a Canadian singer-songwriter based out of Toronto, Ontario. He has relocated to several cities, including his original hometown of Montreal, and Halifax. Evin's primary instruments are the piano and keyboard, though also plays guitar and other instruments. Evin has released six albums and two EPs since 2001. His second album, I'll Bring The Stereo, was co-produced by Barenaked Ladies bassist Jim Creeggan, and his brother, former Barenaked Ladies keyboardist Andy Creeggan.
