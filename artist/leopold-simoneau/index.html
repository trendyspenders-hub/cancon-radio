Léopold Simoneau, (May 3, 1916 – August 24, 2006) was a French-Canadian tenor, one of the outstanding Mozarteans and interpreters of the French lyric repertoire of his time. In 1959 he became the first recipient of the Calixa-Lavallée Award. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
