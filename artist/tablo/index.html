Tablo

Daniel Seonwoong Lee (Korean: 대니얼 선웅 리; born July 22, 1980), better known by his stage name Tablo (타블로), is a Canadian rapper, songwriter and record producer based in South Korea. Tablo is best known as the leader and producer of South Korean hip-hop group Epik High, and the founder of independent music label Highgrnd, which housed bands Hyukoh and The Black Skirts. Tablo became employed as a lyricist while still in high school. Under the supervision of Tobias Wolff, Tablo graduated from Stanford University with honors in English literature and creative writing.
