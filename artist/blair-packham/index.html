Blair Packham

The Jitters were a Canadian rock band formed in Toronto, Ontario, in 1981 by Blair Packham, Danny Levy, and Matthew Greenberg. Five years of constant performing established them as one of the premier club bands in the Greater Toronto Area. They caught the attention of producer Bob Ezrin in 1982, but studio sessions with him did not produce any results.
