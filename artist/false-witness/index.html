False Witness

False Witness is a breakneck fusion of groove metal, death metal, and melodic metalcore. Taking the best from each genre, the band has managed to create a unique sound that appeals to a wide audience of heavy music enthusiasts. False Witness is: McKae Nielsen - Vocals + Guitar Connor Werling - Drums + Vocals Tyler Thurston - Vocals + Guitar Ryan Hall - Bass Source: the artist's YouTube channel
