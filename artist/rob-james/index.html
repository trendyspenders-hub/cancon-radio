Rob James

Rob James (born October 30, 1977) is a Canadian singer and songwriter from Winnipeg, Manitoba who is known for being a former member of the pop group McMaster & James and the seventh-place finalist in the 2006 season of Canadian Idol. In 1997, he formed McMaster & James with Luke McMaster, and released a certified Canadian Gold album with the group in 2000, featuring their hit singles "Love Wins Everytime", "Thank You", "I Understand", and "Sweet Sensation" while opening for pop artists like Christina Aguilera and 'N Sync.
