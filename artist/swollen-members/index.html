Swollen Members is a Canadian hip-hop group from Vancouver, British Columbia, consisting of rappers Madchild and Prevail, and producer Rob the Viking. Former group members include Zodak and Moka Only. Swollen Members have released eight studio albums, three compilation albums, two greatest hits albums, and one live album.
