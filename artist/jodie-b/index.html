Jodie B

From busking on the streets to performing at international festivals, Jodie B has opened for icons like DubFx, The Pharcyde, and Methodman & Redman. A live looping multi-instrumentalist and producer, she fuses indie, hip-hop, and soul into an organic, genre-bending sound. Co-owner and house engineer of Frequency Sound 528 — a female-led studio and creative hub — Jodie champions safe, collaborative spaces for artists to create and be heard. Her music speaks truth, balance, and perseverance — a sound rooted in authenticity and growth. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
