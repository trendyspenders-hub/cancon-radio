Ronald James Hawkins is a musician from Toronto who is best known as a member of the band The Lowest of the Low. He has also released music as a solo artist, and fronted the bands Ron Hawkins and the Rusty Nails and Ron Hawkins and the Do Good Assassins. He has produced albums for multiple independent bands and artists and is also an accomplished painter. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
