Finch

Finch is an American rock band from Temecula, California. The band is best known for their single "What It Is to Burn" from the album of the same name. Their second album, Say Hello to Sunshine, peaked within the top 30 on the Billboard 200. Following setbacks within the group, they released the album Back to Oblivion.
