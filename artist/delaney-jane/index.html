Delaney Jane is a Canadian singer and songwriter. She rose to prominence as a guest vocalist on numerous popular dance tracks including the Juno-nominated "Limitless" before starting her own independent label imprint Dirty Pretty Things. Her official debut solo single, "Bad Habits", was released in 2018 to commercial success. Jane released her debut 13-song studio album, Dirty Pretty Things, in 2019. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
