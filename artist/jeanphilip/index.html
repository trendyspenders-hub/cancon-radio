Jeanphilip

French electro-rock artist from Québec. Jeanphilip delivers a rich blend of French electro-rock, where sharp guitars collide with cinematic synths. Rooted in North America’s indie scene and tinted with European influences, his sound is modern and visceral. With emotionally honest and socially aware lyrics, his songs reflect today’s complex world. Active since 2005, he’s a mainstay in the Québec and French indie scenes, with five albums, multiple European tours, and over 300 shows under his belt. Once fully independent, he’s now expanding his artistic reach beyond borders. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
