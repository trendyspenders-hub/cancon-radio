Frais Dispo is the name chosen by Foreign Diplomats's he musicians to launch their very first album in French. Much more than a side project, Frais Dispo marks a new milestone in the careers of Élie Raymond, Antoine Lévesque-Roy, Thomas Bruneau Faubert, Charles Primeau and Antoine Gallois.
