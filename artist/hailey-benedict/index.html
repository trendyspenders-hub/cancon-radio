Hailey Benedict is a Canadian country music singer and songwriter from St. Albert, Alberta. She is currently signed to Big Loud Records and its imprint Local Hay. She has charted on Canada Country with the singles "Wanted You To", "Damn You July", and "Things My Mama Says". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
