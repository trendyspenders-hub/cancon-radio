Simon Collins

Official site

Simon Philip Nando Collins (born 14 September 1976) is a British drummer and the former lead vocalist of the progressive rock band Sound of Contact. Collins is the son of English drummer and singer Phil Collins and Collins' first wife, Andrea Bertorelli. He is the brother of actress Joely Collins and paternal half-brother of actress Lily Collins and drummer Nic Collins. Reviewers have compared his vocals to those of his father.
