Dallas Harms was a Canadian country music singer-songwriter. Twenty of Harms' singles made the RPM Country Tracks charts, including the number one single "Honky Tonkin' ". Harms was inducted into the Canadian Country Music Hall of Fame in 1989. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
