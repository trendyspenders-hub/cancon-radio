Crush

Crush is a Canadian band based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Leading band members are Paul Lamb and Cory Tetford, accompanied by Ronald J Hynes (bass) and Kris MacFarlane (drums). Past members included Santiago Serna, Scott MacFarlane and Brian Talbot. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
