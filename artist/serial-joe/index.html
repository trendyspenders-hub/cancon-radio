Serial Joe

Deezer

Serial Joe was a Canadian rock band from Newmarket, Ontario, which consisted of high school students Ryan Dennis (vocals, guitar), Ryan Stever (guitar), John Davidson (bass guitar), Dan Stadnicki (drums), and Jamie Fulcher (backing vocals). The group released four studio albums. Dennis stated that the band's name came from a girl with a speech disorder who would mispronounce the name "Sergio" as "Serial-Joe".
