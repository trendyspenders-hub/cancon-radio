George Zukerman

Official site

George Benedict Zukerman, (February 22, 1927 – February 1, 2023) was a Canadian bassoonist and impresario. He was the younger brother of the musicologist, Joseph Kerman. From 1980, he lived with his partner, violinist and teacher, Erika Bennedik, in South Surrey, B.C. He was the founder of White Rock Concerts. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
