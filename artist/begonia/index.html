Begonia

Alexa Dirks, also known by her stage name Begonia, is a Canadian pop singer and songwriter. She is most noted for her 2019 album Fear, which was longlisted for the 2020 Polaris Music Prize and shortlisted for the Juno Award for Adult Alternative Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2021.
