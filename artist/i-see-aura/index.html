A driving power house trio from the depths of southern Ontario, Canada. I See Aura blends and bends the rules of progressive metalcore/hardcore into a new sound. Adding melodic hooks with unforgettable grooves, this band will capture your eyes, ears and make you think deeper into your existence. With lyrics of enigmatic self actualization and cult inspiration, this artist has a dark yet meaningful message to the world. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
