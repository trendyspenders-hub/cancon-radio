Darrian Gerard

Darrian Gerard, a Canadian Pop-Rock virtuoso, crafts her own musical universe from the depths of her home studio in a small town in British Columbia, Canada. From learning guitar at 7 years old to seamlessly embracing bass, piano, drums, and vocals, Gerard's multi-instrumental prowess defines her artistry as she writes, records, and produces all of her own music.
