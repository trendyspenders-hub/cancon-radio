Charles Daellenbach

Conrad Charles Daellenbach C.M. ( DEL-ən-bahk; born July 12, 1945) is an American and Canadian tubist. He is best known as one of the founding members of the Canadian Brass, in which he remains the quintet's tuba player, publisher, business administrator and professional relationships manager. Daellenbach is one of the most recorded tuba performers in history. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
