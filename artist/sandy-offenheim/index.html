Sandy Offenheim

Official site

Sandy Offenheim's music was published by Berandol. In the 1970s, Sandy’s music initiated the rush into children’s pop music; followed by Sharon, Lois and Bram. Her works for children remain popular today as new generations of children discover Sandy. The CD Let's play ya statue Game again is a compilation of Sandy's most popular songs.
