The Golden Dogs

BandcampOfficial site

The Golden Dogs are a Canadian pop-rock band based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Formed in 2001, the band is known for their wild, energetic live shows and were labelled as one of the best live bands in Canada by Chart Magazine. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
