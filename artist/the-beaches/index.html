The Beaches are a Canadian rock band formed in Toronto in 2009 by Jordan Miller (lead vocals, bass), Kylie Miller (guitar, backing vocals), Leandra Earl (keyboards, guitar, backing vocals), and Eliza Enman-McDaniel (drums). The band released two EPs titled The Beaches (2013) and Heights (2014) before signing to Universal Records. In 2017, they released their debut album Late Show, which led to the band winning the 2018 Juno Award for Breakthrough Group of the Year. To follow their debut, they released two further EPs called The Professional (2019) and Future Lovers (2021). A 2022 compilation Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
