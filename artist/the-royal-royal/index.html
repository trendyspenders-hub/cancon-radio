The Royal Royal

The Royal Royal is a Canadian Christian contemporary worship music duo originating from Ontario, Canada. It is made up of two brothers, Gabriel and Nathan Finochio. The duo was active as of 2011 and is with Essential Records. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
