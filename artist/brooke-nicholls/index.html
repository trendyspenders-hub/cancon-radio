Brooke Nicholls is a Canadian contemporary worship music singer and songwriter. Since her debut album in 2016, she has been awarded ten Covenant Awards and been nominated for two Juno Awards. In 2024, she became the Worship Pastor of Village Church in Surrey, BC. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
