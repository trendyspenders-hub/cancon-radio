Evelyne Brochu

Bandcamp

Evelyne Brochu is a Canadian actress and singer-songwriter. In 2013, she became well known for her English-speaking role as Delphine Cormier, a French scientist on the hit Canadian science fiction thriller TV series Orphan Black. She has also released two studio albums as well as several singles. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
