Mato Wayuhi

Mato Standing Soldier, professionally known as Mato Wayuhi, is an Oglala Lakota actor, musician, producer, and composer from South Dakota, United States known for his studio albums and his original soundtrack for the television series Reservation Dogs. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
