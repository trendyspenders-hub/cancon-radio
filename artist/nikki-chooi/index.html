Nikki Chooi

Nikki Chooi (born January 31, 1989) is a Chinese-Canadian-American classical violinist. Chooi is currently concertmaster of the Buffalo Philharmonic Orchestra and Santa Fe Opera Orchestra. He is a prize winner of the Queen Elisabeth Music Competition and Tchaikovsky International Violin Competition and first-prize winner of the 2013 Michael Hill International Violin Competition, Montreal Symphony Manulife Competition, and Klein International Strings Competition.
