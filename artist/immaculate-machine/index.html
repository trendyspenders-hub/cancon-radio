Immaculate Machine

Immaculate Machine was a Canadian indie pop band from Victoria, British Columbia, active from 2001 to 2011. The band's name is taken from the lyrics of "One-Trick Pony" from the album One-Trick Pony by Paul Simon. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
