Shirley Gnome is a Canadian singer and comedian from Vancouver, British Columbia. Best known as a singer of comedy songs about taboo topics such as sexuality, she received a Juno Award nomination for Comedy Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2021 for her 2020 album Decoxification. Gnome grew up in Surrey, British Columbia, a place she describes as “a big place with a lot of contradictions. From the south you could smell the salty sea air. From the north you could hear gunshots and sirens". Her other albums have included Ho Down (2010), C*untry Music (2012), The Lady of the Night (2015) Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
