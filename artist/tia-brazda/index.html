Tia Brazda

Tia Brazda is a Canadian singer and songwriter, known for performing jazz music that incorporates elements of swing, blues, rockabilly, soul, electro and pop. She has been recognized as a notable figure in the electro-swing movement, and has performed at prominent international jazz festivals such as the Montreux Jazz Festival and the Festival International de Jazz de Montreal. Her 2015 album Bandshell was named one of the best of the decade in the Italian press. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
