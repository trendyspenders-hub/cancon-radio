Adam Saleh

Adam Mohsin Yehya Saleh (Arabic: آدم محسن يحيى صالح; born June 4, 1993) is an American YouTuber, singer, and crossover boxer. He gained prominence through his various YouTube channels, where he posts vlogs, comedy sketches, and social experiments. He began his career in 2012 as part of the collaborative YouTube channel TrueStoryASA, before transitioning into solo content creation.
