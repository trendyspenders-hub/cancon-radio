Ruth Minnikin

Official site

Ruth Minnikin is a Canadian singer-songwriter. She was formerly a member of Booming Airplanes, The Heavy Blinkers, The Guthries, and Reels, and now performs as Ruth Minnikin and her Bandwagon. She received nominations for Best Female Album and Best Folk Album at the 2007 Nova Scotia Music Awards. Minnikin has performed at the East Coast Music Awards, South by Southwest, and North by Northeast. Minnikin has collaborated frequently with other artists, and has contributed to 45 recordings.
