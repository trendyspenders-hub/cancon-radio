Allan Moon

Allan Moon is a Canadian singer-songwriter, musician, artist, poet and producer. Moon was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada and grew up in the New York City. In 1992, Moon emigrated to Tel Aviv, Israel. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
