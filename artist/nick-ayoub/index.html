Nick Ayoub

Nicholas ("Nick") Ayoub was a Canadian jazz saxophonist, oboist, English horn player, and composer. While he focused on jazz in his career, he also performed classical music at some points. He was born in Trois-Rivières, Quebec on September 7, 1926 and died on May 2, 1991. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
