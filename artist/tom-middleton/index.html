Tom Middleton

Tom Middleton is a Canadian pop singer from Victoria, British Columbia. Tom Middleton's first band, The Marquis, broke up in 1969. In the summer of 1971, Middleton approached Michael Easton, a local Victoria broadcaster, for help entering the recording industry. Easton became Middleton's manager, which led to the signing of a contract with Columbia records. Middleton recorded the album It Wouldn't Have Made Any Difference, which was released in May 1973.
