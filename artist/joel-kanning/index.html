Joel Kanning

Joel Kanning is a musician/producer best known for his enigmatic style of music. Besides releasing his own solo music, Joel has also produced remixes for artists including Michael Cretu [Enigma] and Eric Mouquet [Deep Forest]. In the studio, Joel has worked alongside musician/producer Jens Gad [Achillea, Enigma] and many other talented songwriters and producers. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
