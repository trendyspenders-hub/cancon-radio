The Evidence

The Evidence was a Canadian rock band from Calgary, Alberta whose sound was described as "precision-punk", "progressive-post-punk", and "gentleman's metal". The band was a power trio consisting of members Dean Rud (bass, vocals), Tyler Pickering (guitar, vocals), and Casey Lewis (drums, vocals). It released three albums, and three music videos.
