Timothy Wallace, better known by his stage name Timbuktu, is a Canadian underground hip hop artist and member of the Backburner crew. He is a former member of the group Toolshed, and a member of the groups Wolves, Swamp Thing and My Giants. He also makes up one half of the duos Teenburger and Sequestrians.
