Monowhales were a Canadian alternative rock band from Toronto, Ontario. Originally called Ginger Ale & the Monowhales, their lineup consisted of lead vocalist Sally Shaar, drummer Jordan Circosta, and guitarist Zach Zanardo. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
