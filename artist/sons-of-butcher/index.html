Sons of Butcher

BandcampOfficial site

Sons of Butcher is a Canadian adult animated comedy television series based on the rock band of the same name. It is animated using a variety of programs, put together using Adobe Flash. The band's music fuses vulgar absurdist comedy with rock music in a style that is described as "mock rock". Their band alter-egos are the focus of an animated television series developed by S&S Productions and aired on Canadian animation network Teletoon's programming-block network The Detour On Teletoon. Several of the band's songs were included in the soundtrack for the 2007 film Pigs, along with a cameo by Trevor Ziebarth, and his brother Jay Ziebarth. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
