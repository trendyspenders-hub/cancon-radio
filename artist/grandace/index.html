GrandAce

GrandAce (aka Jody Jones II) is an artist/producer from Cincinnati/Dayton, Ohio that has made Joy his revolution. Specializing in creating Alternative Hip Hop / Cosmic Groove music, his music serves the purpose of spreading light and building a platform for honest expression. Having a background in film, he combines vivid imagery and expressive language to craft sonic worlds that reflect his reality. Largely self-taught, he produces and engineers his own music drawing influence from a variety of genres from Jazz to House to create an ethereal groove. contact/booking - theegrandace@kodagrand.com Source: the artist's YouTube channel
