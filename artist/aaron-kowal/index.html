Aaron Kowal

Aaron Kowal is an enigmatic Canadian pop act who blends emotive songwriting with high-energy, dance-driven performance and a mysterious electronic sound. With influences in electropop and dancepop, his artistry is thematic, visual and provocative, and his narratives touch on the psychology of a generation. Aaron returns with a collection of songs addressing trust, uncertainty, and the anxieties of the age. A transition from the upbeat, playful sound of his earlier catalog, this upcoming body of work represents a new era for the artist - a more open and honest sound that embodies contemporary pop music in it’s truest form.
