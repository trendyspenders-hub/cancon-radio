Teagan Littlechief

Teagan Littlechief is a proud Cree Saulteaux woman from Saskatchewan. Winner of the last Queen's jubilee, and the only Indigenous person to sing the Canadian national anthem in Cree, English and French for the Opening of GreyCup. A country singer with a background in good old Rock n Roll. Teagan's Brand and sound are the perfect combination of high energy rock, and storytelling country. 2025 #SaskMusic #Indigenous Contemporary Artist of the Year. SCMA Indigenous Artist of the Year 22,23-24. Teagan has played some of the largest Festival stages across Canada bringing the power of her voice and the stories of her people.
