347aidan

Aidan Fuller (born June 8, 2003), known professionally as 347aidan, is a Canadian rapper and singer-songwriter from Cambridge, Ontario. He is most noted as a two-time Juno Award nominee at the Juno Awards of 2022, receiving nods for Breakthrough Artist of the Year and Fan Choice. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
