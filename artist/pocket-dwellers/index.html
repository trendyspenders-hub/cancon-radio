Pocket Dwellers

The Pocket Dwellers are a Canadian seven-member experimental hip-hop group from the Toronto area. The band's main genre is hip-hop, but their music is influenced by jazz, funk, soul, and breakbeat. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
