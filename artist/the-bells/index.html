The Bells

The Bells, or The Five Bells, were a Canadian soft rock band from Montreal, active from 1964 to 1974. They released five albums and several singles, two of which were major hits in the early 1970s, "Fly Little White Dove Fly" and "Stay Awhile". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
