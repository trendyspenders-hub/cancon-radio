dbClifford

Daniel Clifford (born 1979), better known by his stage name dbClifford, is a multi instrumentalist, singer, composer and producer of French and English descent. Born in France, he previously lived in Los Angeles, Victoria, Toronto, London and currently resides in Bergerac. His debut album, Recyclable, was released in 2007. The first single, "Simple Things", was a top 40 hit on Canadian, French and Dutch Hot AC radio and his second single "Don't Wanna" was a number-one hit in Japan.
