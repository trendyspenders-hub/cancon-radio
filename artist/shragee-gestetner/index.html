Shragee Gestetner

Shraga Eliyahu "Shragee" Gestetner (1986 – April 30, 2021) was a Canadian-American Hasidic singer and songwriter. He released two albums and composed songs for other singers before leaving the music industry in 2014. He died in the 2021 Meron crowd crush. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
