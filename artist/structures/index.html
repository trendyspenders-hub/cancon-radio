Structures (stylized as STRUC/TURES) is a Canadian metalcore band from Toronto, formed in 2009. Their current line-up consists of guitarists Spyros Georgiou and Brendon Padjasek, who also performs vocals and bass, and drummer Andrew McEnaney. They have released two studio albums: Divided By (2011) and Life Through a Window (2014). After reforming in 2021, they released an EP titled None of the Above. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
