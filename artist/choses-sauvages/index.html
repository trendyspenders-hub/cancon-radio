Choses Sauvages is a Canadian dance-punk group from Montreal, Quebec. They are most noted for their 2025 album Choses Sauvages III, which was longlisted for the 2025 Polaris Music Prize. The band consists of Félix Bélisle on vocals, bass and keyboards, Marc-Antoine Barbier on vocals, guitars and keyboards, Tommy Bélisle on vocalists and keyboards, Thierry Malépart on vocals, guitars and keyboards, and Philippe Gauthier Boudreau on vocals, drums and percussion.
