Gilad

GILAD is an award-winning Israeli-Kenyan singer-songwriter whose Swahili love songs have earned him the titles "King of Love" and "Balozi wa Mapenzi." A two-time AFRIMA winner, he is known for timeless hits, heartfelt storytelling, and collaborations with some of East Africa's biggest artists. From love songs and country-inspired anthems to music rooted in faith, family and the Kenyan countryside, GILAD continues to share his unique journey through song. Karibu.
