Jesse Giddings

Jesse Giddings is a Canadian television host, producer, musician, actor, former exotic dancer, artist and photographer born in Langley, British Columbia and went to high school at Langley Secondary School. He was formerly a VJ starting in 2009 at MuchMusic, a music television station in Canada and later a host on E! News in Los Angeles. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
