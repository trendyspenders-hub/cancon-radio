Serge Fiori

Serge Fiori (March 4, 1952 – June 24, 2025) was a Canadian musician who was the lead vocalist and guitarist for Harmonium, a progressive rock band from Quebec. After Harmonium broke up, he pursued a solo career. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
