Taya Lebel

Taya Lebel is a country-rock artist from Prince Albert, SK, with a captivating, powerful voice and an impressive vocal range. She’s performed at the CCMA’s, SCMA’s, and many other festivals and venues across Saskatchewan. A 2022 SCMA Emerging Artist nominee, she’s now writing and recording, her latest single, "Didn't Get Far" is out now. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
