Carol Welsman is a Canadian jazz pianist who accompanies her own easy listening, conversational style singing. She is the granddaughter of the founder and first conductor of the first Toronto Symphony Orchestra, Frank Welsman, and is the sister of composer John Welsman. She has been nominated six times for a Juno Award, Canada's equivalent to the Grammy. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
