Ryan Mitchel Chassels, known professionally as Freddie Dredd, is a Canadian rapper, singer, producer and songwriter. He gained prominence on TikTok with several viral songs including "GTG", "Opaul", "Limbo" and "Cha Cha". Dredd is a member of the music collective Sixset and his music is released with RCA Records and Doomshop. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
