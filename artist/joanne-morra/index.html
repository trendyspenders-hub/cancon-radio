Joanne Morra

Welcome et bienvenue to my channel! My name is Joanne Morra, I've been singing for over two decades in and around Toronto in both official languages. My first love in terms of singing is jazz, most notably ballads à la Sarah Vaughan, but I've been known to belt classic funk tunes in an 8 piece band and am currently singing traditional tunes, folk, blues and originals. I'm a huge fan or metal and heavy rock, Quebecois "chansons à boire et à répondre" and anything that makes my booty move. I don't believe I need to stick to one genre, my motto is "I sing songs I love". For more info please check out my site joannemorra.com Source: the artist's YouTube channel
