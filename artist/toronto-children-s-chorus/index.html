Toronto Children's Chorus

The Toronto Children's Chorus (TCC) is a leading Canadian children's choir based in Toronto. It was founded in 1978 by Jean Ashworth Bartle. The group has close to 350 members aged 6 to 18. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
