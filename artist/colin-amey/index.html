Colin Amey

Colin Amey (born Dunnville, Ontario) is a Canadian country music artist. Amey has released three studio albums which include 1998's Colin Amey, 2000's What My Heart Don't Know and 2006's Getaway. Two of Amey's singles reached the Top 20 on the RPM Country Tracks chart in Canada, "I Wish She Was Mine" and "What My Heart Don't Know." Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
