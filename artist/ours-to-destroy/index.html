Ours to Destroy

Ours To Destroy is a Canadian folk rock band from Calgary. The current line-up is David Morley, Steven Dodd, and Roland Griffith. The band's name is taken from Jeff Tweedy's reference to their music "We made it, it's ours to destroy" in the Wilco documentary I Am Trying to Break Your Heart on the making of Yankee Hotel Foxtrot. The band formed in 2004 and released their first eponymous full-length CD Ours To Destroy in 2006. The CD has received favorable reviews for its experimental nature and exploration of genres. Ours to destroy toured in 2008, culminating in Toronto's NXNE Festival Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
