Parlour Panther

Parlour Panther is a nonbinary-led indie rock band from Vancouver known for rich vocal harmonies, lush guitar tones, and spacey synths. Their 2024 album BLOOM earned praise from Exclaim!, CBC, and Range. Exclaim! said it “boasts a vibrant mix of blues-inflected vocals, playful synths and an energetic low-end...” BLOOM is out now. Parlour Panther is currently writing new music with release plans for 2026/27. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
