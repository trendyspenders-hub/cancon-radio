Kim Esty

Kim Esty is a Canadian Eurodance musician. She is best known for her 1993 song, "Summer in the Street" which reached number 5 on the Canadian RPM Dance chart. She is also known for collaborating with The Boomtang Boys on two singles, the top 10 hit, "Squeeze Toy" and the top 30 hit, "Pictures". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
