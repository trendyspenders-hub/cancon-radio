Kee Avil

Kee Avil blends guitar, voice, and electronic production into deconstructed songs shaped by avant-pop and experimental folk. Her first albums, ‘Spine’ (2024) and ‘Crease’ (2022), were Bandcamp Album of the Day and received praise from The Wire, The Quietus, Le Devoir, BBC6, Line of Best Fit, and Exclaim!. As a compelling voice in experimental songcraft, Kee Avil evokes PJ Harvey, Eartheater, Shygirl, SOPHIE, and Lucretia Dalt, where Juana Molina meets Autechre. Her third album ‘Vapor’ will be released throughout 2026 on NNA Tapes. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
