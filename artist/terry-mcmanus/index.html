Terry McManus

Terrence McManus (1946 – December 18, 2021) was a Canadian singer-songwriter who is known for launching the Songwriters Association of Canada. He was also an artists manager representing The Birthday Massacre, The Essentials, "Survivorman" Les Stroud, and Canadian experimental artist JoJo Worthington. As an educator he taught at Fanshawe College in London, Ontario, in the Music Industry Arts program for over 30 years, and taught online at Algonquin College in their Music Industry Arts program.
