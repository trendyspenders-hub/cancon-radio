Factor

Graham Murawsky (born March 28, 1982), better known by his stage name Factor Chandelier (formerly Factor), is a Canadian underground hip-hop producer from Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. He is a founder of Off Beat Production and Side Road Records. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
