Bouvier Normal

Bouvier Normal is a magnet attracting two opposite poles. They mesh Indy Bouvier’s silky vocal stacks with Mike Normal’s creeping distortion and unapologetic blasting drums. Bouvier Normal is an exploration of new sonic territories with lyrics that reference codependency, loneliness, depression, and social anxiety. One might believe that they are pessimists. Quite the contrary, at the end of their moody tunnel is always their love for each other and their music, shining brighter than ever. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
