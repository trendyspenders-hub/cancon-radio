Spiral Beach

Official site

Spiral Beach was a Canadian indie rock band, formed in Toronto, active from 2003 to 2009. The group consisted of vocalist and guitarist Airick Woodhead, vocalist and keyboardist Maddy Wilde, bassist Dorian Wolf, and drummer Daniel Woodhead. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
