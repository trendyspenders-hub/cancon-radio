Ted Leonard

Ted "Teddy" Leonard is a Canadian blues musician, multi-instrumentalist, producer and vocalist. He is best known for his long standing association with the Canadian blues band Fathead and has been a working professional musician since 1977. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
