Kristen Martell

Kristen Martell is a singer-songwriter from Nova Scotia whose music transcends boundaries and connects deeply with her audience. Known for her feathery, soothing vocals and heartfelt lyrics, Kristen weaves together intimate and moving songs that linger long after the music fades. After two successful English studio albums, numerous award nominations and accolades, Kristen returns with a new chapter—an enchanting French album that pays tribute to her Acadian roots on March 28, 2025. Her music is a tender exploration of love, identity, and the beauty of the human experience, carried on soft melodies that are as light and ethereal as her voice.
