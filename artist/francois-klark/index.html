Francois Klark

Official Francois Klark Music Channel Francois Klark is a South African born, Juno Nominated Canadian singer-songwriter and producer. The Toronto based Universal Music Award Winner has shared the stage with such acts as Grammy winner Jon Bellion. Francois' songs have been recorded by Hollywood Records artist Sofia Carson, and his music features in the Netflix film 'Feel The Beat' and HBO's 'The Good Doctor'.
