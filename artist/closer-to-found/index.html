Closer to Found

Closer to Found is a Canadian metalcore band from Montreal, Quebec, formed in 2014 by Lawrence Leylekian in the aftermath of his departure from Of Atlantis. Their current lineup consists of Lawrence Leylekian (Lead Vocals), Zackari Bourgeois (Clean Vocals), Azalée Baillargeon (Bass guitar), and Yoan Desforges (Drums). To date, the band has released their debut album Always Just As Lost along with three Music Videos. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
