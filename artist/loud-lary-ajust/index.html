Loud Lary Ajust

Loud is the stage name of Simon Cliche Trudeau, a Canadian rapper from Quebec. He is a relationship with actress Catherine St-Laurent. Formerly associated with the band Loud Lary Ajust, he released his debut solo recording Une année record in 2017. His tour to support the album included his first concert performances in France, resulting in positive attention from the influential French music magazine Les Inrockuptibles. In 2018, he collaborated with Cœur de pirate on the track "Dans la nuit" from her album En cas de tempête, ce jardin sera fermé.
