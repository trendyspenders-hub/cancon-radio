Emanuel

Emanuel was an American post-hardcore band from Louisville, Kentucky. It was formed in 1998 under the lesser-known name Emanuel Nice and appeared on four self-released records, before being signed to Vagrant Records and changing the name in 2004. Since then, Emanuel has released a debut album, Soundtrack to a Headrush, and a second album Black Earth Tiger. According to Alternative Press, the band's style has been emulated by newcomers in the years following their breakup. They are considered by some to be a "scene band".
