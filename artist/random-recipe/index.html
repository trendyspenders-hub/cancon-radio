Random Recipe is a Canadian musical group based in Montreal, Quebec. The band members are Frannie Holder, Fab, Vincent Legault, and Liu-Kong Ha. Although the band performs and records primarily in English, many of their songs contain Spanish and Italian lyrics. The band has toured in North and South America and Europe, and has released four albums of pop/folk/rap music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
