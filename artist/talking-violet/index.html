Talking Violet

Talking Violet (Windsor, ON | est. 2018) melds the best elements of shoegaze, grunge, and dream-pop creating a bold emotional “dreamo” statement in their sophomore record "Everything At Once". Produced and mixed by engineer Justin Meli (Universal Canada) and mastered by the iconic Heba Kadry (Slowdive, Bon Iver, Haley Williams etc.) the lively sophomore album packs a memorable punch with tracks like "It's Gonna Be Good" and the release's title track "Everything At Once".
