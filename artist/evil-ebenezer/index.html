Gaelan Anthony Bleasdale, better known by his stage name Evil Ebenezer, is a Canadian rapper and actor from Vancouver, British Columbia. His song "Told You So" won Best Music Video at the 2006 Leo Awards and he has been nominated five times at the Western Canadian Music Awards. He also performed at the Breakout West Festival in 2014 and 2015. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
