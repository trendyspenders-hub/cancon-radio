Terry Sumsion

Terry Sumsion (February 7, 1947 – March 26, 2011) was a Canadian country singer. He received a Juno Award nomination for Country Male Vocalist of the Year at the Juno Awards of 1985. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
