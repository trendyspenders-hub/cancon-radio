Kevin Chen

Kevin Chen (born 7 March 2005) is a Canadian classical pianist. He was the second prize winner at the XIX International Chopin Piano Competition in 2025. He is also the winner of the 2023 Arthur Rubinstein International Piano Master Competition in Tel Aviv and the winner of the 2022 Geneva International Music Competition. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
