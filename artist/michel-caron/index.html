Michel Caron

Robert Laforest (born September 5, 1942), known professionally as Michel Caron, is a French Canadian musician-keyboardist-singer. In his teenage years, he had singing classes with Lucille Dumont and later voice pitch classes with Eliane Catella. He record his first disk, with the help of Pierre Nolès, under the label Carol containing the songs “Dis-moi” and “Je t'ai dit” in 1960. Unfortunately, Fleur de Lys company, the owner of Carol, went bankrupt. At 17 years old, Michel meets Georges Tremblay and together they record a second disk with the record label company London.
