Ragheb Alama

Ragheb Sobhi Alama (Arabic: راغب صبحي علامة; born 7 June 1962) is a Lebanese music artist and television personality. Alama began his career in the 1980s when he appeared as a contestant on the talent show broadcast Studio El Fan, on which he received the Platinum Award. Alama rose to fame after winning Studio El Fan, since then releasing yearly albums and number-one hit singles such as "Ya Rait" (If Only), "'Aan Jad" (Seriously) and "Alby Asheq'ha" (My Heart Adores Her), which was the first Arabic song in history to be made into a music video.
