Suad Bushnaq

Suad Lakišić Bushnaq is a Jordanian-Canadian film and concert composer. She is best known for her work on Secret World of Sound with David Attenborough, Yunan, Flight 404, Hobal, Hanging Gardens, Crashing Eid, Hotel Beyrouth, Al Munataf (The Curve), Twice Upon a Time and Tight Spot.
