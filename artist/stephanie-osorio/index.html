Stephanie Osorio

SpotifyOfficial site

Born on the Colombian Caribbean coast in the city of Cartagena, she is a professional songwriter and singer of various Latin American musical styles. With her voice, she has participated as a singer in many musical projects in Colombia and Canada and is currently the lead vocalist and composer of the Afro-Colombian music group Bumaranga since 2012. Throughout her musical career, Stephanie has been invited to collaborate with several artists from the Quebec music scene and her voice has been used to create theme songs for movies and TV series from HBO, Netflix, Amazon.
