Ghostkeeper is a Canadian experimental pop/rock band led by Shane Ghostkeeper, who writes most of the songs, and includes members Sarah Houle, drummer Eric Hamelin and bassist Ryan Bourne. Their songs combine elements of 1960s girl-group melodies, country music, 1990s indie rock, African pop, and traditional Aboriginal pow wow music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
