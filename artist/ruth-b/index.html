Ruth Berhe is a Canadian singer and songwriter from Edmonton, Alberta. She started by singing songs on Vine in early 2013. In November 2015, she released her debut extended play (EP), The Intro. On May 5, 2017, she released her debut studio album, Safe Haven. It has gathered over 2.5 billion overall streams globally as of June 2024. Her double-platinum single "Lost Boy" has accumulated over 850 million streams on Spotify. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
