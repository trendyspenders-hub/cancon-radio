Jesse Rivest

Jesse Rivest (born 1977) is a Canadian singer-songwriter and guitarist. His hometown is Kelowna. He first surfaced in Calgary, and later was involved in music in New Zealand and Brazil. He currently divides his time between Canada and Brazil. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
