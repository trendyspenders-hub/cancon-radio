Mike Stevens

Official site

Mike Stevens is a Canadian harmonica player from Point Edward, Ontario. He is best known as a bluegrass musician. During his career he has had hundreds of performances at the Grand Ole Opry. He is also known for his work connecting creative artists with indigenous youth in isolated communities as part of the ArtsCan Circle. Stevens' harmonica style was described by one reviewer as "multivoiced and blindingly fast". Stevens entertained at the Central Canadian Bluegrass Awards festival in Huntsville, Ontario in 1998. Stevens often performs with American fiddle and banjo player Raymond McLain. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
