Alaclair Ensemble is a Canadian hip hop musical collective based in the province of Quebec. Their musical style draws on traditional Québécois folk music as well as contemporary hip hop music and electronic music. They are known for PR stunts and tongue-in-cheek lyrics that border on the comedic. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
