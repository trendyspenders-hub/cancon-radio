Inn Echo

Inn Echo crafts a cinematic, high-energy sound rooted in Celtic traditions, blending fierce fiddle, grooving guitar, and soaring cello. Their music pushes genre boundaries with original tunes and bold arrangements. It’s trad music turned on its head—rhythmic, dynamic, and endlessly evolving, like a late-night session that never wants to end. Musicians: Tuli Porcher - 5-String Fiddle & Cello Karson McKeown - Fiddle Tom Gammons - Guitar Source: the artist's YouTube channel
