Eilleen Regina "Shania" Twain is a Canadian singer and songwriter. She has sold more than 100 million records, making her one of the best-selling music artists of all time and the best-selling female artist in country music history. She received several titles including the "Queen of Country Pop". Billboard named her as the leader of the 1990s country pop crossover stars. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

38 wins, 16 nominations; certified diamond and gold and platinum in Canada
