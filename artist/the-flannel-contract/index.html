The Flannel Contract

The Flannel Contract is a professional, fun and energetic 3 piece group of oddballs from Penticton, BC, with a reputation for putting on a high octane show. Blending multiple genres into their style, you'll experience a cocktail of punk rock, grunge and hard rock with a dash of funk. Alternating lead vocalists & instruments lend a very unique diversity to the band's repertoire of original songs and select covers with a twist; feeling both nostalgic and current with the band's many musical inspirations. The Flannel Contract released their debut album "Sorry, Mom" earlier this year Source: the artist's YouTube channel
