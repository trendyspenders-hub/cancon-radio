Doreen Hume

Doreen Hume (born Edith Doreen Hulme; July 14, 1926 – July 2, 2022) was a Canadian soprano soloist who performed in North America and Europe from the 1940s through to the 1970s. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
