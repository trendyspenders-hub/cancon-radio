His breakthrough album ‘Universal Language’ squarely in the rearview; Simon is now deep in the pocket - crafting stripped-back house with groove, swing and staying power. New label [RECESS] Capturing the feeling of carefree fun with friends; the Recess imprint is delivering a range of house music for dance floors, and events of the same ethos. Showcases in Miami, London, Toronto, Brooklyn and Buenos Aires pave the way for more global Doty-led programming.
