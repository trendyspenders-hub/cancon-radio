Actors are a Canadian post-punk band from Vancouver, British Columbia, formed in 2012. The band consists of founder Jason Corbett, Shannon Hemmett, Kendall Wooding, and Adam Fink (drums). Their debut album, It Will Come To You, was released in 2018, followed by their second album, Acts of Worship, in 2021. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
