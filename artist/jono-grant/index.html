Jonothan Jonas Grant (born September 13, 1969) is a Canadian composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist who was nominated for a Genie Award for "Achievement in Music/Original Score" on his first film score Jacob Two Two Meets the Hooded Fang in 1999. He was also nominated for a Gemini award in the category of "Best Original Music Score for an Animated Program or Series" in 2006 for the first episode of Captain Flamingo, an animated series created by Breakthrough Films and Television. Grant has created scores for many television series and films from his personal studio Victory Drive Music Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
