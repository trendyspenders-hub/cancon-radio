Revenge is a Canadian war metal band formed in 2000 by James Read, following the collapse of his previous group, Conqueror. Revenge's black metal continues Read's previous band's direction. The band has rejected the term "war metal", instead calling themselves black metal or black death metal. They have released seven full-length albums. The band's lyrics explore anti-religion, misanthropy, self-ascension, and the collapse and rebuilding of mankind. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
