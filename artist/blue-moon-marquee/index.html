Blue Moon Marquee is a Canadian swing blues duo from British Columbia, consisting of vocalist and guitarist A.W. Cardinal and vocalist, bassist and drummer Jasmine Colette. They are most noted for their 2022 album Scream, Holler & Howl, for which they won the Juno Award for Blues Album of the Year, and were nominated for Contemporary Indigenous Artist of the Year, at the Juno Awards of 2024.
