Cunter (formerly known as Hunter) is a five-piece hardcore punk supergroup from Brampton, Ontario, whose members are from Moneen, Alexisonfire, BWC Studios, and The Abandoned Hearts Club. The band motto is "All intensity, all the time," and they are known for short, ferociously intense songs. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
