Smashing Satellites

Smashing Satellites was a Canadian alternative rock band formed in 2014 by Sal Costa, former lead guitarist of the band "My Darkest Days" that rose to fame in the late 2000s and early 2010s. The band is best known for its 2016 song "Gamblin' Man" and released one full-length studio album in 2015 and two EPs in 2014 and 2017. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
