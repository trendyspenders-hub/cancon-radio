Steven Jay Page (born June 22, 1970) is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and musician. He co-founded the rock band Barenaked Ladies with Ed Robertson in 1988 and served as one of its principal vocalists and songwriters until his departure in 2009. His compositions for the group include "Brian Wilson", "The Old Apartment", and "It's All Been Done"; he also co-wrote such songs as "If I Had $1000000" and "Pinch Me". Outside Barenaked Ladies, Page has released several solo albums and has composed music for productions at the Stratford Festival.
