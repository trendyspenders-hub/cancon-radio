Samuel Landry, also known by his stage name Sami Landri, is a Canadian drag artist and social media personality of Acadian descent. He initially gained fame after a 2021 video of him as Sami Landri asking "As-tu une cigarette?" went viral. Sami, along with Barbada de Barbades, co-hosted a documentary series Drag! d'la tête aux pieds on TFO. She placed joint third on the sixth season of Canada's Drag Race, as well as winning the Shark fan favourite award, and later returned to compete on the premiere season of Canada's Drag Race All Stars.
