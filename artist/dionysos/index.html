Dionysos

Dionysos was a French Canadian progressive rock band based in Salaberry-de-Valleyfield, Quebec, Canada. The founding members were Paul-André Thibert (vocals, harmonica, flute), Éric Clément (guitars), Jean-Pierre Legault (bass), André Mathieu (keyboards) and Robert Lepage (drums). They had a hit with their 1970 single "Suzie". The band is also known for being the first rock band from Quebec to write and play music sung entirely in "joual", the slang-influenced dialect of Quebec itself, an important step in Quebec culture.
