Himeka

Catherine St. Onge (born July 20, 1981), better known by her stage name Himeka (ヒメカ) (stylized as HIMEKA), is a Canadian anime song singer no longer active in Japan. While fluent in English and her native French, she is moderately proficient in Japanese. Her stage name, Himeka, comes from a character she created in a story she wrote when she was 13 years old.
