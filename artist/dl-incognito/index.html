Oliver Nestor, better known as DL Incognito, is a Canadian rapper and producer from Ottawa, Ontario. He is of Haitian and French-Canadian descent. He previously signed a distribution deal with Urbnet Records but also operated his independent label Nine Planets Hip Hop along with his brother. In 2007 and 2009, he was nominated for a Juno for Best Rap Recording. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
