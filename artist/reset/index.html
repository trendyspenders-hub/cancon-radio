Reset is a Canadian punk rock band formed in 1993 in Montreal, Quebec. The band was originally founded by Pierre Bouvier and Chuck Comeau when they were just 13 years old. Originally known as Roach, they changed their name to Reset after discovering another Canadian band already used that moniker. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
