Almost Through April

Almost Through April is an alternative rock band started in St. Catharines, Ontario, comprised of Frankie Turco (Bass), Simon Giulietti (Drums) and Zakk Milne (Guitar & Vocals). Music has always been a means of coping for them, and their songs attempt to break down their personal lives and experiences into art that speaks to the tragedy of the human condition. They hope you are able to find a piece of yourself in their work Source: the artist's YouTube channel
