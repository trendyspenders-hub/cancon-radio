Glenn Morrison is a Canadian DJ , record producer, musician and composer from Toronto, Ontario. He is best known for his tracks, "Contact", "No Sudden Moves", "Hydrology", "Circles" and “Goodbye”. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
