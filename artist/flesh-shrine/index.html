Flesh Shrine

Flesh Shrine is a death metal band from Montreal, Canada. Having shared the stage with giant acts such as Ingested, Signs Of The Swarm, Wormhole, Equipoise, and many more, Flesh Shrine delivers a fresh modern touch to the genre with hard hitting riffs, face melting gravity blasts, and an electrifying presence on stage making them a force to be reckoned with. Their latest release, "The Grand Apostasy", has received much praise and love from old and newcomers alike.
