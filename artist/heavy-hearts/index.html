Heavy Hearts is a Canadian alternative rock band from Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario, formed in 2013. The band has released three EPs and one studio album. Their sophomore album Room With a View was released on June 26, 2020. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
