Ol' Savannah

Ol' Savannah is a Montreal-based folk band known for its vocal harmonies and use of traditional Appalachian and Cajun instruments. Ol' Savannah has released six albums and two EPs, performed at a number of festivals, and toured in Canada, the US, and Europe. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
