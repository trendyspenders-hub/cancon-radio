Joey Kitson

Joey Kitson (born November 23, 1969) is a Canadian musician, best known as the lead singer of the Celtic rock band Rawlins Cross. Born in Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, Kitson is also a stage performer, with notable performances in the Charlottetown Festival productions of Anne of Green Gables: The Musical and Canada Rocks! Kitson first performed in a high school production of Bye Bye Birdie on the Charlottetown Festival stage.
