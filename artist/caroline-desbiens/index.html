Caroline Desbiens

Caroline Desbiens (French pronunciation: [kaʁɔlin debjɛ̃]) is a Canadian politician and singer who served as the member of Parliament for the riding of Beauport—Côte-de-Beaupré—Île d'Orléans—Charlevoix from 2019 to 2025 as a member of the Bloc Québécois. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
