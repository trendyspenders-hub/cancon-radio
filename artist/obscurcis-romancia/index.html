Obscurcis Romancia (Orchestral Black Death Metal Quebecois) Official Youtube Channel Obscurcis Romancia assaults listeners’ ears with their distinctive and powerful concoction of furious blast beats, vehement vocals, aggressive yet melodious riffs and leads, and fiendishly wicked atmospheres juxtaposed against a harmonious piano backdrop.
