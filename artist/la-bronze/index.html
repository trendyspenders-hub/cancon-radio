Nadia Essadiqi, better known by her stage name La Bronze, is a Moroccan-Canadian musician and actress. Although usually singing in French, she is currently best known for her Maghrebi Arabic version of the Stromae hit "Formidable". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
