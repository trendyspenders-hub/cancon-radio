Jodie Resther

Jodie Lynn Resther (born c. 1977) is a Canadian actress and singer. In the 1990s she played Kiki in the children's live-action show Are You Afraid of the Dark?, and has been involved in various dramas including Extra! Extra!, Vampire High, Undressed, and Fries With That. In cartoons, she is best known for voicing Francine in the PBS children's animated program Arthur. She also voiced Tecna in the Cinélume dub of Winx Club, and in Deus Ex: Human Revolution.
