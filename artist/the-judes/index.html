The Judes

The Judes are a Canadian indie rock band formed in 2007 in Winnipeg by Cory Pritchard (bass guitar, vocals) Jim Anderson (guitars, vocals), and Romi Topper (drums, vocals). Michael Dickson later replaced Topper on drums. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
