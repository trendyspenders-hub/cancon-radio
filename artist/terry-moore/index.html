Terry Moore

Terry Moore (1936 – September 24, 2018) was a Canadian broadcaster, actor, opera singer, best-selling author, and television personality. His career lasted 62 years and spanned several stations in Canada and a short stint during the late 1960s in New York City. Since the early 2000s, Moore was a regular on CFAX 1070. He was described as a showman, mentor, and larger than life presence with a great personality by colleague Adam Stirling, Joe Perkins, Mel Cooper, who originally hired him to CFAX, and others.
