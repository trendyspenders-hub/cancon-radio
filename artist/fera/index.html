Fera

Fera is a Canadian pop rock/singer-songwriter duo composed of twin sisters Courtney and Stephanie Fera. Over the past ten years Fera has recorded three albums: The Music Room (2005) – produced by Paul Airey (Michael Bublé); Familiar Feeling (2007) – produced by CCMA award-winner Tom McKillip (Aaron Pritchett, One More Girl); and a 2009 self-titled EP, produced by McKillip and Paul Shatto (The Higgins).
