Ceréna

Ceréna is a Canadian pop and electronic musician. She is most noted for her single "see", which was a Juno Award nominee for Dance Recording of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2022. Born in Toronto, Ontario, to immigrant parents from Colombia, Ceréna released music, including the full-length album House Arrest in 2018, as Andrés Sierra before coming out as transgender, and was one of the organizers of the Club Quarantine series of Zoom-based club nights for LGBTQ audiences during the COVID-19 pandemic.
