Desire is a Canadian electronic music duo from Montreal, based in Palm Springs, California. Formed in 2009, the band consists of vocalist Megan Louise and producer Johnny Jewel, a former member of Chromatics and Glass Candy. Nat Walker was formerly a member of Desire, playing synthesizer and drums. Their lyrics are in French and English. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
