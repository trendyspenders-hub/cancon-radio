Corrupt Leaders

Corrupt Leaders is a Canadian grindcore/crust band that formed in April 2013. They released two 7-inch EPs through Moshpit Tragedy Records (Phobia, Extreme Noise Terror, Doom, etc.) and one 7-inch on Give Praise Records. In early 2015, Corrupt Leaders released the four-song Grindmother EP, which features a guest vocal from frontman Rain Forest's 66-year-old mother. A video of the recording session went viral, and in April 2016, Grindmother released her own album Age of Destruction, featuring music by Rain Forest and Tyson Apex.
