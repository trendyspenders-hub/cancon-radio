Rebecca Foon

Bandcamp

Rebecca Foon is a Canadian cellist, vocalist, composer and visual artist. Foon currently records under her own name, as well as the alias Saltland, and is a member and co-founder of the modern chamber ensemble Esmerine as well as was a member of A Silver Mt. Zion. She also works with her sister Aliayta Foon-Dancoes. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
