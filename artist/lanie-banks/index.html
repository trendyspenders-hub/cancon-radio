Lanie Banks

Lanie Banks (born Micheal Osings, October 10, 1996) is a Canadian-Ugandan rapper, songwriter, community activist and music video director. He has performed alongside Pallaso, Davido and Tory Lanez. In December 2015, Osings began to record his songs and albums and launched his professional debut as a rapper. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
