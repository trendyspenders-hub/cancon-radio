Eve Parker Finley

Eve Parker Finley is a producer, multi-instrumentalist, media maker, and facilitator based in Montreal. Along with a rotating cast of talented friends, she combines classical influences with a love of electronic soundscapes, dance music, ambient, indie pop, and folk to create sonic safe spaces for emotional experiences.
