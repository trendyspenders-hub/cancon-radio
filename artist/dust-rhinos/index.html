Dust Rhinos

SoundCloud

This is the Video Channel for Winnipeg’s best Celtic Rock band. Using traditional airs, reels, and jigs the Rhinos conjure up the Ireland of olde and then, like a shillelagh wrapped in a potato sack, they knock the bejeebers out of that lovely image and unleash a freight train loaded with banshees & the furious ghost of Cuchulainn. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
