Metalwood is a Canadian jazz band from Toronto, Ontario. The band was active in the late 1990s and early 2000s, and reunited in 2016. The members are saxophonist Mike Murley, pianist/trumpeter Brad Turner, bassist Chris Tarry, and drummer Ian Froman. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
