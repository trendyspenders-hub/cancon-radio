Field Day

Field Day was a Canadian pop-punk band from Calgary formed in 1994. The band's original lineup consisted of guitarist and singer John Hiebert, drummer Jay Kreway (who were both members of the Calgary punk band "Ninth Configuration") along with bassist Jonas Smith (also known as Joner Baloner).
