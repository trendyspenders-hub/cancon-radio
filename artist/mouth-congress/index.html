Mouth Congress

Mouth Congress was an experimental rock band featuring Paul Bellini and Scott Thompson, formed concurrently with their rise to fame as members of The Kids in the Hall. Mouth Congress took its name from the Sir Richard Burton translation of The Kama Sutra. It is the term Burton used for oral sex. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
