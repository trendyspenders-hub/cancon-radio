Wave was a Canadian pop rock duo composed of Dave Thomson and Paul Gigliotti. They formed in 1999 in Niagara Falls, Ontario, and are best known for their 2001 singles, "California" and "Think It Over", both of which were Top 10 radio hits in Canada. Their debut album, Nothing as It Seems, was a success, achieving gold certification. However, their second album, State of Mind, was a commercial failure. The band folded in 2003, with Thomson and Gigliotti pursuing separate careers in the music industry. The duo reunited in 2016 for a single show in their hometown. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
