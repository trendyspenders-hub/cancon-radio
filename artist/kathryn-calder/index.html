Kathryn Calder

Official site

Kathryn Jane Calder (born June 17, 1982) is a Canadian indie rock musician, who performs as a solo artist, and is a member of the bands the New Pornographers and Frontperson. She is a former member of Immaculate Machine. Calder started with the New Pornographers by filling in for Neko Case for live performances and was made a permanent member in 2006.
