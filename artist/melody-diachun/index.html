Melody Diachun

Melody Diachun (born December 30, 1968) is a Canadian singer, songwriter and recording artist. She won the Jazz Artist of the Year award at the 2023 Western Canadian Music Awards and was named the 2026 Cultural Ambassador for the City of Nelson, British Columbia. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
