Mike Smith

Michael Edwin Smith is a Canadian actor, screenwriter, comedian, and musician. He is best known for his portrayal of Bubbles and as co-writer of the cult classic television program, films and stage production Trailer Park Boys. He was also the guitarist for the Canadian rock band Sandbox. Smith earned a degree in English from St. Francis Xavier University in Antigonish, Nova Scotia. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
