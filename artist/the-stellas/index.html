The Stellas

The Stellas were a married Canadian country music duo from Whitby, Ontario. They finished in fourth place on the second season of the CMT series Can You Duet in 2009. Their self-titled debut album was released by EMI Music Canada in September 2011. The first single was a cover of the Fairground Attraction's song "Perfect". In July 2014, the duo released "Gravy", which doubles as the first single off their second album. An Irish rock and roll showband by the same name was active in the sixties releasing a single covering Wooly Bully and "Fortune Teller". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
