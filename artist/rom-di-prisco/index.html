Rom Di Prisco

SoundCloudOfficial site

Rom Di Prisco, sometimes known as Morphadron, is a Canadian electronic composer. He has provided music for over 30 video games, including the Need for Speed series, SSX Tricky, the NHL series (2000–2002), Spy Hunter 2, Unreal Tournament 3, Guacamelee!, Fortnite, Foxhole, and Anvil Empires.
