Tesher

Hitesh Sharma, known professionally as Tesher, is a Canadian rapper, singer-songwriter and producer of Indian origin. He is best known for his singles "Young Shahrukh" and "Jalebi Baby", both released in 2020. A remix version of "Jalebi Baby" was released in 2021 with Jason Derulo. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
