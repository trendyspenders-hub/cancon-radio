David Hill

David Hill, MBE is a choral conductor and organist. Since July 2013, he is Professor Adjunct of Choral Conducting and Principal Conductor of Yale Schola Cantorum at the Yale Institute of Sacred Music. He has been Musical Director of The Bach Choir, London, since April 1998. . He also holds the positions Chief Conductor of the Southern Sinfonia, Music Director of the Leeds Philharmonic Society, and Associate Conductor of the Bournemouth Symphony Orchestra. Hill is President of the Incorporated Association of Organists, succeeding Catherine Ennis. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
