The Partland Brothers are a Canadian pop/rock band, best known for their 1986 hit single "Soul City". Originally fronted by brothers Chris and G. P. Partland with supporting session musicians, more recently they have recorded and performed as a trio with their brother Robin Partland. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
