John Hammond

John Paul Hammond was an American blues singer and guitarist whose career spanned six decades. He was the son of record producer John Henry Hammond Jr., a descendant of Cornelius Vanderbilt, and performed as John Hammond and John Hammond Jr. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
