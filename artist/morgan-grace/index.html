MORGAN GRACE

Morgan Grace is an American singer-songwriter of rock music who has been active in the music scene in Portland, Oregon. In 2006, she won American Idol Underground with her song "The Rules of Dating". In 2008, she released her solo album Valentine. In 2009, she was named Best New Artist at the 2009 Portland Music Awards. She has also worked as a long-haul truck driver.
