Ian Campbell Thomas (born 23 July 1950) is a Juno Award-winning Canadian singer-songwriter, actor and author. Best known for his 1973 hit "Painted Ladies" and his role on The Red Green Show as Dougie Franklin, he is the younger brother of comedian and actor Dave Thomas. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
