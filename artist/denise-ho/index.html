Denise Ho

Denise Ho Wan-see (born 10 May 1977) is a Hong Kong and Canadian singer and actress. In 2014, Ho was blacklisted by the Chinese government following her support for pro-democracy movements in Hong Kong. As of 2024, her passport has been confiscated by the government and there have been no updates since then as to whether she is permitted to leave Hong Kong. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
