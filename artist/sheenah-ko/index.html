Sheenah Ko

Sheenah Ko is an electronic pop artist based in Québec, Canada. She has had a successful career touring international stages with The Besnard Lakes before embarking on her solo career. Her debut album, Nowhere In Time, ranked in the top 10 of national radio charts for electronic music. Her music video, Wrap Me Up, won the Best Choreography Award (against Beyoncé) in 2021 and was nominated for the 2021 Juno Awards. Her second album, Future Is Now, won Pop Album of the Year at the 2023 GAMIQ Awards.
