Pete McCormack

Pete McCormack (born January 27, 1965) is a Canadian author, filmmaker, screenwriter and musician. He directed the Academy Award short-listed documentary Facing Ali and the Leacock Award-nominated novel Understanding Ken. He is the creator of the HBO Canada documentary television series Sports on Fire. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
