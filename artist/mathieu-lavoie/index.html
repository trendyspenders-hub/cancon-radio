Mathieu Lavoie

Mathieu Lavoie is a Quebec politician who was the elected to the National Assembly of Quebec for Montarville in the 2026 Quebec general election as a member of the Quebec Liberal Party (PLQ). He unseated Nathalie Roy from the Coalition Avenir Québec (CAQ). Lavoie is a graduate of the University of Montreal. He is a musician by profession. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
