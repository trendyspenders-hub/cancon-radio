Siibii is a Cree singer-songwriter from Mistissini, Quebec, Canada. They are most noted for their 2025 EP Siibii, which earned them a Juno Award nomination for Contemporary Indigenous Artist of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2026. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
