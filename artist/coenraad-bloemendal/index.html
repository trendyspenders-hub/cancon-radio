Coenraad Bloemendal

Coenraad Bloemendal (born April 30, 1946, in Amsterdam) is a Dutch-born Canadian cellist, who has performed, taught and recorded primarily in the field of classical music during a career that has spanned more than four decades. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
