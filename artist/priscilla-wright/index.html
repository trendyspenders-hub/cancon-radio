Priscilla Wright

Priscilla Wright (born August 14, 1940, in London, Ontario) is a Canadian singer, most famous for the song "The Man in the Raincoat", which she first released in 1955, and re-recorded in 1988. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
