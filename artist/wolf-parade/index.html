Wolf Parade is a Canadian indie rock band formed in Montreal, in 2003. The band consists of Spencer Krug, Dan Boeckner and Arlen Thompson (drums). During live performances, the band are currently joined by Arcade Fire's Tim Kingsbury on various instruments. Multi-instrumentalist Dante DeCaro and synth-player Hadji Bakara are both recurring former members of the band. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
