Kyle Bobby Dunn

Kyle Bobby Dunn is a Canadian composer and performer of ambient and drone-based music. His work has been characterized as containing a classical music influence which "operates outside of categories" and is typified by the use of electric guitar, organ and "meticulous post-processing". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
