Dynesti

Dynesti delivers royal hip-hop/soul with a dash of rudegyal–feminine Afro-Caribbean attitude. As a Caribbean rapper, singer and dancer, she fuses neo-soul, conscious hip-hop, and reggae/dancehall in her sound. Emerging from six years in-and-out of the shelter system, her art centres around healing and liberation through bold vulnerability and humour. After opening for major artists such as Sean Paul and Dax, Dynesti has been serving her fans a dynamic range of impressive singles leading up to her first album, The Dyna: Breakfast themed around navigating love, relationships and mental health as a Black girl from a broken home.
