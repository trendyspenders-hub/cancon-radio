Bobby Wills is a Canadian country music singer-songwriter. Wills has released five albums – Man with No Past (2010), If It Was That Easy (2012), Crazy Enough (2014), Tougher Than Love (2016) and an EP Longshot Bar & Grill (2019). Many of his singles have made it onto the Billboard Canada Country Chart, including the hits "Somebody Will", "Down by the River", and "Won't You Be Mine", which each peaked at the number 9 spots. In 2013, Wills won the Canadian Country Music Association Rising Star Award. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
