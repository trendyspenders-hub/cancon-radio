Andrina Turenne

SpotifyBandcampOfficial site

Andrina Turenne is a Métis Canadian folk singer-songwriter from Manitoba, best known as a member of the Juno Award-winning folk band Chic Gamine. A member of the Franco-Manitoban community, she records and performs music in both English and French. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
