Kataklysm is a Canadian melodic death metal band. As of 2024, they have released fifteen studio albums, two EPs, and two DVDs. Kataklysm received their first Juno Award for Best Album of the Year in the "Heavy Metal" category for their 2015 album Of Ghosts and Gods. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
