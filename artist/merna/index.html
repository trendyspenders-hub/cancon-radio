Merna Bishouty, professionally known as Merna, is a Palestinian-Canadian singer, songwriter, and producer based in Toronto, Ontario. She is known for her solo work as well as for writing and producing across R&B, hip-hop, and pop. She has also released music under the alias Ayah. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
