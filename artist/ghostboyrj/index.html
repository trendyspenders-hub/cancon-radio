Ghostboyrj

Ghostboyrj, born in Toronto and raised in Brampton, started his music journey at 12 with multiple instruments. His love for poetry and hip-hop inspired him to create music after being introduced to his love for Hip-Hop, where he learned more about the culture. He is the owner of Ghostown Records & works to push creative boundaries within Hip-Hop. He’s been mentored by Unity Charity, studied at the Manifesto Music Business Program and Soundstock Academy, and has been supported by the Ontario Arts Council, TD Bank, and others. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
