Pkew Pkew Pkew is a Canadian punk rock band formed in Toronto, Ontario. Pkew Pkew Pkew's self-titled debut album was released on June 10, 2016 on Royal Mountain Records. On June 21, 2017 Pkew Pkew Pkew signed with SideOneDummy Records, and re-issued their debut album. They released their sophomore effort in 2019. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
