OTYKEN

OTYKEN is an ethno-musical group from Siberia. The main members of the group are the Chulyms (a small indigenous people of central Siberia). OTYKEN perform the "World Music" genre. The group combines folk musical instruments with modern trends. Here we publish music videos and talk about folk crafts (beekeeping, herbalism). We cover the life and culture of the small peoples of Siberia. Subscribe to the channel, support us! Source: the artist's YouTube channel
