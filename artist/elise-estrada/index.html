Elise Estrada (born July 30, 1987) is a Filipino-Canadian pop singer-songwriter. She is known for her hit singles such as "Insatiable", "One Last Time" and "Lipstick" which were released in Canada. She is signed to Universal Music Canada's XOXO Entertainment. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
