Scott St. John

Official site

Scott St. John (born 4 December 1969 in London, Ontario, Canada) is a Canadian violinist and violist. A recipient of an Avery Fisher Career Grant, he was a member of the St. Lawrence String Quartet and on the faculty of Stanford University, where he taught violin and chamber music.
