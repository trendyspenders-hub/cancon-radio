Bill Livingstone

William John Roderick Livingstone (March 20, 1942 - February 22, 2025) was a Canadian musician, band leader, composer, and author. He was the founding Pipe Major of the 78th Fraser Highlanders Pipe Band and the first non-Scottish Pipe Major to win the world championship. He published an autobiography, two books of compositions, seven albums, and five volumes of A Piobaireachd Diary and received several awards and accolades. He was also a highland bagpipe teacher and adjudicator Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
