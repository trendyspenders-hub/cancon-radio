http://www.karynellis.com/ Karyn Ellis (B.C./ Ontario) -- Guelph Mercury calls her a "revelation". The Globe & Mail states she has "a magical way with song" and included her as one of "Five must-see songstresses" at NXNE, Toronto Canada's sister festival to SXSW. Ellis creates music that is "twangy and cool and unpredictable... difficult to classify and impossible to resist." (Indie-music.com) "This is a woman with stories" (Calgary Herald).
