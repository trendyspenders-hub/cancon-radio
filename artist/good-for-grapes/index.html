Good for Grapes

Good for Grapes is a Pacific Northwest indie-folk band based in Surrey, British Columbia, Canada. The band consists of Daniel McBurnie, Graham Gomez, Alexa Unwin, Robert Hardie, Alex Hauka, Gregory McLeod and Will Watson. Their first self-titled EP, Good for Grapes was released on iTunes Canada on April 15, 2011. They have won both the Rogers urMusic Battle of the Bands, and Supernova's 'Band on the Run to the UK.'
