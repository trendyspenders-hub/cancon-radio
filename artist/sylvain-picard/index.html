Sylvain Picard

Sylvain Picard is a composer, guitarist and mixer based in Canada. He has studied mixing under Tom Elmhirst (David Bowie, Adele, Frank Ocean). He collaborated with OSM and French hip hop legends IAM; with Yannik Rieu; he released five albums. When he is not in the studio, he runs on mountain tops. www.sylvainpicard.com/english Source: the artist's YouTube channel
