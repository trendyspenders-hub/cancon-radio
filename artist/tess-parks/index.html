Tess Parks

Tess Parks (born 1990) is a Canadian singer-songwriter and photographer from Toronto. Having released five studio albums, Parks is known for her dreamy psychedelic music. Among others, Parks has collaborated with Brian Jonestown Massacre front figure Anton Newcombe on two albums. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
