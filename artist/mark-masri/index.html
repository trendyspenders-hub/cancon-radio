Mark Masri

Mark Masri (born 23 August 1973) is a Canadian singer, songwriter and producer. He is signed to the EMI record label and has released five albums: Mark Masri, La Voce, Christmas Is..., A Christmas Time with You, and Intimo, as well as a special US release: See My Face US. He sings in English and also has a multilingual repertoire of interpretations. On his album La Voce he sings a collection of classic songs in six languages. He joined The Tenors in 2022.
