8485 or Eighty is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and music producer. She is affiliated with the hyperpop scene and formerly of the collective Helix Tears. She frequently collaborates with other experimental artists, such as Anamanaguchi, Aldn, Danny Brown, Ericdoa, Midwxst, Underscores, and Whethan. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
