DenMother

DenMother is the experimental electronic music project of Canadian multidisciplinary artist Sabarah Pilon. Her sound is described as atmospheric and ethereal converging with the feral, primal and instinctual, incorporating echoing vocal elements over layers of texture, drum and synth. DenMother creates an intimacy with her audience during her performances, which become living extensions of her music and act as stand alone rituals. http://denmothermother.com https://instagram.com/denmothermother https://denmother.bandcamp.com ​ Source: the artist's YouTube channel
