Kiya Tabassian (born on August 11, 1976, in Tehran) is an Iranian musician, singer, and composer. He moved to Canada in 1990 and created the musical ensemble Constantinople, assuming the roles of both artistic director and composer. Constantinople includes the interplay between Persian musical traditions and a variety of global styles. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
