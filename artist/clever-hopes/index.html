Clever Hopes

Clever Hopes is a Canadian alternative country and folk rock band based in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, whose core member is singer-songwriter Andrew Shaver. Originally from Toronto, Ontario, Shaver is also an actor and theatre director, best known for his supporting role as David Rizzio in the fourth season of the television series Reign, and has been a radio host on CKVE-FM and CFLB-FM. He formed the band after spending some time in Australia following the end of a romantic relationship.
