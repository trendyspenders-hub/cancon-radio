Ron Allen

Ron Allen is a Toronto based JUNO Nominated music producer/songwrite/DJ and founder of legendary dance music label Strobe Records. Revered as one of the most influential and credible underground independent ‘house music’ labels to help shape the burgeoning house music scene of the early 1990’s, Toronto’s Strobe Records set the gold standard for solid ‘deep’ house music blueprint production, from it’s very first releases back in 1991 Source: the artist's YouTube channel
