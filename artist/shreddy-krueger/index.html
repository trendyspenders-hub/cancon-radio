Shreddy Krueger

Shreddy Krueger is a post-hardcore band formed in Kelowna, British Columbia in 2011. The band released an EP Curses in 2012 and two albums, The Grieving in 2013, and Deeper Darker in 2015 through inVogue Records. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
