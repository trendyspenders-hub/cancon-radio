Joan Watson

Joan Thelma Watson (June 28, 1953 – March 12, 2015) was a Canadian French horn musician and teacher. She served as principal horn of the Canadian Opera Orchestra and was a founding member of the True North Brass quintet. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
