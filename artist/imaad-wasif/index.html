Imaad Wasif

Imaad Wasif (born October 27, 1977) is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and guitarist whose music combines elements of psychedelic folk and rock. Wasif's music has been described as "unbelievably intense with sparkling, raga-influenced guitar and a mystic bent." Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
