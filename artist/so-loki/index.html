So Loki is a Canadian hip hop band from Vancouver, British Columbia, consisting of rapper Sam Lucia and producer Geoffrey Millar. The band is most noted for the singles "Athletes World", whose video was a longlisted Prism Prize nominee in 2019, and "Elephant Man", which was shortlisted for the SOCAN Songwriting Prize in the same year.
