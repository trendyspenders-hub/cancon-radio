Harley Olivia

Harley Olivia is a neurodivergent Toronto-based alt-rock artist, known for her powerhouse vocals, emotionally charged lyrics, and instantly hooky melodies. A former flight attendant who traded the skies for the stage, she released her debut EP ‘Hiding Little Pieces’ and went on to win both Gussapolooza’s Rising Sun Award and the Jack Daniel’s Supporting Act Competition. With over 12K Instagram followers and 11K on TikTok, she has built a passionate fanbase, earning her the opportunity to share stages with Finger Eleven, Bleeker, SUMO CYCO, My Son the Hurricane and Softcult.
