The Men of the Deeps

Official site

The Men of the Deeps are a male choral ensemble composed of former and working coal miners from Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia. The choir requires each member to have at least 2 years experience working in or around a coal mine. They are the only choir where the second requirement is to sing. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
