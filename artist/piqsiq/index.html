PIQSIQ is an Inuit throat singing (katajjaq) duo composed of sisters Tiffany Ayalik and Inuksuk Mackay. They blend traditional Inuit throat singing practices with beats, looping, and other sorts of electronic manipulation to create complex, multi-layered songs. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
