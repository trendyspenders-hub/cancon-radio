Garfield

Garfield was a Canadian progressive rock band based in Toronto, Ontario, in the late 1970s, fronted by vocalist and songwriter Garfield French, and including Walter Lawrence on guitar and electric cello, Paul O'Donnell on guitar, harmonica and banjo, Jacques Fillion on keyboards, Dennis French on drums, Chip Yarwood on flute and keyboards, and Maris Tora on bass guitar. The band received a Juno Award nomination for Most Promising Group at the Juno Awards of 1977. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
