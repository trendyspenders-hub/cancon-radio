Cheyanne Summer

Cheyanne Summer is a rising country-rock artist from Calgary, AB, blending the storytelling of country with the energy of rock and blues. Cheyanne grew up inspired by country legends like The Chicks and Miranda Lambert, and today delivers a fresh, powerful sound driven by her dynamic vocals and heartfelt songwriting. Her latest single 'Today’s Been Hell' showcases that signature edge and has been making waves on radio and streaming platforms. Her music has been streamed over 250,000 times worldwide, with placements on international Spotify and Apple Music charts. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
