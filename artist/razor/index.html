Razor is a Canadian thrash metal band formed in 1983 at Guelph, Ontario. They are recognized as Canadian thrash metal pioneers, and have been referred to as one of the country's "Big Four", alongside Sacrifice, Voivod, and Annihilator. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
