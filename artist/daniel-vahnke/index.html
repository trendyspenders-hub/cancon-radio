Daniel Vahnke

Daniel Vahnke (born April 2, 1965), sometimes credited as Anton Rathausen, is a Canadian composer and lyricist. He is best known as a co-founding member of Vampire Rodents, a band that integrated elements of classical, ambient, and industrial music into its work. Daniel uses German, Latin and Chinese in his compositions. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
