Aurora Bella

Aurora Bella is a Canadian/American/Austrian singer-songwriter who performs solo on piano, guitar, and ukulele, and as a member of the band Little Darkness. Her work is emotional, observant, and catchy, with an intelligence, wit and power woven into every song. Her debut album Persephone was released January 26th 2024. Persephone was produced by Earl Pereira (The Steadies, Wide Mouth Mason) and Efren Pereira Jr. at Dr. Juicebox Studio in Saskatoon, SK, Canada. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
