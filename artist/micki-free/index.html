Micki Free

Micki Free is a guitarist and singer of Native American descent. He won a Grammy Award for his contribution to the Beverly Hills Cop movie soundtrack and has won two Native American Music Awards. He is the director of Promotions and Special Events for the Seminole Tribe of Florida, owners of Hard Rock International. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
