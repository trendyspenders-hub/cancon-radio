Doldrums

Doldrums is the musical identity of Canadian singer, producer and visual artist Airick Asher Woodhead. Doldrums began in 2010 as a moniker to release home recordings before expanding into a live band and releasing three LPs: Lesser Evil on Arbutus Records, The Air Conditioned Nightmare on Sub Pop records, and Esc on Endless. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
