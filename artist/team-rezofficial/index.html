Team Rezofficial

Team Rezofficial was a Canadian aboriginal hip hop group (primarily Cree from First Nations surrounding Maskwacis, Alberta). Founded by former members of War Party, the group included musicians Karmen "Hellnback" Omeasoo, Drezus (Jeremiah Manitopyes), Jay Mak, Big Stomp, Lakes Aqui, Tomislav Milardovic, and Big Slim.
