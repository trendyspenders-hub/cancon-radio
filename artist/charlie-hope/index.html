Charlie Hope is a Canadian children's singer and songwriter. Her debut album I'm Me! A Collection of Songs for Children, won two Independent Music Awards in 2010 for Best Album and Best Song for the title track, in the Children's Music category. This album was nominated for a Juno Award for Children's Album of the Year, that same year. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
