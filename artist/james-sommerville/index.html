James Sommerville

Official site

James Sommerville is a Canadian orchestral hornist and conductor. He was the principal hornist for the Boston Symphony Orchestra, and former conductor and music director of the Hamilton Philharmonic, in Hamilton, Ontario. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
