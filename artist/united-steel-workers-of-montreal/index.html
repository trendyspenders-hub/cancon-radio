United Steel Workers of Montreal

United Steel Workers of Montreal (or USWM) were a six-piece alternative country ensemble based in downtown Montreal, Quebec. The core band members included Gern f Vlchek (vocals/acoustic guitar), Felicity Hamer (vocals/accordion), Sean "Gus" Beauchamp (vocals/acoustic guitar), Matt Watson (electric guitar), accompanied by Roger Dawson, then Eddy Blake Eaton and finally Flipper Frumignac (on bass), and Kevin Mcneilly, then Christopher Reid, and finally Dylan Perron (on banjo/mandolin). The band drew on punk rock, bluegrass and country influences. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
