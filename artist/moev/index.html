Moev is an electronic band based in Vancouver, British Columbia. Initially formed in 1981, Moev released their debut album in 1982, Zimmerkampf, through the independent label Go! Records. After various issues with Go!, the members of Moev co-founded the label Nettwerk Records as an outlet for the band's music, which led to the release of Dusk and Desire in 1986. Although Nettwerk grew in popularity over the subsequent few years, Moev also signed with the major label Atlantic Records.
