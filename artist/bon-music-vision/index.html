Bon Music Vision

Bon Music Vision is a multidisciplinary artist duo based in London, United Kingdom, consisting of Yerosha Windrich and Elfed Alexander Morris. They produce music, audiovisual works, and installation projects that incorporate electronic music, sound art, and visual storytelling, with a practice that prioritises collaboration with people of colour and within the queer community.
