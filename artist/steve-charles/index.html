Steve Charles

Steve Charles is an Australian Songwriter and Performer. Steve writes and records many styles of music from Roots and Acoustic to Orchestrated Ballads, Rock, Pop, Country, Smokey Blues, and more. Hits latest album "Two Legged Fish" is a collection of his most popular songs and collaborations spanning over thirty years. Current release - “GODDESS” the Messenger album.
