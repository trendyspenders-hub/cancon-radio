Irish Mythen

Irish Mythen is an Irish-born Canadian contemporary folk singer-songwriter. In recent years, Mythen has performed with Rod Stewart, Gordon Lightfoot and Lucinda Williams at major festival stages the world over. Mythen's first two full-length albums were Sweet Necessity in 2008 and Open Here in 2011. They moved to Prince Edward Island in 2007, where they won the Songwriter of the Year Award in 2016 for their song "Gypsy Dancer".
