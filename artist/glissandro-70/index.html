Glissandro 70

Glissandro 70 is a Canadian Afrobeat band based in Toronto, Ontario. Its members are Craig Dunsmuir (a.k.a. Kanada 70, Guitarkestra) and Sandro Perri (a.k.a. Polmpo Polpo), who play a blend of dance and experimental rock styles. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
