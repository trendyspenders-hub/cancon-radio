Caity Gyorgy is a Canadian jazz singer from Calgary, Alberta. She received the Juno Award for Vocal Jazz Album of the Year in 2022 for her EP, Now Pronouncing: Caity Gyorgy, in 2023 for her debut LP, Featuring, and in 2025 for her LP, Hello! How are you?. Gyorgy was born in Calgary, Alberta. Her paternal grandfather's family had come over in the 1930s from Hungary. In 2022, Gyorgy received her Masters of Music in jazz performance from McGill University under the tutelage of Christine Jensen. She is a graduate of the music program at Humber College and received her Masters of Music in jazz Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
