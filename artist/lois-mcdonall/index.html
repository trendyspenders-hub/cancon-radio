Lois McDonall

Lois Jeanette McDonall (born February 7, 1939) is a Canadian operatic soprano, especially known for her performances in the operas of Mozart and Donizetti. She was appointed as a Member of the Order of Canada in 2023. She currently resides in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
