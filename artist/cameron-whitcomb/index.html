Cameron Donald Whitcomb is a Canadian singer and songwriter from Nanaimo, British Columbia. He competed on season 20 of reality television show American Idol. He is currently signed to Atlantic Records. Whitcomb has charted multiple songs internationally, including "Quitter", "Medusa", "Options", and "Kingdom of Fear". His debut album The Hard Way was released on September 26, 2025. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

6 wins, 2 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
