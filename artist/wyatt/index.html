Wyatt

BandcampOfficial site

Wyatt is a Canadian country music group from Saskatoon, Saskatchewan composed of Scott Patrick, Daniel Fortier, Bray Hudson (drums) and Cam Ewart (bass). Following the release of a Christmas album, Snowed In, in 2005, Wyatt released their debut album, Hard Road, in 2007. No singles were released from the project because the band "didn't feel it was quite there yet." Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
