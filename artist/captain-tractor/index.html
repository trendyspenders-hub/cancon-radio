Captain Tractor

Captain Tractor is a Canadian folk rock band, based in Edmonton, Alberta. They play a punk-influenced variant of Celtic folk music, which has been compared to Great Big Sea, The Pogues or Spirit of the West. Their songs are often rich in local, cultural, and geographical references. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
