Les BB

Les B.B. was a Canadian band from the province of Quebec. It had great success in the 1980s and the early 1990s. It is made up of Patrick Bourgeois, Alain Lapointe and François Jean. Bourgeois, the main songwriter for the band's releases, died in November 2017. Bourgeois's son, Ludovick Bourgeois, is a pop singer who was the winner of the fifth season of La Voix.
