Yung Tory

Tory Lester Zellars (born April 24, 1996), better known by his stage name Yung Tory (or Mr. Yaw Yaw), is a Canadian rapper from Mississauga, Ontario. He initially signed with Lil Durk's Only the Family in 2017, but parted ways the following year in favor of Timbaland's Mosley Music Group, an imprint of Def Jam Recordings. He released his debut studio album, Rastar, in early 2019. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
