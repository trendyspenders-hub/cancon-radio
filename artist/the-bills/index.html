The Bills

Official site

The Bills are a high-impact, acoustic folk music quintet from the West Coast of Canada. Formed in 1996 (as The Bill Hilly Band), they have performed extensively throughout North America and Europe, with their strongest followings in Western Canada, the Western US, Ontario and the UK. Though the lineup has had a few changes since the band first formed, the present-day Bills are: Marc Atkinson - mandolin, guitar, vocals Richard Moody - violin, viola, vocals Chris Frye - guitar, lead vocals Adrian Dolan - fiddle, piano, accordion, vocals Scott 'Grampa Bill' White - upright bass, vocals The group Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
