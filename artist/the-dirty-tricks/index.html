The Dirty Tricks

The Dirty Tricks are a Canadian rock band formed in Montreal, Quebec, Canada in 2003. (Not to be confused with "Dirty Tricks", a rock band that recorded for Polydor Records 1975–1977) They toured with Malajube (Dare to Care Records), The Sainte Catherines (Fat Wreck Chords) and shared the stage with established bands such as NOFX, Against Me!, The (International) Noise Conspiracy and many more. Their first single was the theme song of the skateboarding TV program called The Under Attack Show, aired on Musique Plus and Razer channel (now known as MTV2). Two other songs were used on Fast Cars Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
