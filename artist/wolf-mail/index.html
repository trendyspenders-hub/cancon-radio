Wolf Mail

Wolf Mail (born 1972) is a Canadian-born guitarist and singer-songwriter. Mail has recorded eight full-length albums, internationally distributed and has toured in over twenty-six countries. He is influenced by blues, jazz, soul and country. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
