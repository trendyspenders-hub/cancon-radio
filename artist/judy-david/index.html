Judy & David

Official site

Judy & David were a Canadian children's music duo formed in 1991 in Richmond Hill, Ontario. They are recipients of a 1998 Juno Award in the category of Juno Award for Children's Album of the Year. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
