Barney Bentall and the Legendary Hearts

Official site

Barney Bentall and the Legendary Hearts are a Canadian rock band, based out of Vancouver, British Columbia, that formed in 1980. The band's name was taken from the title of Lou Reed's 1983 album. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
