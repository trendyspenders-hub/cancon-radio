Light Drawn In

Rock/metal group Light Drawn in is from Edmonton, AB and surrounding area. Founded in 2021, the band quickly worked on a sound that would separate themselves from the others. Comprised of 5 members, guitarist Jared Martin and Steve Barrett, bass guitarist Jordan De Vos, drummer Lance Greenwall, and vocalist Dayn Armsworthy, the band is looking to make a name for themselves. INFLUENCES Van Halen, Pantera, Dream Theatre, Disturbed, Trivium, Alice In Chains, Killswitch Engage, I Mother Earth, Rain City Drive, Slaves, Landmvrks, Led Zeppelin, Breaking Benjamin, Sevendust Source: the artist's YouTube channel
