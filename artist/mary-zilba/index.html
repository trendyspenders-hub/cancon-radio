Mary Zilba

Mary E. Zilba is an American-Canadian former singer and television personality best known for her singing career in Canada and her reign as Miss Ohio. Additionally, she received further recognition for her appearance as a cast member on The Real Housewives of Vancouver. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
