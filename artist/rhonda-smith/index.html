Rhonda Smith

Official site

Rhonda Smith is a Canadian bassist, vocalist, and songwriter, best known for her long-running work with Prince and Jeff Beck. She has also recorded and toured with artists including Chaka Khan, Beyoncé, T.I., Sheila E., Patrice Rushen, and Rachelle Ferrell. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
