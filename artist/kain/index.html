Kaïn is a folk rock group from Drummondville, Quebec, Canada. The group of four is made up of Steve Veilleux, Yannick Blanchette, Patrick Lemieux, and Éric Maheu. Their style is a blend of rock and roll with Quebec folk music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
