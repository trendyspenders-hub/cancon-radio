Young Kidd

Frankie Fontaine (born October 1, 1988), better known by the stage name Young Kidd or YK the Mayor, is a Canadian rapper of aboriginal and Jamaican heritage. He began performing in 2005 and has released multiple mixtapes, two albums and was nominated for two APCMA awards in 2010. Fontaine has been successful in the Winnipeg rap scene.
