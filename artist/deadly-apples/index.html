Deadly Apples is a Canadian rock / industrial metal band based in Montreal, Quebec. The band consists of frontman / lyricist Alex Martel (vocals) and songwriter / producer Antoine Lamothe (drums, synths), with live musicians joining them on stage. The band is known for its high energy and intense live performances. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
