Deanne Rose Moore

Independent Cree Singer/Songwriter from James Bay, Canada. Her debut single in 2020 rose to #1 on the R&B and Soul Charts and #24 on the genre charts. Deanne writes and grounds her music in storytelling and resilience blending emotion, identity and connection. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
