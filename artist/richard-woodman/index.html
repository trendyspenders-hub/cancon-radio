Richard Woodman

Richard Woodman is a Canadian singer/songwriter from Northern Alberta with Cree & Metis roots. Along the picturesque shores of Lesser Slave Lake, Alberta, where he calls home, is where Richard Woodman finds his connection. Big skies, purple sunsets, and the serenity of nature's inspiring beauty. With a blend of country and rock, Richard's voice has been described as smooth and edgy and pure. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
