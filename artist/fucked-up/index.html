Fucked Up are a Canadian hardcore punk band from Toronto, Ontario, formed in 2001. The band consists of guitarists Mike Haliechuk and Josh Zucker, bassist Sandy Miranda, lead vocalist Damian Abraham, and drummer Jonah Falco. From 2007 to 2021, the band also included guitarist and vocalist Ben Cook. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
