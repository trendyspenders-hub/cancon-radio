Digging Roots is a Canadian musical group consisting of husband and wife duo Raven Kanatakta and ShoShona Kish, whose musical style blends folk-rock, pop, blues, and hip hop. They won the Juno Award for Aboriginal Album of the Year in 2010 for their album We Are.... Kanatakta is originally from Winneway, Quebec, while Kish is from the Batchewana First Nation in Northern Ontario. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
