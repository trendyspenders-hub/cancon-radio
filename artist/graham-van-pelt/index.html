Graham Van Pelt

I'm a musician and DJ based in BC, Canada (formerly Montreal, also formerly Toronto). My latest collection of ambient music, 'Atlantis Tapes', is out as of February 23rd, 2023. My recent releases range from dreamy ambient excursions to dubby vocal house. Also, I host a weekly online radio show called 'Island City' on FSR.live, playing global jazz, R&B, disco, reggae and lots of other things. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
