Antoine Corriveau is a Canadian folk rock singer-songwriter and author from Quebec. He is most noted for his 2014 song "Le Nouveau vocabulaire", which won the French division of the SOCAN Songwriting Prize in 2015, and his 2016 album Cette chose qui cognait au creux de sa poitrine sans vouloir s'arrêter, which was longlisted for the 2017 Polaris Music Prize.
