Kon Kan is a Canadian synth-pop project conceived and formed in 1988 by Barry Harris in Toronto, Ontario. Kon Kan won a 1990 Juno Award for the song "I Beg Your Pardon" and was nominated for a 1991 Juno Award for the single "Puss N' Boots/These Boots Are Made for Walkin'". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
