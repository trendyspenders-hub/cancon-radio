Tre Mission is a Canadian rapper and record producer, known as one of the first North American artists to be associated with the predominantly United Kingdom-based grime genre. Tre Mission is a member of the Tizzy Gang grime crew alongside London based MCs Merky ACE and Cadell. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
