Laura Vinson

Official site

North Carolina native Laura Vinson brings her small-town Goldsboro roots into a sound that blends country, rock, and pop. She's wanted to give people an experience through music since she was five years old — a spark that turned into a deep love of songwriting as she developed her craft. Electronic textures, pop hooks, and country storytelling all run through her music's DNA. As she makes her way through the industry, her biggest goal stays the same: inspire and motivate her fans to chase whatever brings them peace. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
