LiftedPro

LiftedPro: hip hop artist and producer from Vancouver Island, BC (formerly Dante Jordan). Boom bap with West Coast and G-funk flavour. Written, recorded, produced, mixed and mastered in house at Lifted Productions since 2004. This is my form of free therapy, analysis of self, and beats built from the ground up. Albums include CREATURE (2026), Soul On Sleeve (2022), LIFTED2020, Realism, ...the Other Side of Reality and No Better Time.
