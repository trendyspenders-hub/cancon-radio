Finely Tuned Elephant (est. 2019) is an electrifying instrumental jazz-rock fusion quartet based in Ottawa, ON. They deliver a sound that is technically staggering and undeniably catchy, matched by a reputation for explosive sold-out shows. The group features Jordan Robinson (guitar), Cyrus Robertson Orkish (bass), Kyle Iveglia (drums), and Alex Lugli (keys). They are poised to release their third album and magnum opus, No Goats, in March 2026. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
