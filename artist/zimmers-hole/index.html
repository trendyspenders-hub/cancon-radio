Zimmers Hole is a Canadian comedic heavy metal/thrash metal band from Vancouver. It was formed by vocalist Chris Valagao Mina in 1991 and also features former Strapping Young Lad members Byron Stroud, Jed Simon and Gene Hoglan. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
