Manuel Tadros

Hany Manuel Tadros (Arabic: مانويل تادرس; born 30 September 1956) is an Egyptian-Canadian singer, songwriter, actor, comedian and voice actor. He currently works and lives in Quebec. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
