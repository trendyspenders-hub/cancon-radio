La Patère Rose was a Canadian francophone electro-pop group formed in 2003 in Sherbrooke, Quebec. The band was composed of keyboardist Roboto, drummer Kilojules, and singer-pianist Fanny Bloom. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
