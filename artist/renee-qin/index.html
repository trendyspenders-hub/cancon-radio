Renée Qin

Welcome to Renée Qin’s official YouTube channel! Renée is a young Canadian harpist and composer, one of CBC Music’s "30 Hot Canadian Classical Musicians Under 30" for 2024. Subscribe to join her journey as she brings the harp into new realms of sound and creativity, blending classical elegance with modern storytelling. Discover captivating performances, original compositions, and unique harp covers that showcase her innovative approach to both classical and contemporary music.
