Whitehorse

Whitehorse is a Canadian folk roots band, composed of husband-and-wife duo Luke Doucet and Melissa McClelland. Based in Hamilton, Ontario, Doucet and McClelland were both established singer-songwriters before opting to put their solo careers on hold to work together as Whitehorse. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
