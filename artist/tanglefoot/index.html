Tanglefoot

Official site

Tanglefoot is a Canadian folk band from Owen Sound, Ontario. Formed in Peterborough in the early 1980s by Joe Grant, Bob Wagar, and Tim Rowat to play traditional music, they became a five-piece band playing largely original music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
