Heather Bishop

Official site

Heather Bishop, (born April 25, 1949) is a Canadian folk singer-songwriter primarily known for her work as a social justice advocate and in the field of folk music and children's music. For her dedication to social justice, she has been awarded the Order of Canada, the Order of Manitoba and an Honorary Doctorate of Laws among many other awards. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
