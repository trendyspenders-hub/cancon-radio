Nastasia Y

~Ukrainian born artist in Toronto ~ Resistance & Ancestral Magic~ Ukrainian-born Nastasia Y [nas-TAH-s’yah] merges ancestral traditions with a futuristic vision, blending pop, funk, electro, R&B, and jazz, with Slavic folk music. Raised on her late father's resistance songs, her journey took her to Canada, where she earned a jazz piano degree, originally releasing English-language music, evolving into the cultural fusion project Nastasia Y Band (formerly DoVira). Her mission is to tell the world about Ukraine through music. Instagram @Nastasia.Y Source: the artist's YouTube channel
