Hanson Brothers

The Hanson Brothers were a Canadian punk rock band formed in 1984 in Victoria, British Columbia, and later based in Vancouver, British Columbia. The group included John and Rob Wright and Tom Holliston, all members of the punk rock band Nomeansno. The Hanson Brothers' band name references characters in the cult ice hockey film Slap Shot.
