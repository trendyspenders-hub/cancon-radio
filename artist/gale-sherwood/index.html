Gale Sherwood

Gale Sherwood (born Jacqueline Nash; March 4, 1929 – December 31, 2017) was a Canadian singer and actress. She was best known as the singing partner of Nelson Eddy from 1953 until his death in 1967. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
