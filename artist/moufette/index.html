Moufette

Moufette is a Canadian indie rock group whose name is the French word for 'Skunk'. They were formed in Montreal in 2004. The duo, which includes Steve Durand - formerly of Tinker and known for his work with Auf der Maur - and Ariel Engle, also mix electrionica and folk music in to their standard indie/pop sound. Their first album was released in 2005, and their first extended play was released in 2009. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
