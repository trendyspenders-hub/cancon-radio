Queen City Kids

Queen City Kids (QCK) was a Canadian hard rock band from Regina, Saskatchewan, and later based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, who scored a number of Canadian hits in the early 1980s including "Girls", "Dance", and "Black Box." The band was active from 1968 to 1983 and consisted of vocalist Alex Chuaqui, guitarist Kevin Fyhn, bass player John L. Donnelly, and drummer Jeff Germain. Queen City Kids formed in Regina in 1969 under the name Cambridge, while members were still in high school.
