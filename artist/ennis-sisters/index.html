Ennis Sisters

Official site

The Ennis Sisters are a Canadian musical family group from St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, active since 1997. The group consists of three sisters Maureen, Karen, and Teresa Ennis and performs in genres including Celtic music, folk rock, roots music, and country pop. The trio rose to prominence in the late 1990s, releasing their debut album ''Red is the Rose'' in 1997, which sold more than 40,000 copies and was named Best Folk Album by the Music Industry Association of Newfoundland & Labrador.
