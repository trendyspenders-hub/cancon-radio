The 3tards

The 3tards was a Canadian hardcore punk rock band that was formed in Brampton, Ontario, in 2001. The 3tards released three full-length albums – Greatest Hits Vol. 2, Crystal Balls, and 333: Halfway to Hell. They were known for their provocative, vulgar and sexual lyrics, and political satire. Their shows featured live births, circumcisions and a mock gay wedding. They have been self described as "Canada's most politically incorrect punk band." Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
