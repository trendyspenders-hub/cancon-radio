Tim Louis

Tim Louis (born 1969) is an American-Canadian Liberal politician and musician first elected as a member of Parliament in the House of Commons of Canada to represent the federal riding Kitchener—Conestoga during the 2019 Canadian federal election, defeating incumbent Harold Albrecht.
