Teresa Stratas

Teresa Stratas (born Anastasia Stratakis on May 26, 1938) is a retired Canadian operatic soprano and actress of Greek descent. She is especially well known for her award-winning recording of Alban Berg's Lulu. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
