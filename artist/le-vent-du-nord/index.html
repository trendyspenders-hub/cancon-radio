Le Vent du Nord is a Canadian folk music group from Saint-Antoine-sur-Richelieu in Quebec. The band performs traditional Québécois music, as well as original numbers in this style, in French. In 2018, the group's membership consists of André Gagné, Nicolas Boulerice, André Brunet, Réjean Brunet, and Olivier Demers. Their first eight recordings have been nominated for multiple awards.
