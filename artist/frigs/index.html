FRIGS were a Canadian indie rock band from Toronto, Ontario, whose 2018 album Basic Behaviour was longlisted for the 2018 Polaris Music Prize. The band members are vocalist Bria Salmena, guitarist Duncan Hay Jennings, bassist Lucas Savatti and drummer Kris Bowering. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
