Yondani Butt

Yondani Chak Cheung Butt (January 13, 1945 – August 28, 2014) was an orchestral conductor. He was born in Macau of Chinese parentage. He studied music at Indiana University Bloomington and the University of Michigan. He also had a PhD in chemistry, on which subject he has published numerous research papers, particularly on polyene photochemistry. He studied conducting with Wolfgang Vacano (1906–1985) at Indiana University, majoring in chemistry.
