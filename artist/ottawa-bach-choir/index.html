Ottawa Bach Choir

The Ottawa Bach Choir is a professional vocal ensemble from Ottawa, Canada founded and directed by Lisette Canton specialising in early music. Their album Handel: Dixit Dominus; Bach & Schütz: Motets won a Juno Award for Classical Album of the Year – Vocal or Choral Performance at the Juno Awards of 2020. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
