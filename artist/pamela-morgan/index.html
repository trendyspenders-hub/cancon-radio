Pamela Morgan

Pamela Morgan (born November 25, 1957) is a Canadian musician, songwriter and owner of independent label Amber Music, now living in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador. From 1976 to 1995, she was lead singer of folk rock band Figgy Duff. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
