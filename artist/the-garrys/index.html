The Garrys

From the coldest part of the landlocked Canadian prairies, sister trio The Garrys are making surf music. The Garrys use layered 3-part blood harmonies, hazy sun-bleached melodies, vintage guitar tones, and syncopated surf beats to create a unique doom-wop sound, dripping with nostalgia. Their music is likely to leave you with an urge to do the Watusi, bittersweet thoughts about slow-dancing with your middle school crush, or a nagging feeling that it’s not quite safe to go back into the water. Check out more of our music and merch at: https://thegarrys.bandcamp.com/ Source: the artist's YouTube channel
