Daniel Panetta

Daniel Panetta (born March 24, 1992), also known as Danny Payne is a Canadian singer, songwriter, musician and producer. In addition to his solo career, he is also the vocalist for the alternative rock band St.Yesterday. He was born in Angus, Ontario. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
