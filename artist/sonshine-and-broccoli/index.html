Sonshine and Broccoli

Official site

Sonshine and Broccoli are a Canadian children's music duo, from Toronto, Ontario, consisting of Lisa Sonshine and Brock Burford. They are most noted for their 2018 album It's Cool to Be Kind, which was a Juno Award nominee for Children's Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2019. The duo met when they both attended Sheridan College. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
