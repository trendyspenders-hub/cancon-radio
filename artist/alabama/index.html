Alabama

Alabama was a Canadian band of the early 1970s. The band members were Buster Fykes, Hector McLean, Rick Knight, and Len Sembaluk. The band performed a mix of progressive rock, cajun, country, and soul music, including songs written by Fykes and Knight. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
