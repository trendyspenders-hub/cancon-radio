Judi Singh

Judi Singh (May 9, 1945 – July 18, 2021), sometimes spelled Judy Singh, was a Canadian jazz musician based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. She was born to Indian and Black parents, making her a biracial person. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
