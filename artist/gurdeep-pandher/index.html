Gurdeep Pandher

Gurdeep Pandher is a Sikh-Canadian author, teacher and performer best known for sharing Punjabi dance videos. His videos have been featured in French-Canadian media outlets, as well as on television shows including ITV's breakfast show hosted by Lorraine Kelly, CTV's eTalk, and Rachel Maddow's MSNBC show.
