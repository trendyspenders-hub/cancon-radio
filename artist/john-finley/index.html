John Finley

John Finley (born May 6, 1945) is a Canadian singer-songwriter who came to prominence with Toronto R&B band Jon and Lee & the Checkmates and later with the Elektra supergroup Rhinoceros. Finley's talents reach over many genres, including R&B, Gospel, Jazz and Rock, and is acclaimed for his song writing, recording and live performances. After Rhinoceros, Finley was a member of Blackstone before engaging in projects with Homestead and Michael Quatro.
