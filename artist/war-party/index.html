War Party

War Party is a Cree hip-hop group from Maskwacis, Alberta. Its founding members are Rex Smallboy and Ryan Small. The members are ex-wife Cynthia, Karmen "Hellnback" Omeasoo, Bryan Omeosoo, and Tom Crier. The band's music combines rap rhythms with aboriginal themes and stories. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
