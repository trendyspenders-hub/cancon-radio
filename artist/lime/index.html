Lime

Lime is a Canadian disco band from Montreal, Quebec. The group was originally composed of married couple Denis and Denyse LePage who had a 1981 number one U.S. Dance hit with "Your Love". They continued to perform as recently as 2018, although others have also performed under the name of Lime. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
