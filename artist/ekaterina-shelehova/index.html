Ekaterina Shelehova captivated the world in 2021 with her viral Earth Melodies performance on Italy’s Got Talent, amassing over 500 million views. Blending ethno-operatic sounds, she has since then performed with ERA, alongside Hans Zimmer with the Limitless Orchestra, collaborated with Worakls Orchestra, earned the Golden Buzzer on Spain’s Got Talent (2024), composed for film (La Tempesta) and featured her own compositions in the video game Atomic Heart. A Milan Conservatory graduate with a Master’s in Opera, she has starred in productions such as L’Elisir d’Amore, Così Fan Tutte and Don Giovanni.
