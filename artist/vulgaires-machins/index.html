Vulgaires Machins

BandcampOfficial site

Vulgaires Machins is a French Canadian punk rock group from Granby, Quebec. The group consists of Guillaume Beauregard, Marie-Ève Roy, Maxime Beauregard (bass), and Pat Sayers (drums). Their lyrics denounce consumerism and modern decadence. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
