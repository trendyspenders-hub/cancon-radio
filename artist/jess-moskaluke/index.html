Jessica Moskaluke is a Canadian country singer and songwriter. She released her debut studio album, Light Up the Night in April 2014, which includes the platinum-certified single "Cheap Wine and Cigarettes". She has one number-one hit on the Billboard Canada Country chart with "Country Girls". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

2 wins, 4 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
