Out of Your Mouth

Out of Your Mouth was a Canadian rock band from Calgary, Alberta. It was fronted by guitarist, vocalist, producer, and writer Jason Darr. The band is best known for their cover of Madonna's song "Music". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
