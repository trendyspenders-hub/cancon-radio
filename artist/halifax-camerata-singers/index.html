Halifax Camerata Singers

The Halifax Camerata Singers is a Canadian chamber choir of 26 to 30 singers, one of a number of singing groups based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. It forms the core of the Symphony Nova Scotia chorus. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
