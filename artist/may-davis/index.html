May Davis

Welcome to my channel! Based in the heart of the Ottawa Valley there is no shortage of inspiration for music. I am a one-woman band who loves harmonies, humour and a good dad joke. Inspired by the musical muses Joni Mitchell, Norah Jones and Stevie Nicks my style ranges from folk ballads to blues. I am a visitor on the traditional lands of the Anishinaabe Algonquin and respectfully honour the land, cultures and traditions. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
