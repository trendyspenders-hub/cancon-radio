The Swinging Belles

The Swinging Belles are a Canadian swing band for children based in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador. Known for their energetic performances and unique blend of swing music for young audiences, the group has won the Juno Award for Children's Album of the Year twice, in 2016 and 2024. The band consists of child educators and performers Laura Winter and Erin Power, alongside award-winning guitarist Duane Andrews. Their work is grounded in the belief that children are intelligent and musically receptive, and their songs are designed to appeal to both children and their families.
