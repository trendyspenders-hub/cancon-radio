Flore Laurentienne

Flore Laurentienne is a Canadian instrumental music project, whose core member is Mathieu David Gagnon. Born in La Pocatière, Quebec, Gagnon grew up in Sainte-Anne-des-Monts. He is the brother of singer-songwriter Klô Pelgag. He studied classical and orchestral music in France at the Conservatoire de Bordeaux and the Conservatoire d’Aubervilliers, and upon returning to Quebec he worked as an arranger and producer for Pelgag, Les Hôtesses d'Hilaire and Guillaume Arsenault.
