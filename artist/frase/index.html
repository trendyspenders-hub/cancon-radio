Just as much at home behind an acoustic guitar and a mic, as he is at the helm of a raving dance floor. As a multi-instrumentalist, singer, producer, engineer, and DJ, Frase lives and breathes music. With a buttery voice, heartfelt songwriting, and magnetic stage presence he has built a fan base worldwide for the past decade one show at a time. With a high output and numerous collaborations, (SkiiTour, Moontricks, Dub Fx, Moglii, Father Funk, Stickybuds, Etc) his catalog highlights the nimble ability to have a soul, a mind, and a really good time all at the same junction.
