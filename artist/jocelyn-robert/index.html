Jocelyn Robert

BandcampOfficial site

Jocelyn Robert is a Canadian post-modern interdisciplinary artist from Quebec City, Quebec. In 1993, he co-founded the collective Avatar in Quebec City, which became the province's flagship in sound and electronic art. He was its first President and from 2004 to 2009 its artistic director. He also participated in the founding of the Méduse cooperative. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
