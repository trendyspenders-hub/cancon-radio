Threat Signal is a Canadian heavy metal band from Hamilton, Ontario. The band has had numerous lineup change. Jon Howard is the only remaining original member. Their music is often classified as metalcore, melodic death metal, or groove metal. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
