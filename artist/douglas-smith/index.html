Douglas Smith

Douglas Alexander Smith (born June 22, 1985) is a Canadian actor known for his work on the HBO series Big Love as Ben Henrickson. His other work includes Santa's Slay (2005), Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters (2013), Terminator Genisys (2015), Vinyl, Miss Sloane (both 2016), When We Rise (2017), The Alienist (2018–2020), and the second season of HBO's Big Little Lies (2019).
