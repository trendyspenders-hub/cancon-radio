Katie Moore

Official site

Katie Moore is a Canadian singer and songwriter based in Montreal, Quebec, who records and performs with Socalled and El Coyote, and as a solo artist. She released her debut album, Katie Moore, in 2004. Her second album Only Thing Worse, was released in 2007, and followed up with Montebello in 2011. In 2011, Moore's song "Wake Up Like This" won the SOCAN Songwriting Prize, an annual competition that honours the best song written and released by 'emerging' songwriters over the past year, as voted by the public.
