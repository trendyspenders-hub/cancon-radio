Kyla Tilley

I'm Kyla Tilley. I'm a Canadian singer-songwriter and guitar player. Here you'll find all my released music, and official videos as well as early versions of my songs performed in a most casual manner. You'll find some replays from my twitch streams here. Those are mostly me talking out loud as I write songs. I'm a big fan of Celtic music, so periodically I delve into some archive to dig up old songs and give them a bit of life. I also do a weekly Live Stream here. It's the Weekly Wednesday Warble, where I present 10 songs on a theme. Usually a mix of my originals and covers. I think that's about the size of it.
