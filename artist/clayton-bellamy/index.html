Clayton Bellamy

Clayton Bellamy is a Canadian singer-songwriter. He grew up in Bonnyville, Alberta, before his family settled in Nashville, Tennessee. He has won many awards including a JUNO for Best Country Recording, a five time Canadian Country Music Award winner Canadian Country Music Awards, and SOCAN Songwriter of the Year award, Radio Music Award song of the year, five CMAB awards for song, video, Entertainer, Group, and Roots Artist.
