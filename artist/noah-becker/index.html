Noah Becker

Noah Becker (born 1970) is an American and Canadian artist, writer, publisher of Whitehot Magazine of Contemporary Art, and jazz saxophonist who lives and works in New York City and Vancouver Island. He has written for Art in America, Canadian Art, VICE, Interview, The Guardian (UK), and the HuffPost. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
