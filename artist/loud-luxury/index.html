Loud Luxury are a Canadian music production and DJ duo consisting of Andrew Fedyk and Joe De Pace. They are currently based in Los Angeles, California. They gained recognition following their 2017 release "Body" and their remix of the Martin Garrix and Dua Lipa single "Scared to Be Lonely". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

2 wins, 8 nominations; certified diamond and gold and platinum in Canada
