Kaiya Gamble is a Canadian pop singer and songwriter from Calgary, Alberta, notably recognized as a contestant on American Idol Season 22. She is one of two Canadians to receive a golden ticket and the first Albertan in the history of the show. Gamble was the Teen First Place Winner of the American Songwriter 2023 Song Contest.
