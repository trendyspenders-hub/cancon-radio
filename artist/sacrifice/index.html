Sacrifice

Official site

Sacrifice is a Canadian thrash metal band from Toronto, Ontario. Guitarists Rob Urbinati and Joe Rico formed the band in 1983. The band released four studio albums before breaking up in 1993. After reuniting to play a concert in 2006, they released their fifth studio album, The Ones I Condemn, on Brazilian label Marquee Records in 2009. Despite numerous line-up changes, especially early on, Urbinati, Rico, and bassist Scott Watts recorded every studio album and demo with drummer Gus Pynn, except the Apocalypse Inside demo and CD.
