Félix Leclerc

Official site

Félix Leclerc (August 2, 1914 – August 8, 1988) was a Québécois singer-songwriter, poet, writer, actor and political activist. He was made an Officer of the Order of Canada on December 20, 1968. Leclerc was posthumously inducted into the Canadian Songwriters Hall of Fame for his songs "Moi, mes souliers", "Le P'tit Bonheur" and "Le Tour de l'île" in 2006. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
