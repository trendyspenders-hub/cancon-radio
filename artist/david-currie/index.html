David Currie

David Currie is a Canadian conductor who was the music director and conductor for the Ottawa Symphony Orchestra (OSO) from 1992 until 2016. Currie is also an assistant professor at the University of Ottawa, where he teaches double bass and conducting, and conducts the university orchestra. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
