Doucette

Jerry Victor Doucette was a Canadian guitarist, singer-songwriter, and leader of the band Doucette. He was noted for his hit single "Mama Let Him Play", which made the Billboard Top 100. His band won the Juno Award for Most Promising Group of the Year in 1979. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
