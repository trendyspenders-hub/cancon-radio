Robin Cisek

Robin Cisek is a Métis alt-pop artist from Edmonton, blending edgy electronic music with alternative melodies, commanding vocals and vulnerable lyrics. A two-time CBC Searchlight finalist and award-winning artist, Cisek has charted internationally on radio and has been featured in international press, including on the Global Morning show and CP24. In 2025, Cisek released her album called Tempered featuring her own production, writing and composition. Robin’s bold artistry shines in her emotionally resonant music; she has turned personal struggle into powerful art rooted in resilience. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
