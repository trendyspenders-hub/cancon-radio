The Balconies

BandcampOfficial site

The Balconies were a Canadian alternative music trio formed in Ottawa, Ontario. The band originally consisted of Liam Jaeger, and siblings Jacquie and Steve Neville. The group performed with such bands as Cold War Kids, Mother Mother, Devo, Peter Bjorn and John, USS, Sloan, Stars, Wide Mouth Mason, Big Sugar, Tokyo Police Club, Bad Religion, and Sam Roberts Band.
