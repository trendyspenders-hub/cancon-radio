Silverstein is a Canadian rock band from Burlington, Ontario, formed in 2000. Their band name is a reference to the famous children's author Shel Silverstein, whom the band had admired and whose work they had read as children. They have released a total of thirteen studio albums, seven EPs, a compilation album and a live DVD/CD. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
