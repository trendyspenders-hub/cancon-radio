Junkhouse

Junkhouse is a Canadian rock band formed in Hamilton, Ontario in 1989. They released three albums during the 1990s, which spawned numerous charting singles in Canada. They initially disbanded in 1998, and have performed occasional shows afterwards. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
