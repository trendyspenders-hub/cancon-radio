Alta Moda

Official site

Alta Moda was a Canadian funk rock band formed in 1979 in Toronto. It originally consisted of singer Molly Johnson and guitarist Norman Orenstein. Drummer Steven Gelineau and bassist Etric Lyons were added in 1982 and 1983 respectively. After performing for several years in Toronto and releasing the promotional single "Train" in 1986, the band signed to CBS Records and released their debut album, Alta Moda, in 1987.
