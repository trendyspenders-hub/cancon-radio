Olivia Khoury

Born and raised in Montreal, Olivia Khoury is a singer-songwriter and interdisciplinary artist who found her voice somewhere between dance and music. Her sound, shaped by dreams and travels, blends indie-folk, alt-pop, and jazz influences into her own luminous, hybrid universe. Inspired by Feist, Lianne La Havas, and Jordan Rakei, she combines vulnerability, authenticity and groove. Her jazz influences shine through the band's skill on stage, where the interplay between instruments and vocals makes each performance unique. Olivia won the Jazz EP of the Year at the 2023 GAMIQ awards for her bilingual debut EP, Portraits. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
