Jose Miguel Veloso, known professionally as Mico, is a Canadian singer-songwriter and musician from Toronto. He initially grew a fanbase online through Discord and Twitch, breaking out commercially with his 2022 single "cut my hair", which became a top-30 radio hit in Canada. In 2025, he signed with Columbia Records following a string of independent extended plays. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
