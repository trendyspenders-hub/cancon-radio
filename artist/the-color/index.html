The Color is a Canadian Christian music group from Winkler, Manitoba. They are a 2 time Juno Award winning band that is most noted for their 2017 album, First Day of My Life, which won the Juno Award for Contemporary Christian/Gospel Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2018, and their 2021 album, No Greater Love, which won the Juno Award for Contemporary Christian/Gospel Album of the Year in 2022. The band is led by Jordan Janzen as lead singer, Larry Abrams on guitar and vocals, and Tyson Unrau on drums. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
