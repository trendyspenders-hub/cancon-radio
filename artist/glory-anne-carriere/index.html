Glory-Anne Carriere is a Canadian country singer, who received Juno Award nominations for Most Promising Female Vocalist at the Juno Awards of 1978 and Best Country Female Vocalist at the Juno Awards of 1980. She has recorded and performed both as a solo artist and as a duo with her husband, Ronnie Prophet. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
