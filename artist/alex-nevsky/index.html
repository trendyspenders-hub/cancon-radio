Alex Nevsky

Alexandre Parent (born February 17, 1986, in Granby, Quebec, Canada), better known as Alex Nevsky, is a singer-songwriter from Quebec, Canada. He graduated from École nationale de la chanson in Granby in 2007. He participated in Festival international de la chanson de Granby (Granby International Song Festival) in 2009 reaching the semi-final. He also took part in the 14th Francouvertes contest where he was a finalist. He released his debut album De lune à l'aube in 2010 on Audiogram. The album received two nominations at the Gala ADISQ for "Revelation of the Year" and "Best Album of Year – Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
