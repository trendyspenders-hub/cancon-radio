The Stanfields

SoundCloud

The Stanfields are a Canadian rock band based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Described by some sources as "the bastard children of AC/DC and Stan Rogers", the band blends a hard rock style with elements of traditional Atlantic Canadian folk music. The Stanfields are known for their powerful live show, and they have toured mostly in Canada and Germany. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
