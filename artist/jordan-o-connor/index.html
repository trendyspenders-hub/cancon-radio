Jordan O'Connor

Jordan O'Connor (born November 20, 1972, in Ottawa, Ontario) is a Canadian musician (electric and double bass), composer and audio engineer. He is signed to Candyrat Records and also works through his own production company The Breath and is composer-in-residence for the avant-garde music ensemble Euphonia. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
