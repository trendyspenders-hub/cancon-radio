Luksimi Sivaneswaralingam

Luksimi Sivaneswaralingam (born 26 September 1993) is a Canadian playback singer and classical dancer from Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Luksimi Sivaneswaralingam was three years old when she began her lessons in music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
