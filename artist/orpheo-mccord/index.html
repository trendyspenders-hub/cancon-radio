Orpheo McCord

Orpheo McCord (born July 19, 1979) is an American composer and percussionist. He was a founding member of the now-defunct Edward Sharpe and the Magnetic Zeros and was a former member of The Fall, Fool's Gold and Cass McCombs. His debut solo album, Recovery Inhale, was released on January 26, 2018. His second album, Music for Ketamine Therapy Vol. I, was released on October 8, 2023. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
