Prime Boys

Prime Boys are a Canadian hip hop collective based in Toronto. The original lineup consisted of Jay Whiss, Jimmy Prime, and Donnie, and the trio released their debut studio album, Koba World, in 2018. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
