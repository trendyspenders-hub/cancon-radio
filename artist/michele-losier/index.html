Michèle Losier

Michèle Losier (born March 27, 1978) is a Canadian mezzo-soprano. A laureate of the Queen Elisabeth Competition (2008), she has appeared at major opera houses including the Metropolitan Opera, the Paris Opera, La Scala, the Royal Opera House, the Vienna State Opera, and La Monnaie. She teaches voice at the Royal Conservatoire Antwerp and the Royal Conservatory of Brussels. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
