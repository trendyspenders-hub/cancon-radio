Will Blunderfield

Mark William Kent Blunderfield (born July 18, 1985) is a Canadian singer-songwriter and yoga teacher. He is signed to Nettwerk Records/Sony Music (WMG) with music released through Spirit Voyage Records. Since 2021, Blunderfield has received media attention for his "male rewilding" practice, which uses methods including semen retention to help men "connect with their bodies and each other".
