Zachary Lucky (born January 4, 1989) is a Canadian songwriter, folk and Country artist based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. He is the grandson of Canadian country music artist John "Smilin Johnnie" Lucky. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
