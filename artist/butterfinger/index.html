Butterfinger

Butterfinger was a Saskatchewan-based Canadian alternative rock band which released one independently recorded studio album. Their song Breathe won several awards including the national songwriting competition in conjunction with Canadian Music Week in 2001. Its success inspired the band to work on a full-length album, directed by Producer, Mixer, and Engineer Jared Kuemper. Butterfinger was nominated for "Outstanding Rock Recording" at the Western Canadian Music Awards in 2003.
