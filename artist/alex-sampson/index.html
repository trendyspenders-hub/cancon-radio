Alex Darren Sampson is a Canadian singer and songwriter, best known for finishing as a semi-finalist on the 19th season of America's Got Talent. His sophomore EP Hopeless Romantic (2024) was released by Warner Music Group, and featured his single "Pretty Baby". He was featured on People magazine's "Emerging Artists" list in 2023.
