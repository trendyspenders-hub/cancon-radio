Dead Obies

BandcampOfficial site

Dead Obies is an experimental hip hop band originating from South Shore, Quebec, Canada, that was formed in 2011 by a collective of five MCs: Jo RCA, Yes McCan, Snail Kid, 20Some and O.G. Bear, and Quebec producer Vince Carter. The group identifies itself as post-rap. They are signed to the independent label Bonsound. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
