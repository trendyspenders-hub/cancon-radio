Barbara Hannigan

Barbara Hannigan is a Canadian soprano and conductor, and is currently the Chief Conductor and Artistic Director of the Iceland Symphony Orchestra. She is particularly known for her performances of contemporary classical music, and in 2025, she received the Polar Music Prize. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
