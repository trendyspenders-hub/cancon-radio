INCURA

Official site

INCURA is a Canadian cinematic metal band based in Lethbridge, Alberta. Their musical style is a unique blend of hard rock, prog and metal instrumentals, with high vocals. The band's name is a combination of their previous name, INVEIN, and the Cura spell from the Final Fantasy game series. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
