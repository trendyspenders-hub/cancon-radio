Devora

A desperado of modern culture, DEVORA, bridges outlaw country melodies with dark pop hooks drawing you into her surrealist western world. Coining the genre, “Outlaw Pop”, DEVORA weaves visual soundscapes from a moody surrealist wild-west dream world. DEVORA is not just music, but an immersive multimedia artistic experience of the that world, re-imagined in 2020. It’s not her story, but all of our stories, to choose your own adventure; to harness your own inner outlaw.
