Bruce Coughlan

I’m Bruce Coughlan and I am a Pacific Canadian songsmith, storyteller and a career performing artist with a lifetime of recording and touring history. My Music: I am what you might term a Roots Music chameleon. Throughout my career I have immersed myself in all fashions of Roots Music including Celtic, Folk & Americana, World Music, Retro Soul, Rhythm & Blues. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
