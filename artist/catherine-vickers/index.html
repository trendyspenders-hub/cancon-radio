Catherine Vickers

Catherine Vickers (born 1952 in Regina) is a Canadian pianist. Vickers studied in Edmonton and then in Germany with Bernhard Ebert and Hans Leygraf at Hochschule für Musik und Theater Hannover. She won the 1979 Ferruccio Busoni International Piano Competition and was awarded the 1981 Sydney Competition's 3rd prize. As a classical pianist she performed in Europe, North and South America and Asia, her repertoire includes standard and avantgardistic works of the piano literature.
