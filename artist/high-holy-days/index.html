High Holy Days

High Holy Days is a Canadian post-grunge music group based in North Bay, Ontario, Canada. The most current lineup consists of vocalist Marc Arcand, guitarist Bill MacGregor, bassist Chris Amey, and drummer Brian Finner. Former members of the band include Dave Thompson (guitarist), Jeremy Galda (bassist), Brian Bird (drummer), Brett Hind (guitarist), and Jason Guindon (drummer). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
