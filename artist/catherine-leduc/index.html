Catherine Leduc

Catherine Leduc is a Canadian singer-songwriter from Trois-Rivières, Quebec. Formerly lead singer of the indie pop band Tricot Machine, she has released several solo albums under her own name since the end of that project, although her Tricot Machine bandmate Matthieu Beaumont remains involved in her solo career as a collaborator. With Tricot Machine, she won the SOCAN Songwriting Prize (French category) in 2007 as the writer of the band's single "L'Ours".
