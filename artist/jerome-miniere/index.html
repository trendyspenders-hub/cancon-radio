New album “La dérive des rêves,” released on October 16, 2026 Jérôme Minière is a singer and multidisciplinary artist from Orléans (France), based in Montreal. After studying cinema at INSAS in the early 90s, he has released over a dozen albums, garnering numerous awards in Canada and Europe. He works as a composer, songwriter, producer and remixer with numerous artists. His curiosity has also led him to collaborate in the visual arts, video, film, dance, theater and performance. He has also published a first novel, L'enfance de l'art (2014). Source: the artist's YouTube channel
