Jane Archibald

Jane Archibald is a Canadian operatic soprano from Nova Scotia. Born in Truro, she began her professional opera career in Canada before joining the San Francisco Opera as an Adler Fellow. Her career was elevated by a performance as Zerbinetta in Ariadne auf Naxos in 2007, a role she has reprised more than any other. Her first solo CD was the winner of a 2012 Juno Award for Classical Album of the Year. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
