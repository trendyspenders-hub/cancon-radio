LOV

LOV is a Treaty 6 Saskatchewan-based artist known for her smoky, expressive voice and empowering odes to independence and strength. LOV embraced a career to pursue her authentic vision, crafting music inspired by UK soul with Montréal producer Connor Seidel. Together, they’ve created a collection of elegant, heartfelt tracks that blend maturity and fun. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
