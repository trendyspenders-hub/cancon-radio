Simone Andrea Denny is a Canadian singer and songwriter. She is best known as a vocalist with the dance music groups, BKS, Love Inc. and Widelife, appearing most prominently on the hit dance singles "Broken Bones", "You're a Superstar", and "All Things (Just Keep Getting Better)".
