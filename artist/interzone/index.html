Interzone

Interzone were a German blues/rock/heavy metal band from the early 1980s, headed by vocalist Heiner Pudelko. During the period from 1979 to 1986 the band released three albums and a number of singles. In 2019, 24 years after the death of Pudelko, Interzone released the album Letzte Ausfahrt, which featured songs with lyrics set to the works of Wolf Wondratschek.
