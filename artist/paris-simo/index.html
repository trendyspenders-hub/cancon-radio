Paris & Simo, previously known as Paris FZ and Simo T, was a Canadian electronic dance music duo from Montreal, consisting of Peiras "Paris" Fotis-Zoubris and Simone "Simo" Teti. Formed in 2010, they performed at Las Vegas' Electric Daisy Carnival, TomorrowWorld, Pier 94 and Guvernment among others. They also toured alongside David Guetta in Canada and frequently performed in various locations such as Japan, Indonesia, Malaysia, South America and others. The group changed their name in 2018, with Paris performing solo as Prince Paris and Simo working only on production. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
