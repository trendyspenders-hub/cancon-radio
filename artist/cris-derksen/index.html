Cris Derksen

Crystal Dawn Derksen (April 20, 1981 – May 15, 2026) was a Canadian Cree two-spirit cellist and composer from Northern Alberta. Nominated for a Juno Award, Derksen was known for her unique musical sound which blends classical music with traditional Indigenous music. Her music is often described as "electronic cello" or classical traditional fusion. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
