Obscene Eulogy

Obscene Eulogy is a Canadian death/black metal band from Saint John, New Brunswick. The band recorded with Impaled Nazarene's Mika Luttinen on vocals. Their current singer is Tapio Wilska of Finntroll. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
