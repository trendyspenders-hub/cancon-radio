Alessia Patrizia De Gasperis-Brigante, formerly known as Kai, is a Canadian singer-songwriter from Toronto, Ontario. Best known for her collaborations, she co-wrote and was featured on Flume's Grammy-nominated "Never Be Like You", Diplo's "Revolution", and "Mind" from Jack Ü's Grammy award-winning 2015 album.
