Flashlight Brown were a Canadian punk rock band, originally from Guelph. They were formed in 1995. At the time, they were just known as Flashlight. The band's most known lineup consisted of vocalist/guitarist Matt Hughes, bassist/vocalist Fil Bucchino, drummer Tim Thomson, and guitarist Mike Conroy. They're known for their singles "Ready To Roll" and "I'm A Human", as well as their cover of "Save It for Later". Their music received some airtime on radio and TV stations in Canada (such as MuchMusic) and has been featured in Rugrats Go Wild, Smallville, and Sky High. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
