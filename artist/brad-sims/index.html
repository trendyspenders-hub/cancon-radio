Brad Sims

CCMA Country Music Recording Artist. Traditional sound with a modern twist! If you love 90’s Country Music you’ll love Brad Sims! Multi award winning. With a top 10 single on the National Aboriginal Music Charts. Named the Male Indie Artist to Watch in 2016. High energy, with vocals compared to Randy Travis and moves all his own.
