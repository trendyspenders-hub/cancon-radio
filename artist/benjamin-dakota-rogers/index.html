Benjamin Dakota Rogers is a Canadian folk singer-songwriter. He is most noted for his 2023 album Paint Horse, for which he was a Juno Award nominee for Traditional Roots Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2024. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
