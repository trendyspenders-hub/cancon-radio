Heather Blush

Heather Blush is a Canadian jazz singer. She performs with her band The Uppercuts, which includes Steve Hazlett (drums) and Dale Ulan (upright bass), under the name Heather Blush and the Uppercuts. In The Calgary Sun, her voice was described as a combination of Joni Mitchell and Norah Jones. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
