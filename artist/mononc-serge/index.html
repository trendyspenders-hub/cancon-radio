Mononc' Serge

SpotifyOfficial site

Mononc' Serge is a Québécois musician and poet known for his satirical songs about politicians, local celebrities, and social issues. His lyrics often mix humor with sharp criticism, particularly of the Quebec federalist movement and Canadian politics. "Mononc' Serge" translates to "Unc Serge" in French.
