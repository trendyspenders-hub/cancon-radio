Salome Bey

Salome Bey (October 10, 1933 – August 8, 2020) was a U.S.-born Canadian singer-songwriter, composer, and actress who lived in Toronto, Ontario, from 1966. In 2005, she was made an honorary Member of the Order of Canada. In 2022 she was honoured by Canada Post with a commemorative postage stamp for her contributions to Canadian music and theatre.
