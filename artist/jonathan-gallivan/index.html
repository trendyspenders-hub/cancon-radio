Jonathan Gallivan

Jonathan Gallivan (born in Toronto, Ontario, living in Gatineau, Québec) is a producer, musician, and CTO for Reimagine AI in Montréal. He co-founded the band My Brilliant Beast with Byron Kent Wong and Julia Galios in 1993. For 1995, My Brilliant Beast's song “Fall Away”, from their self-titled EP, was a Top 30 hit on Canadian radio and featured in an episode of the hit TV show Melrose Place. He joined Random Media Core Inc. in 1993, a Canadian company dedicated to music, design, marketing, and new media.
