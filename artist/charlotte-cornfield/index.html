Charlotte Cornfield (born October 14, 1988) is a Canadian singer-songwriter. She has released six albums: Two Horses (2011), Future Snowbird (2016), The Shape of Your Name (2019), Highs in the Minuses (2021), Could Have Done Anything (2023), and Hurts Like Hell (2026) Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
