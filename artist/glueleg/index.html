Glueleg

Glueleg is a Canadian alternative rock band that was formed in 1990 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The band released three albums and one EP before disbanding in 1998. In 2023, a newly-reformed version of the band released an album entitled Horror Vacui on multiple streaming platforms. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
