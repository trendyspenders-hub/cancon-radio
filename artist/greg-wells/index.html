Greg Wells

Greg Wells (born 1968) is a Grammy winning and Oscar nominated Canadian record producer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and mix engineer. He has worked with Quincy Jones, Adele, Kid Cudi, Ariana Grande, Timbaland, Burt Bacharach, Cynthia Erivo, Lin-Manuel Miranda, Celine Dion, Rufus Wainwright, Taylor Swift, Michael Bublé, Ryan Tedder, OneRepublic, John Legend, Missy Elliott, Josh Groban, P!nk, Mika, Aerosmith, Sir George Martin, Sir Andrew Lloyd Webber, Stephen Schwartz, Dua Lipa, Crash Test Dummies, Deftones, Katy Perry, Weezer, Otep, and Twenty One Pilots.
