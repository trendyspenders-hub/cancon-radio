Luke McMaster

Luke McMaster (born March 22, 1976) is a Canadian singer, songwriter, recording artist and producer. McMaster experienced early success as half of the music duo McMaster & James originating from Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Named after McMaster and his performing partner, Rob James, the duo released a self-titled gold album and scored the radio hits "Love Wins Everytime", "Thank You," "I Understand", and "Sweet Sensation." Despite their initial success, the band dissolved in 2002. Since then, McMaster has worked as a songwriter, producer, solo performing and recording artist living in Toronto Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
