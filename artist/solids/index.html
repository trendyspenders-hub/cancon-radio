Solids were a Canadian punk rock band from Montreal, Quebec, active in the 2010s. Consisting of singer and guitarist Xavier Germain Poitras and drummer Louis Guillemette, the duo released their sole full-length album Blame Confusion on Fat Possum Records in 2014. The album was longlisted for the 2014 Polaris Music Prize. They subsequently added Guillaume Chiasson as a second guitarist, and followed up in 2016 with the EP Else.
