Willie Thrasher

SpotifyDeezer

Willie Thrasher is a Canadian Inuvialuk musician from Aklavik, Northwest Territories. He has recorded both as a solo artist, and as a member of several bands, including The Cordells, and Red Cedar, with Morley Loon. Thrasher has advocated for Inuit and First Nations issues for much of his career. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
