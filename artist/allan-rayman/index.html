Allan Rayman is a singer/songwriter based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. He is signed to Communion Music and has released six albums: Hotel Allan (2016), Roadhouse 01 (2017), Harry Hard-on (2018), Christian (2020), Roadhouse 02 (2022), and The All Allan Hour (2024), as well as many EPs, including Courtney (2017) and Verona's Mixtape (2020).
