Ora Cogan's approach to songwriting melds traditional, experimental, and psychedelic into something wholly unique. Cogan is known for her singular voice and cinematic compositions. She has collaborated with a multitude of artists while touring extensively throughout Europe & North America, sharing the stage with the likes of Grouper and Mazzy Star. Her new album Hard Hearted Woman is to be released March 13th on Sacred Bones Records. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
