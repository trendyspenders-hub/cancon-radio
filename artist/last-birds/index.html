Last Birds

Canadian Folk Music Award nominated artists, Lindsay Arnold and Mike Davis. Last Birds' mesmerizing combination of close harmonies and dynamic guitar work harkens back to the early days of country folk music while exploring dilemmas of the modern day. The duo’s simple, two voices, two guitars and one microphone live performance style allows for dark, haunting storytelling to shine through while their on-stage banter reflects the humour infused in their relationship.
