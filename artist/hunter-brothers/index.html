Hunter Brothers is a Canadian country music group from Shaunavon, Saskatchewan, composed of brothers Luke, J.J., Ty, Brock, and Dusty Hunter. They signed with Open Road Recordings and released their debut single, "El Dorado", in February 2016. It debuted on the Billboard Canada Country chart in March 2016. The band earned their first Top 10 single at Canadian country radio with their hit "Born and Raised". In 2019, their single "Lost" became their first #1 hit. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
