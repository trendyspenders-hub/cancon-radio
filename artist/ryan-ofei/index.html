Ryan Ofei

Ryan Ofei is a contemporary Christian artist whose music is rooted in worshipping God in spirit and in truth. Of Ghanaian and Canadian heritage, his sound reflects the beauty of the global Church, drawing from gospel, R&B, African rhythms, and modern worship to create music that bridges cultures and generations. His debut album Restore (June 2024) connected with listeners around the world, surpassing 30 million streams and earning the 2025 JUNO Award for Contemporary Christian/Gospel Album of the Year. As a member of GRAMMY-winning collective Maverick City Music, Ryan gained international recognition through projects such as Jubilee and Kingd Source: the artist's YouTube channel
