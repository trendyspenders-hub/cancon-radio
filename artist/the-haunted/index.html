The Haunted

The Haunted is a Swedish heavy metal band from Gothenburg, formed in 1996. The original members were Anders and Jonas Björler, Peter Dolving, Adrian Erlandsson and Patrik Jensen. Both of the Björler brothers and Erlandsson were members of the seminal melodic death metal band At the Gates, a pioneering force in the Swedish death metal scene. The band currently consists of Marco Aro, Patrik Jensen, Ola Englund, Jonas Bjorler, and Adrian Erlandsson. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
