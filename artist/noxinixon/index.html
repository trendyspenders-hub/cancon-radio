NOXINIXON

Official site

www.noxinixon.com Canadian Folk-Rock. A diverse band from all parts of Canada. Donny Childs (acoustic guitar, vocals), William Mowat (rhythm guitar, backing vocals), Kerry Hirsh (lead guitar), Alexis Dumont (bass guitar) and Jarrett Plett (Drums) form Noxinixon, weaving folk-harmonies with catchy rock riffs.
