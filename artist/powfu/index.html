Isaiah Faber, known professionally as Powfu, is a Canadian rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer. He is the son of Dave Faber from the band Faber Drive. He amassed popularity following the release of his first charting single, "Death Bed ", featuring Beabadoobee, which peaked at number 23 on the Billboard Hot 100. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
