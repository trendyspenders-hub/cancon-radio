Erika Angell

Swedish vocalist and composer Erika Angell has spent the past two decades in the avant-garde music scenes of Stockholm from 2004 and Montréal from 2012. Across sixteen independently produced albums and innumerable collaborations, Angell has been a poetic and audacious force in genre-defying music. Her primary projects include the Polaris-nominated art-rock group Thus Owls, the New Music trio Beatings Are In The Body and more recently solo performances under her own name. Her voice has been described as “a guiding presence” (Exclaim) that “sits alongside the greatest indie female artists who are not afraid of anything” (Le Devoir).
