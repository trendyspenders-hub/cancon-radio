Jennifer Russell

Jennifer Wylie-Russell is a Canadian physician who was Chief Medical Officer of Health from 2015 to 2023 for the province of New Brunswick, Canada. Her handling of the COVID-19 pandemic in New Brunswick brought her widespread visibility in Canada. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
