Kicksie

Kicksie is a Canadian Indie Rock musician, singer-songwriter, and producer based in Toronto, Ontario. The project began in 2017 with Giuliana Mormile writing, recording, and producing her own music. She released four full-length albums independently before signing with Counter Intuitive Records in 2023 for her album Slouch. After parting ways with the label, Kicksie returned to independent releases for her sixth studio album, BIG SUCKER, on June 6, 2025. Kicksie has toured extensively in North America as a headlining act, as well as with bands such as Wheatus and Oso Oso. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
