Matt Haimovitz

Matt Haimovitz (Hebrew: מאט חיימוביץ; born December 3, 1970) is a cellist based in the United States and Canada. Born in Israel, he grew up in the US from the age of five. He plays mainly a cello made by Matteo Goffriller in 1710. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
