God Made Me Funky

Official site

God Made Me Funky (GMMF) is a Canadian funk music group, formed in 1996 in Toronto. The group started as an "instrumental funk fusion experiment", and developed a popular following in the Toronto music scene. Their self-titled debut album was released in 2005, and was supported by a cross-Canada concert tour and airplay on CBC Radio 3. Their second album, We Can All Be Free, was released in 2007, and garnered a Juno Award nomination for R&B/Soul Recording of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2008. The album featured guest appearances by Maestro, Michie Mee and Thrust. The single "Won More Time" Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
