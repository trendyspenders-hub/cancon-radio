Sean Ashby

Sean Ashby is a singer, songwriter and guitarist who has performed with Sarah McLachlan since 1996. He also played and recorded with Delerium, Ginger (formerly Grapes of Wrath), Wild Strawberries, Mae Moore, DMC of Run-D.M.C. and many others. Ashby formed the group Jack Tripper in 1999. He recorded two albums, Jack Tripper and 9 Easy Pieces and released a solo album "Brass and Gold" in 2008. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
