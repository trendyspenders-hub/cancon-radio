RALEIGH is a Canadian experimental indie-rock band from Calgary, Alberta. Born out of a collaboration between songwriters Brock Geiger and Clea Anais in 2010, the band has evolved to currently include Matt Doherty (drums) and Will Maclellan (bass). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
