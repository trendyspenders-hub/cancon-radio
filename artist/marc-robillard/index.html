Marc Robillard (born March 23, 1974) is a Canadian musician, composer and songwriter, residing in Venice, California. He is best known for his songs "So Much More" (featured on a nationwide Sun Chips commercial) and "Contagious" (featured on MTV's Jersey Shore). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
