Joshua Henry

Joshua Henry is a three time Tony and Grammy-nominated Broadway performer, writer, and musician. His vision is to celebrate the mindful actions artists take to thrive and learn how we can improve our relationship with our art. Joshua's debut album 'GROW' is out now.
