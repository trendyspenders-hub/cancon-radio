Jo Hikk

Jo Hikk is a Canadian country music group founded in Calgary, Alberta. This four-piece band is composed of brothers Kelly Sitter (lead vocals, bass guitar) and Kenny Sitter (guitar, ganjo, vocals), Al Doell (drums, vocals) and Don Jorgensen (keyboards, mandolin). Their first album, Ride, was re-released in early 2009. Their first single, "Closer", charted in the Top 40. The song was written and originally recorded by Thrasher Shiver in 1996. Jo Hikk scored two more hit singles with "My Kind Of Radio" and "Sweet City Woman" (cover version of a hit song by The Stampeders, both hit top #20) Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
