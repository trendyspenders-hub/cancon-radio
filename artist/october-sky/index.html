October Sky

October Sky is a Canadian rock band from Montreal, Canada. Formed in 2000, the group currently consists of lead vocalist and guitarist Karl Raymond and drummer Alex Racine. The band's early music, including their debut album Hell Isn't My Home, has been described as progressive. They later incorporated electronic elements into their sound on subsequent releases. The Aphotic Season marked the beginning of this stylistic shift, while their most recent EP, Rise, represented a significant evolution in their musical direction.
