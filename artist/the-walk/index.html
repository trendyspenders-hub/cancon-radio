The Walk

The Walk were a Canadian alternative rock band. Originally from Sudbury, Ontario, Canada, the band was formed in 1987 by vocalist/guitarist Dave Allan, guitarist/keyboardist Jason Frederick, bass guitarist Eric Forget, and drummer James Prudhomme. They disbanded in 1996. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
