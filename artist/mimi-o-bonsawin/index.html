Mimi O'Bonsawin

Award-winning alt-folk singer-songwriter Mimi O'Bonsawin creates music rooted in nature, storytelling, and connection. French Canadian and W8banaki (Abénaki), and a member of the Odanak First Nation, she blends folk traditions with groove and playful experimentation into a sound all her own. Her forthcoming album, Evoke (August 28, 2026), explores identity, language, and the natural world through richly textured songs and heartfelt collaborations.
