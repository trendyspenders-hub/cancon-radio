Marie-Josée Simard

Official site

Marie-Josée Simard (born November 29, 1956) is a Canadian percussionist and music educator living in Quebec. She was born in La Baie (now part of Saguenay). She performed on the vibraphone with her parents' orchestra. Simard was taught by her mother until she entered the Conservatoire de musique et d'art dramatique du Québec in 1974. Then, from 1976 to 1979, she studied at the Conservatoire de musique du Québec à Montréal (CMM).
