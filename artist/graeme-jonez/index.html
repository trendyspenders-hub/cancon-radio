Graeme Jonez

Graeme Jonez is an Ojibway singer-songwriter based in Toronto, Ontario. A storyteller at heart, Jonez's Indigenous culture and love for old folk & blues music have shaped his unique musical style, a blend of folk, rock and indie pop. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
