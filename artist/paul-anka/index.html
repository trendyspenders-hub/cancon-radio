Paul Albert Anka is a Canadian and American singer-songwriter and actor. His songs include "Diana", "You Are My Destiny", "Lonely Boy", "Put Your Head on My Shoulder" and "(You're) Having My Baby". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

2 wins, 10 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
