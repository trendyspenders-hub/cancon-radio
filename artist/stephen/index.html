Stephen

Raised in a quiet Virginian town, the 27 year old artist Stephen possesses a unique skillset as a singer, writer, instrumentalist, and producer on all his records and molds a modern yet raw sound. With transparent vulnerability in his worlds rarely seen in young artists, he has cultivated a fan base through his personal reflections and experiences that resonate with the contemporary youth. After the stunning success of his first single Bullet Train which garnered hundreds of millions of plays as an independent release, Stephen dropped out of his final year of college to write his debut album Sincerely, a societal call for empathetic awareness Source: the artist's YouTube channel
