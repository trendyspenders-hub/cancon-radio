Let's Go to War

Let's Go to War is a Canadian electronic music trio formed in Toronto, Ontario, Canada in 2008. The music group is made up of members Henry Walter, Adrien Gough, and Peter-John Kerr. The band is signed to Last Gang Records. Let's Go to War has remixed songs for Jully Black, Fritz Helder & The Phantoms, Brick & Lace, The Carps, and Hilary Duff. In 2008, Let's Go To War produced and co-wrote the track "Mmm Papi", alongside Nicole Morier and Britney Spears, which appeared on Spears' album Circus.
