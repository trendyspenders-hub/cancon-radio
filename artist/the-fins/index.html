The Fins

The Fins are a charming rock ensemble based in Naarm/Melbourne, Australia. THe Fins stylishly flaunt their eclecticism through an amazingly colourful & varying palette of musical styles & influences. The Fins have been building up a solid reputation for their unapologetically theatrical & dynamic live shows. The Fins boldly thrust their musical statements onto display within the execution of their energetic performances. The Fins aim is to stun and to massage your sweet ear holes to the point where you find yourself involuntarily curling your toes. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
