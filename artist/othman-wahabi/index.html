Othman Wahabi

Bandcamp

Othman Wahabi is a Moroccan‑Canadian blues musician, singer, and multi‑instrumentalist whose slide‑guitar work, earthy vocals, and soulful songwriting transcend borders. Drawing on his African heritage and the raw spirit of the blues, he shapes a mystical, roots‑driven musical language that feels both ageless and unmistakably personal. He is also the founder of Mudgravel Blues, a hard‑hitting blues rock power trio built around heavy grooves, swamp‑soaked guitar, and a raw, driving energy. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
