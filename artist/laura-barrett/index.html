Laura Barrett

Official site

Laura Alexandra Barrett is a Canadian indie pop singer-songwriter, best known for incorporating the kalimba into her music. In addition to her solo work, Barrett has also performed as a member of The Hidden Cameras, Henri Fabergé and the Adorables, Woodhands, and Sheezer. Barrett was born in Toronto. She studied English literature and linguistics at the University of Toronto, graduating in 2006. She later returned to school to complete a master of education and works as an occasional teacher within the Toronto District School Board.
