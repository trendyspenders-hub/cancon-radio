Briannah Charlie Donolo, known professionally as Rêve, is a Canadian singer and songwriter. She is best known for her singles "Ctrl + Alt + Del", which won the 2023 Juno Award for Dance Recording of the Year and was certified Double Platinum in Canada, and "Whitney", which topped the US Dance radio chart. Her debut studio album Saturn Return received a Juno nomination for Pop Album of the Year. Her second studio album, Delusions of Grandeur, is scheduled for release in 2026.
