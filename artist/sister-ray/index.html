Sister Ray is the stage name of Ella Coyes, a Métis singer-songwriter. They were born and raised in Sturgeon County, Alberta. Their stage name was taken from The Velvet Underground song of the Sister Ray. Their debut full-length album Communion was released in May 2022 on Royal Mountain Records, and was longlisted for the 2022 Polaris Music Prize. Coyes has been based in Toronto, Ontario since 2020.
