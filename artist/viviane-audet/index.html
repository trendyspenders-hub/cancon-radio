Viviane Audet

Viviane Audet (born November 10, 1981) is a Canadian actress, singer and pianist from Quebec. She is most noted as a frequent collaborator with her husband Robin-Joël Cool, both in the band Mentana and on film and television scores. She released her debut album, Le Long jeu, in 2006.
