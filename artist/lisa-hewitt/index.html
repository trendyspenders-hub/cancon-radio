Lisa Hewitt

Lisa Hewitt is a Canadian country music singer. Hewitt released her self-titled debut album on the independent Socan Records in 1999. Her second album, The Road I Chose, was released in 2004 by Royalty Records. Its first single, "One of These Goodbyes," reached the Top 25 on the Radio & Records Canadian country singles chart. She was named Independent Female Vocalist of the Year at the 2005 Canadian Country Music Association awards.
