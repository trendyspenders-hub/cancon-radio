Love You to Death

Love You to Death (formerly The Pettit Project) was a Canadian pop punk band from Toronto, Ontario, active from 1999 to 2015. Founded by vocalist, guitarist, and songwriter Scott Komer, the group was signed to Affluence Music Group. Over sixteen years, the band released fifteen recordings under both names and developed a rotating cast of members that included future participants in Boys Night Out, Avril Lavigne's touring band, Not by Choice, Cuff the Duke, The Creepshow, and Gregory Pepper's solo project.
