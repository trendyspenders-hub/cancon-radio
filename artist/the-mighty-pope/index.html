The Mighty Pope

The Mighty Pope (born Earle Heedram; October 23, 1945) is a Jamaican-Canadian singer. Known for his vocal strength (hence the "Mighty" prefix in his stage handle) and sharp visual presentation (custom tailored suits, shirts, capes, and footwear), he was marketed as a something of a "sex symbol" at the height of his recording career in the mid-to-late 1970s.
