Marie Carmen

Marie Carmen is the stage name of Marie-Carmen Aubut (born August 24, 1959), a Canadian pop singer-songwriter and musical theatre actress born in Sainte-Foy, Quebec. Associated with the bands Accident, Blood et Marie and The Radio Rats in the early 1980s, she subsequently became a backing vocalist for Claude Dubois and The Box, and was later selected by Luc Plamondon to play the role of Marie-Jeanne in a revival of Starmania.
