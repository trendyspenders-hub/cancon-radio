Priori

Priori is the stage name of Francis Latreille, a Canadian electronic musician from Montreal, Quebec. He is most noted for his 2024 album This But More, which won the Juno Award for Electronic Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2025. His prior albums have included On a Nimbus (2019) and Your Own Power (2021). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
