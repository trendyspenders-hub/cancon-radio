David Bradstreet

David Bradstreet (born London, England) is a Canadian-based musician. He spent his childhood in Oakville, Ontario and began his music career in the late 1960s. He is best known for his song "Renaissance" ("Let’s Dance That Old Dance Once More") that was performed by Canadian country and folk musician Valdy. He won a Juno Award in 1978 for "most promising male vocalist" and was nominated three more times for best instrumental album. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
