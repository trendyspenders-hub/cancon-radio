The Knobs

The Knobs is a Filipino pop and alternative rock band formed in 2019. The group consists of family members Jayr Corre (Vocals and Bass), Marco Corre (Drums), and Raffy Corre (Guitar). The Knobs' music often explores themes of love, heartbreak, and personal experiences. Their notable tracks include "Walang Kapantay," "Paalam," "Liwanag," "Paglisan," "Wala Na," "Kung Pwede Lang," and "Oh Giliw Ko." These songs have garnered significant attention on platforms. Known for their engaging live performances, The Knobs have appeared on programs like GMA Playlist, showcasing their musical versatility. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
