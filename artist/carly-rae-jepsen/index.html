Carly Rae Jepsen is a Canadian singer, songwriter and actress. After studying musical theatre for most of her school life and while in university, Jepsen garnered mainstream attention after placing third on the fifth season of Canadian Idol in 2007. Jepsen released her folk-influenced debut studio album Tug of War the following year. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

3 wins, 5 nominations; certified diamond and gold and platinum in Canada
