Lili Haydn

Lili Haydn is a Canadian-born violinist, film and TV composer, singer-songwriter, and former child actor. She won a Grammy Award in 2019 for Opium Moon and was nominated for an Emmy Award for "Outstanding Original Music And Lyrics" in the TV series, Ginny & Georgia in 2023. In 2025, she was nominated for a Grammy Award for Lullabies For The Brokenhearted for Best Chamber Music/Small Ensemble Performance. As a solo artist she has released four albums.
