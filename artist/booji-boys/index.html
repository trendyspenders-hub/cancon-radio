Booji Boys

Bandcamp

Booji Boys are a Canadian garage punk band from Halifax, Nova Scotia. Their music blends DIY Hardcore and Power Pop. The band consists of vocalist Alex Mitchell, guitarists Cody Googoo and Steve Earle, bassist Adam LeDrew and drummer Justin Crowe, and are named for a Devo reference. They are most noted for their 2017 album Weekend Rocker, which was a longlisted nominee for the 2018 Polaris Music Prize. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
