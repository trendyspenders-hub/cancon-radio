Anthony Aaron Lines (born November 17, 1977) is a Canadian country music singer. He has recorded for RCA Nashville, BNA and On Ramp Records, and has charted three singles on the Hot Country Songs charts in the United States, in addition to landing one number one hit on the Canada Country chart.
