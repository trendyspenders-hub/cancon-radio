Skytone

Skytone are a Canadian duo from Ottawa consisting of brothers Rodney and Darius Doddridge. Their sound is generally considered jangle, indie pop with Rodney and Darius singing and playing all instruments on their albums. Recording and mixing is done at their home studio and albums are produced by Rodney. All album releases are on the Ottawa-based label The Beautiful Music. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
