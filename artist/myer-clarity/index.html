Myer Clarity

Myer Clarity, 32, is a Hip-Hop artist and ‘genre bending’ producer based in Toronto, Canada with 5 self-produced albums under his belt. His unique blend of self-aware storytelling and sad raspy punk-rock vocals have gained him a dedicated 71K listenership of like-minded moody millennials from all corners of the world. Humbly sitting at 300K Spotify streams with big name features from Down With Webster’s own Camm Hunter as well as Classified protege Kayo, Myer Clarity is a force to be reckoned with.
