Northern Lights

"Tears Are Not Enough" is a 1985 charity single recorded by a supergroup of Canadian artists, under the name Northern Lights, to raise funds for relief of the 1983–85 famine in Ethiopia. It was one of a number of charity singles of that time frame such as "Do They Know It's Christmas?", "We Are the World", "Cantaré, cantarás" , "Éthiopie" and "Les Yeux de la faim". Although recorded independently of the USA for Africa project, it was included on the full-length We Are the World album.
