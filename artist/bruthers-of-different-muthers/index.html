Bruthers of Different Muthers

Bruthers of Different Muthers was a Canadian rock band from Winnipeg, Manitoba, known for blending grunge-inspired hard rock with elements of First Nations music. The band consisted of Donovan Bruyere on lead vocals and guitar, Jesse Green on guitar, Leroy Constant on bass, and Michel Bruyere on drums. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
