Gene Martynec

Eugene "Gene" Martynec (born March 28 1947) is a Canadian musician, composer, and record producer. Martynec has worked across multiple musical disciplines as a guitarist, synthesist, pianist, and bassist. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
