Harry Manx is a Canadian musician who blends blues, folk music, and Hindustani classical music. Manx plays guitar, banjo, harmonica, and mohan veena, studying on the latter instrument with its inventor, Vishwa Mohan Bhatt. He releases his work on his own label, Dog My Cats Records.
