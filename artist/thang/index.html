Thắng

Vũ Đinh Trọng Thắng (born 6 November 1995), better known as Thắng, is a Vietnamese singer-songwriter and the record producer. He is a songwriter, lead vocalist, rhythm guitarist, and co-founder of band Ngọt, playing a major role in establishing the group as one of the most influential indie pop acts in the Vietnamese music scene. His compositions are often directly inspired by his own thoughts and personal experiences. He currently pursues an independent career following the band's dissolution in 2024.
