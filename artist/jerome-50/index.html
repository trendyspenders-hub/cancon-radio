Jérôme 50

Jérôme 50 has Quebec tattooed on his heart. A true lover of words, Jérôme 50 handles them with as much ingenuity as our great poets. From trad and pop punk to disco and ska, Jérôme is everywhere you least expect him. Building on the success of his debut album La hiérarchill and his project Le camp de vacances de Jérôme 49, his song “Tokébakicitte” broke airplay records and topped the French radio charts. On his second album, “Antigéographiquement”, Jérôme 50 continues to explore familiar themes such as Quebecoisness.
