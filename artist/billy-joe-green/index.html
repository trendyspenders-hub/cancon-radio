Billy Joe Green

Billy Joe Green is an Anishinaabe rock and blues musician from Canada. He is most noted as a three-time Juno Award nominee for Indigenous Music Album of the Year, receiving nominations at the Juno Awards of 2002 for My Ojibway Experience: Strength & Hope, at the Juno Awards of 2006 for Muskrat Blues and Rock & Roll and at the Juno Awards of 2009 for First Law of the Land. Green, a member of the Lac Seul First Nation from Kejick Bay, was the son of David Green, a country musician.
