Subhumans

Subhumans were a Canadian punk rock band formed in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, in 1978. Known by pejorative, punk rock nicknames, original members were known simply as "Useless" (Gerry Hannah), "Dimwit" (Ken Montgomery), "Wimpy" (Brian Roy Goble), and "Normal" (Mike Graham).
