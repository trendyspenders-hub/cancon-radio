Ariana Gillis

Ariana Gillis (born 6 November 1990) is a Canadian singer-songwriter born in Hamilton, Ontario. In 2011 Ariana launched her CD with a live appearance on the US satellite station SiriusXM. The show was hosted by Dave Marsh, author of more than 25 books on rock and roll and known as the "dean of American rock writers", who admits to being her biggest fan.
