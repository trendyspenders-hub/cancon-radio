Elaine Keillor

Frances Elaine Keillor (born 2 September 1939) is a Canadian musicologist and pianist. She has been a professor of music at Carleton University since 1977, specializing in the music of Canadian composers and the music of North American indigenous groups. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
