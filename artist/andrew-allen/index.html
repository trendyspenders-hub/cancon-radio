Andrew Allen

SpotifyOfficial site

Andrew Allen (born 6 May 1981) is a Canadian singer-songwriter from Vernon, British Columbia. Signed to Sony/ATV, he has achieved success with top-ten singles on the Canadian Adult Contemporary charts, including "Loving You Tonight". He has also collaborated with other artists, including recording the song "Where Did We Go?" with Carly Rae Jepsen. Additionally, Allen records cover songs on his official YouTube channel.
