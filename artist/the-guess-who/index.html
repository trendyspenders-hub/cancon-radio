The Guess Who is a Canadian rock band formed in Winnipeg, Manitoba, in 1958. The band found their greatest success from 1969 to 1972, with hit songs including "American Woman", "These Eyes", "No Time", "No Sugar Tonight", "Laughing", and "Share the Land". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

3 wins, 2 nominations; certified gold and platinum in Canada
