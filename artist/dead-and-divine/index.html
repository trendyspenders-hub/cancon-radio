Dead and Divine

Dead and Divine was a Canadian post-hardcore band from Burlington, Ontario. Their initial success spawned from their 2005 EP What really happened at Lover's Lane on Verona Records (a label created by the band themselves, and Silverstein's Shane Told). Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
