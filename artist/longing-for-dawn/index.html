Longing for Dawn

Official site

Longing for Dawn is a Canadian funeral doom metal band based in Montreal signed to Grau Records. The band has released three albums, the most recent of which is Between Elation and Despair, which was released in March 2009. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
