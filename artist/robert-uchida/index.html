Robert Uchida is a Canadian violinist hailed for his “ravishing sound, eloquence and hypnotic intensity” (Strings magazine). In 2013 he was appointed Concertmaster of the Edmonton Symphony Orchestra., having previously held the same position with Symphony Nova Scotia for seven years. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
