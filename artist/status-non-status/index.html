Status/Non-Status is a Canadian alternative rock band from London, Ontario, led by Anishinaabe-Canadian singer-songwriter Adam Sturgeon. The band is most noted for its 2019 album Warrior Down, which was longlisted for the 2020 Polaris Music Prize. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
