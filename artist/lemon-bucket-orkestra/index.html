The Lemon Bucket Orkestra is a Canadian self-described "Guerrilla-Folk-Punk" band based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The ensemble has received many nominations and awards such as winning the 2015 Canadian Folk Music Awards "World Group of The Year" and a nomination for the 2016 JUNO Awards "World Music Album of the Year". Lemon Bucket has also performed all over the world from WOMAD in England and New Zealand, Pohoda in Slovakia, Festival D'Été in Québec City and to New Orleans Jazz Fest in Louisiana.
