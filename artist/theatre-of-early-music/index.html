Theatre of Early Music

The Theatre of Early Music is a choir and Baroque instrumental ensemble based in Montreal, and later in Toronto. It is conducted by Daniel Taylor. The group performs and records early sacred music. Among of the group's better known pieces are various settings of the Stabat Mater. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
