I Hate Sally

I Hate Sally was a heavy metal/post-hardcore band from Ontario, Canada. It was formed in 2000, in Kingston, Ontario, by Marc Garniss, Ben Thomas, and Kelly Payne. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
