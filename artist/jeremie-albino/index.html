Jeremie Albino

Jeremie Albino is a Canadian roots rock singer-songwriter from Toronto, Ontario. The son of a Filipino father and a Québécois mother, he grew up in the Scarborough district of Toronto. After honing his musical skills while working on farms in Prince Edward County for a number of years, Albino released his debut album Hard Time in 2019. In 2021 he followed up with Blue Blue Blue, a collaborative album recorded with Cat Clyde. His second album, Tears You Hide, was released in 2023.
