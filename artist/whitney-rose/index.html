Whitney Rose

Whitney Rose is a Canadian-American country musician from Prince Edward Island, Canada. She has released six studio albums through MCG Recordings, Cameron House Records and Six Shooter Records. Her self-titled debut album Whitney Rose was released in 2012 and Heartbreaker of the Year in 2015. The EP South Texas Suite was released in 2017. Rule 62 was released in October 2017.
