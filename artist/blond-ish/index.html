Vivie-ann Bakos, born October 16, 1988, known professionally as BLOND:ISH, is a Canadian DJ, producer, and environmental activist. Known for high-energy music, she is an artist whose work focuses on inspiring change, with a stated mission to help one billion people live their best lives. She founded the nonprofit charity Bye Bye Plastic to eliminate single-use plastics from music venues and events. She is the founder of the Abracadabra event series and record label, headline resident at Pacha Ibiza, and co-founder of the nonprofit Bye Bye Plastic Foundation. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
