Richard Terfry (born March 4, 1972), better known by his stage name Buck 65, is a Canadian alternative hip hop rapper. Underpinned by an extensive background in abstract hip hop, his more recent music has incorporated blues, country, rock, folk and avant-garde influences. He is also a radio host of the weekday Drive show on CBC Music since 2008. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
