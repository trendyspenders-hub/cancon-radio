Capri Everitt

Capri Aliyah Everitt (born August 30, 2004) is a Canadian singer and children's activist who is best known for receiving a Guinness World Record for "most national anthems sung in their host countries in one year." She sang the national anthem in 80 countries between 2015 and 2016 (though only 76 counted for the record), and was eleven years old at the end of the tour. Everitt sang each country's national anthem in its own language, singing in 41 languages. The purpose of the tour was to raise money and awareness for orphaned and abandoned children, and all donations were given to SOS Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
