Random Killing

BandcampOfficial site

Random Killing is a Toronto-, Montreal-, and Oshawa-based Canadian punk rock band formed in 1984. The band originated as a group of childhood friends who began playing cover versions of their favourite punk rock songs for recreation. This developed into regular rehearsals, original songwriting, and live performances. The band recorded its debut release, Take Our Flag (1985), a 7-inch EP, and produced a music video for the title track. The video aired on Rogers Cable, Citytv News, and MuchMusic, and won a cash prize at Trinity Square Video. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
