Jory Nash

Jory Nash is a folk music-oriented Canadian singer-songwriter and musician based in Cobourg, Ontario, Canada. Nash blends elements of folk, jazz, blues, soul and pop into an original stew of sound. He plays primarily acoustic guitar and piano, and occasionally plays the 5 string banjo. Jory Nash has begun pre-production of his 10th album, to be recorded in Fall 2024 and released in April 2025.
