DVAS

DVAS (Dietzche V. & the Abominable Snowman) was a Canadian electronic dance music group formed in 2003 in Edmonton by Canadian musicians Jered Stuffco and Darren Veres. The group played their final live show on July 17, 2011. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
