Les Chiens

Les Chiens is a Canadian indie rock band from Quebec. The lead singer and guitarist is Éric J. Goulet. The bass player is Nicolas Jouannaut. Olivier Rénaldin and Marc Chartrain have both played drums with the group. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
