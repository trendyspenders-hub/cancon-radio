Syre

Syre is a Canadian rock band composed of Doug Weir (vocals) Chip Gall (guitar, keyboards, vocals), Rick Mead (lead guitar, vocals), Chris Caron (bass, vocals), and Jamie Constant (drums). The band released two albums in the 1990s, toured in Canada and the United States, as of 2017 is continuing to perform locally. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
