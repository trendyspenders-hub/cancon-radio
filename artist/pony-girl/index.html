Pony Girl

BandcampOfficial site

Pony Girl is a Canadian indie art rock band from Ottawa, Ontario, formed in 2012. Their records have appeared on North American college radio charts and in rotation on CBC Radio, CBC Music, CBC Radio 3 as well as Radio-Canada and SiriusXM. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
