The Free Spirits

Please email us at band@thefreespirits.ca for some live original video links. The Free Spirits play Prog Rock with arrangements that are full of surprises. They are Inspired by bands like #yes #gentlegiant #direstraits #jethrotull #thebeatles #jeanlucponty #thedoors, #deeppurple, and every great musician who develops their artistic craft,, Chris Birkett and Shari Tallon are a Power Duo: singer/songwriters, multi-instrumentalists, performers, and with this creative and fun collaboration, Chris Birkett uses his masterful production skills and Shari's passion for music to create a beautiful sound. This duo also loves to perform live! Source: the artist's YouTube channel
