Sarahmée

Sarahmée Ouellet (born 1988 in Dakar, Senegal), mononymously known as Sarahmée, is a Senegalese-Canadian rapper from Quebec. She is most noted for her single "T'as pas cru", which was shortlisted for the 2019 SOCAN Songwriting Prize. The sister of singer-songwriter Karim Ouellet, she released the EPs Retox in 2011 and Sans détour in 2013 before releasing her full-length debut album Légitime in 2015. She followed up with Irréversible in 2019, and received a Prix Félix nomination for Revelation of the Year at the Gala de l'ADISQ in 2019.
