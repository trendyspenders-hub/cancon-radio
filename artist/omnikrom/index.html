Omnikrom is a Canadian rap, crunk, and electro group formed in Montréal. Omnikrom is composed of two MCs, Jeanbart and Linso Gabbo, and their producer Figure8. They are also produced by Tepr, Kid Rolex and Ghislain Poirier. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
