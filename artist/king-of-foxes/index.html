King of Foxes

King of Foxes is a Canadian indie rock band formed in 2013 in Edmonton, Alberta, led by singer-songwriter Olivia Street. King of Foxes was nominated for Rock Artist of the Year at the 2022 Western Canadian Music Awards. Their third album, released September 16, 2022, titled Twilight of the Empire, went to #1 on the CKUA Radio charts. The album was produced by Stew Kirkwood and reviewed in the Edmonton Journal and Cups 'n Cakes Network, which described the album as "a sonic rollercoaster, and one of their best."
