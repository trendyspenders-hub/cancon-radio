Pukka Orchestra

Pukka Orchestra is a Canadian new wave band based in Toronto, Ontario. The group released two albums, an EP and several singles in the 1980s, including "Listen to the Radio," which hit the Canadian charts in 1984. They won a CASBY Award in 1985. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
