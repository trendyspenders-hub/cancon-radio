Papa A.P.

Papa A.P. (born January 6, 1982, in Chalatenango, El Salvador) is a singer and producer of reggaeton. His biggest hits are his cover version of "Gasolina" and "Entre tu y yo (entre toi et moi)" (featuring Linda), released in 2005. He has eight brothers and sisters. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
