Ryan Cook

Ryan Cook (born April 3, 1981) is a singer-songwriter from Nova Scotia, Canada. He has released four studio albums, three of which have been nominated for the Music Nova Scotia Awards "Album of the Year". Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
