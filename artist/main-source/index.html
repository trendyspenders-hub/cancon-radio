Main Source was a Canadian-American East Coast hip-hop group based in New York City/Toronto, composed of Toronto-born DJs and producers K-Cut and Sir Scratch, as well as New York City natives DJ J.O.D and record producer Large Professor. Later, another Queens MC, Mikey D (Michael Deering), replaced Large Professor. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
