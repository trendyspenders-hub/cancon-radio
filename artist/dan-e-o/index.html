Dan-e-o

Daniel Faraldo (born June 10, 1977), in Toronto, known professionally as Dan-e-o, is a Canadian rapper, record producer and actor of Jamaican and Spanish descent. He is a member of the groups Monolith and Perfeck Strangers, both based in Scarborough, Ontario. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
