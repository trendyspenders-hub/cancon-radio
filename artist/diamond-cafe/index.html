Diamond Cafe

Tristan Thompson (born May 22, 1998), known as Diamond Cafe, is a recording artist from Victoria, British Columbia. While critics cite his style as a "blend of synth-pop and R&B", Thompson describes his music as "bathing in a cloud of honey on a very foggy night". He has released two mid-length EPs, and one full-length album. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
