King Reign has developed his own distinct style of relating his thoughts, insights and musical feel. His delivery brings you directly into his vibe. His strong voice bellied by a smoothness that tricks the listener into relaxation as he drops stories of city life, and intuition. He believes, "I don't rep the streets, I rep the people in them".
