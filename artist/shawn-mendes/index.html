Shawn Peter Raul Mendes is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and musician. Mendes gained early recognition for posting popular song covers on the social media platforms YouTube and Vine. He later signed to Island Records and released his self-titled debut EP in 2014. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

12 wins, 19 nominations; certified diamond and gold and platinum in Canada
