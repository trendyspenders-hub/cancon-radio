Loony

LOONY is the stage name of Kira Huszar, a Canadian rhythm and blues singer from Scarborough, Ontario. She is most noted for her 2021 EP, soft thing, which was longlisted for the 2022 Polaris Music Prize. She was previously a SOCAN Songwriting Prize nominee in 2020 for her single "Some Kinda Love". Elton John took a special shine to her on his Apple Radio show "Rocket Hour", playing a handful of her songs and interviewing her on the podcast in late 2022. "I love that LOONY!" he exclaimed.
