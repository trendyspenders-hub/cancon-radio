Adam Cohen is a Canadian singer-songwriter. As a recording artist, he has released four major label albums, three in English and one in French. His most recent album, We Go Home, was released on September 15, 2014. Currently residing in Los Angeles, he was also lead vocalist of the pop rock band Low Millions from California.
