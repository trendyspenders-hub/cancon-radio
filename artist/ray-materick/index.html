Ray Materick

Official site

Raymond George Materick (born August 19, 1946) is a Canadian singer-songwriter, from Brantford, Ontario who particularly was well-appreciated in the mid-late 1970s, who continues to perform. He is best known for writing and performing "Linda Put the Coffee On", a hit single in Canada in 1975, reaching number 21. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
