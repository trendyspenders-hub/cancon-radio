Rapid Tears

Rapid Tears was a Canadian heavy metal band formed in 1977 in Toronto, Ontario. The members included founding member Michael J. Miller on guitar, Rick Nemes on drums, and Brian Frank on vocals. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
