Paul Demers

Paul Demers (March 9, 1956 – October 29, 2016) was a Canadian singer-songwriter. He was best known for writing the song "Notre Place", which came to be recognized as an anthem of the Franco-Ontarian community. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
