Meredith Shaw

Meredith Shaw (born December 4, 1981) is a Canadian radio and television personality, model, body positivity activist, style expert, and singer-songwriter based in Toronto, Ontario. She was the co-host of CityTV's Breakfast Television from 2023 to 2025. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
