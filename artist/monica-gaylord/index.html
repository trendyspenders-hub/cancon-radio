Monica Gaylord

Monica Gaylord (born 1948) is a Caribbean-Canadian pianist, harpsichordist, and teacher. In 2020, during Black History Month, she was honoured by the Government of Canada alongside Measha Brueggergosman, Oscar Peterson, Portia White, and Eleanor Collins among "notable Black people in Canada who have helped shape Canadian heritage and identity."
