Erynn Marshall

Erynn Marshall (born c. August 4, 1977) is a Canadian old-time fiddler, ethnomusicologist, teacher, and author. She is originally from Victoria, British Columbia and later lived in Gibsons, British Columbia. In 1998 she traveled to Toronto to pursue graduate studies at York University, graduating in 2003 with an M.A. degree in ethnomusicology. In 2006 she received an Appalachian Fellowship from Berea College in Kentucky, where studying Kentucky fiddle styles for three months.
