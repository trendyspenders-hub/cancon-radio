Mike Myers

Michael John Myers (born May 25, 1963) is a Canadian and American actor, comedian, filmmaker, musician, and singer. His accolades include seven MTV Movie & TV Awards, a Primetime Emmy Award, and a Screen Actors Guild Award. In 2002, he was awarded a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. In 2017, he was named an Officer of the Order of Canada for his "extensive and acclaimed body of comedic work as an actor, writer, and producer".
