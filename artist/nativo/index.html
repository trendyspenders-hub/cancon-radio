Nativo

NATIVO is a Reggae crossover band from Kiel, Germany formed in 2018 by venezuelan-born singer and guitarrist Fernando Henríquez. NATIVO often combines Latin music, Hip Hop, Arabic music, Funk as well as Blues with Reggae. The band deals with different social issues such as child poverty, climate change, migration, digitization and cultural understanding. However NATIVO also finds space in his lyrics to address fun and Caribbean dance culture. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
