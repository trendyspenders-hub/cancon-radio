Quinzy

Quinzy are a Canadian indie rock band from Winnipeg, Manitoba. They have released one album and several EPs. Many of the band's songs are about Winnipeg. The band includes lead singer and guitarist Sandy Taronno, keyboardist and vocalist Jamie Taronno, bassist and vocalist Jason Pankratz, and drummer David Pankratz. Sandy and Jamie are brothers, as are Jason and David, and the Taronnos and Pankratzes are cousins. The band originated in 2002, and were called The Good Fight, and then Sharp House Candy, before changing their name to Quinzy.
