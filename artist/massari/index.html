Massari

Sari Abboud (Arabic: ساري عبّود; born December 10, 1980), better known by his stage name Massari ( mə-SAR-ee), is a Lebanese-Canadian singer and songwriter. He began his career in the early 2000s and went on to release two R&B albums, Massari (2005) and Forever Massari (2009), before releasing two pop EPs, Hero (2015) and Tune In (2018). He is managed by SAL&CO. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
