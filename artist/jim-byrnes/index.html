James Thomas Kevin Byrnes CM OBC is an American-Canadian actor, voice actor, and blues musician. He is known to television audiences for his roles as FBI agent Daniel "Lifeguard" Burroughs on the crime drama series Wiseguy, and as Joe Dawson in the Highlander franchise. Byrnes was nominated for a Genie Award for Best Supporting Actor for his performance in the film Harmony Cats. For his music, Byrnes has won the Juno Award for Blues Album of the Year three times.
