Messiah

Messiah is a death/black and thrash metal band from Switzerland. The band has had three vocalists over its existence, with vocalist Andy Kaina's tenure the longest. Guitarist R.B. Broggi has been the band's only constant member since inception. Their sound has been described as "hopelessly un-commercial" by AllMusic and has drawn comparisons to Hellhammer and Kreator. The band's lasti influence on the development of extreme metal substantially eclipses their commercial success. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
