Eclipse

Eclipse is a Swedish hard rock band from Stockholm, formed in 1999. The band's current lineup consists of vocalist Erik Mårtensson, guitarist Magnus Henriksson, drummer Magnus "Adde" Andreasson and bassist Victor Crusner. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
