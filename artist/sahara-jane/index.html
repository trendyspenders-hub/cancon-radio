Sahara Jane

Sahara Jane is a vocalist, composer and sarangi player. She is a multi-faceted musician blending influences from Indian, Afghan and Canadian musical styles in a unique and uplifting way. In their new show an album, Something Good, Sahara Jane and rhythm master Ken Shorley create a soundscape of global electro-acoustic fusion. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
