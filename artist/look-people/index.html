Look People

Look People was a Canadian alternative rock band formed in 1985 in Toronto. They were known as "outlandishly quirky", and had a moderate hit in 1991 with a cover of War's "Lowrider". They received five CASBY Award nominations for their CD Small Fish, Big Pond. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
