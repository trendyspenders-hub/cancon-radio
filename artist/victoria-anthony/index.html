Victoria Anthony

Victoria Anthony (born August 27, 2005) is a Canadian singer-songwriter. When she was 12 years old, she sang at a Pink concert, which went viral and received attention from national news outlets. She released her first original song, "Without You", at the age of 13, and her first album, Real Life, at the age of 15. She has been featured twice in The Recording Academy's series Press Play At Home and once in their series It Goes To 11. In March 2023, she was included in People magazine's list of emerging artists.
