Acadian artist from New Brunswick — without ever confining his music to the traditional — Nick Neo writes as he pleases: in French or in Chiac, depending on his inspiration and desires. Experimenting with a thousand and one flourishing ideas, Nick doesn’t limit himself to any particular genre, exploring the musical paths that open up to him. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
