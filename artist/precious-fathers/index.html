Precious Fathers

Precious Fathers is a Canadian instrumental post-rock shoegazer ensemble, based in Vancouver, British Columbia. Its members are linked to other projects, including Destroyer, Loscil, Sparrow, and The Battles. Their music combines pulsating rhythms with multiple guitar effects and dynamic volume shifts.
