Washboard Hank

Official site

Hank Fisher (born 1954), more commonly known as Washboard Hank, is a Canadian musician, songwriter and stage performer. He was born in Peterborough, Ontario in 1954. He plays a number of instruments, including banjo, guitar and percussion. He is known for his unusual performances with self-created instruments. In 2016 he performed with his band, the Wringers. Fisher is a regular performer at children's events, including the Peterborough Folk Festival. He creates and plays improvised instruments, including his "signature" washboard. and kitchen sink tuba. He sings humorous traditional tunes Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
