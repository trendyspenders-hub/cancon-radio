Krist

Perawat Sangpotirat, nicknamed Krist, also known by the Chinese name Wang Huizhen, is a Thai actor, singer, and host under GMMTV. His breakthrough role came in 2016 with the popular boys' love series SOTUS, where he portrayed the character Arthit. His other notable series including Mint To Be, Love Beyond Frontier and Who Are You. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
