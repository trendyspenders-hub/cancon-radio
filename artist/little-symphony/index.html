Little Symphony

Little Symphony's mission is to create nature ambient music using sound recorded from across the world. We want to help others learn the art of field recording and ambient music creation in hopes to inspire more people to get into nature ambient music creation. We will be sharing short tutorials on field recording, music creation, mixing, mastering, as well as videos about our mission and the places we have been travelling to record sounds. Currently we have sounds from 6 continents, including South America, North America, Asia, Europe, Africa, and Oceania! Thank you for stopping by! Source: the artist's YouTube channel
