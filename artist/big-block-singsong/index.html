Big Block SingSong

Big Block Singsong is a Canadian children's animated musical television series and group created by Warren Brown and Adam Goddard. They are best known for their regular series of animated music videos which have aired as interstitial programming on channels such as Disney Junior in the United States, Nick Jr. in the UK, ABC Kids in Australia and CBC Kids in Canada since 2012 and as the winners of the Juno Award for Children's Album of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2020 for their album Greatest Hits, Vol. 4. It is directed, designed, and animated by Brown and composed and sung by Goddard. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
