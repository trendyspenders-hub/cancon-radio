Fernie

A Montreal native, Brazilian-Canadian Fernie shines through his vulnerable lyrics and powerful melodies. Over the past year, Fernie has toured in Quebec and around the world, playing many festivals: Osheaga, Le Festival International de Jazz de Montréal, Le Festival d'été de Québec and in Brazil (SIM São Paulo), France (Bars en Bars, MaMA festival) and the UK (The Great Escape). He was also invited to join Patrick Watson on stage during his MTelus series concert in February. Follow his socials so as not to miss new music coming in 2024. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
