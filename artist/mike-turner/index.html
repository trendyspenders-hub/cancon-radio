Mike Turner

Michael A. Turner (born June 5, 1963), also known as Emtee, is an English-Canadian musician and producer. He is best known as the former lead guitarist and founding member of the band Our Lady Peace and current member of alternative rock supergroup Crash Karma. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
