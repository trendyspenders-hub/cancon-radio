Jon McKiel is a Canadian singer-songwriter, originally from Halifax, Nova Scotia and later based in Baie Verte, New Brunswick. He is most noted for his 2024 album Hex, which was a longlisted nominee for the 2024 Polaris Music Prize. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
