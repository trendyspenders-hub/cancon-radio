Retrofit

Official site

Harboured in the Fraser Valley comes a supergroup of friends that have played music all their lives to form the one & only Retrofit. A band that’s made up of all the best parts of 70’s psychedelic folk rock & cosmic country. Retrofit is an emotional journey that sets out to bring people together to dance or get lost amongst the seasonal melancholy using lush simple chord structures & dialed in vocal harmonies that wrap around listeners like morning glory upon the dawning of a cozy Sunday.
