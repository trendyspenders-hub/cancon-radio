Lucerin Blue

Lucerin Blue were a Canadian (Goderich, Ontario) band whose music has been described as Christian rock, post-grunge, nu metal, heavy metal, and hardcore punk. Formed in 1997, they released one studio album, Tales of the Knife in 2003, with Tooth & Nail Records. The band disbanded in 2005. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
