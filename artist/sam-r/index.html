Sam-r

Sam-r is a Toronto-based Hip-Hop/Blues Artist, “A rapping drummer telling honest stories of resilience, culture, and community.” Sam-r is a Canadian artist of Lebanese-Syrian heritage, blending the raw storytelling of hip-hop with the soul of blues. Known for his rare combination of rapping and drumming, and his unique style of story telling, his music reflects the struggles and triumphs of his immigrant experience. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
