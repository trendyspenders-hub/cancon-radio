Tineta

Tineta Couturier, better known as Tineta (August 24, 1972 – June 2, 2025), was a Canadian country music artist. She released two albums on Royalty Records, Love on the Line (1991) and Drawn to the Fire (1995). Her 1995 single "Walkin' That Line" reached the Top 20 of the RPM Country Tracks chart.
