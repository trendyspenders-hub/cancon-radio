Leandra Gold

Leandra Gold is the artistic project of Colombian cellist and songwriter Natalia Bohórquez Lozano. Blending cello, voice, loops, and elements of Latin American and contemporary music, Leandra Gold explores identity, migration, longing, and the spaces between cultures and languages. Here you’ll find music, live performances, behind-the-scenes moments, and "More Than a Cellist", my DIY vlog series about the life, thoughts, creative process, and realities behind being an independent artist.
