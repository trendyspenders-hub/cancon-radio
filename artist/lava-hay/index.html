Lava Hay

Spotify

Lava Hay were a Canadian folk rock duo formed in 1989 in Toronto, Ontario, consisting of Suzanne Little and Michele Gould. Little and Gould first met in 1986, while working at Now. In 1989, they recorded an album with the intention of distributing it independently, but it soon came to the attention of Nettwerk. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
