Kyra Daniel

Kyra Daniel is a young singer/songwriter with experience and talent beyond her years. At 15 years old, she has travelled to Nashville to write and record her debut EP, toured North America with the Mini Pop Kids, and has been praised by artists like Mickey Guyton, Brittany Spencer, and Wanya Morris (Boyz II Men) for her vocal talent. Her singles 'Like It's Summer,' a catchy pop jam, and her new empowerment anthem 'Words Mean Something' are available now as well as "Be Free".
