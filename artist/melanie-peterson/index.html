Melanie Peterson

This Saskatoon born, Toronto based, singer-songwriter has quietly become one of the brightest lights on the Toronto indie folk scene. With her aching vibrato and prairie roots, her work has been consistently praised by listeners and music critics alike for her captivating voice and exceptional songwriting.
