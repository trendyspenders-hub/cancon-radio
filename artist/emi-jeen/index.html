Emi Jeen

Emi Jeen (born Emilie Bernier) is a Canadian alt-pop singer-songwriter from Saint-Jean-Port-Joli, Quebec. Her debut single, "Runaway", was released on August 7, 2020. Her debut EP, The Other Side, was released on October 30, 2020. In 2022, she gained attention after a viral TikTok video (17 million views) of her singing a cover of "Bring Me To Life" by Evanescence. She released her second project, the EP Why So Serious, on September 16, 2022.
