Brent McAthey

Brent McAthey (born August 4, 1967) is a Canadian country music artist. McAthey has released six studio albums. He reached the Top 20 of the RPM Country Tracks chart in 1998 with "Chevy Blue Eyes." Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
