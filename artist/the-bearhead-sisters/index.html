The Bearhead Sisters

The Bearhead Sisters are a musical trio from Paul First Nation, Alberta, Canada. Their album Unbreakable, won the Juno Award for Traditional Indigenous Artist of the Year at the Juno Awards of 2023. The group, consisting of sisters Allie, Trina, and Carly Bearhead, perform traditional First Nations pow-wow music. Source: the artist's YouTube channel
