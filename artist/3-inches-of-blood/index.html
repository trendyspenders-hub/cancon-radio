3 Inches of Blood is a Canadian heavy metal band formed in 1999 in Victoria, British Columbia. They disbanded in 2015 and reunited in 2023 with a line-up of Cam Pipes, Justin Hagberg, Shane Clark, Nick Cates, and Ash Pearson. Their music is influenced by the new wave of British heavy metal.
