Tearjerker

Official site

Tearjerker is a Canadian indie rock band based in Toronto, Ontario, consisting of Micah Bonte (vocals, guitar), Trevor Hawkins (drums, samples, production), and Taylor Shute (guitar, bass, vocals, keyboards). The band's music has been described as 'shoegaze' and 'dream pop'. Source: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA
