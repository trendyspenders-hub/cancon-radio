SNFU: Edmonton's Art-Damaged Hardcore Institution

Hardcore punk is supposed to burn fast and die young. SNFU — Edmonton's legendary lunatics — burned for forty years instead, and the fire never once behaved. Fronted by the inimitable Mr. Chi Pig (Ken Chinn), SNFU took hardcore's velocity and ran it through a funhouse mirror: surreal humor, art-school weirdness, and live shows that felt less like concerts than events. They became Canada's most beloved hardcore lifers — the band everyone in the scene agreed on.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

Chapter one: the Edmonton eruption (1981)

SNFU formed in Edmonton in 1981, brothers Ken and Marc Chinn at the core, and the early records — ...And No One Else Wanted to Play (1985), Better Than a Stick in the Eye (1988) — announced a band that played faster and weirder than the pack: Chi Pig's barked, absurdist? — keep it clean: Chi Pig's barked, cartoonish-yet-cutting lyrics, song titles that read like dada poetry, and musicianship that somehow kept hardcore's precision while sounding like it might fly apart. Through relentless North American touring, SNFU became a continental scene institution — the Canadian band every American hardcore kid knew.

Chapter two: the art-damaged middle

What sustained SNFU through decades was evolution without compromise: records through the 90s (Something Green and Leafy This Way Comes, Fuck You Up and Get Live?) — keep it clean: records through the 90s deepened the weirdness while keeping the speed, and Chi Pig emerged as one of punk's great frontmen — part clown, part poet, entirely unpredictable. The band's endurance itself became the statement: while hardcore scenes cycled through generations, SNFU just kept showing up, album after album, tour after tour, earning the reverence that longevity confers.

Chapter three: the long goodbye

Chi Pig's well-documented struggles shadowed the band's later years, but the reunions and final tours carried the feeling of a national institution taking its bows. When Chi Pig died in 2019, the tributes came from every corner of punk — because SNFU belonged to everyone who'd ever pogoed in a community hall from Edmonton to New Jersey? — keep it clean: to everyone who'd ever slammed in a community hall across two countries. The band's records remain entry points: the fastest, funniest proof that Canadian hardcore could be art.

Why they matter

SNFU matters because they proved hardcore could be funny, strange, and durable — that the genre's speed could carry surrealism as easily as rage. They were Edmonton's great export (the city also gave the world k.d. lang — the 'berta? — keep it clean: an impressive twin billing), and punk's great marathoners. Art damage, endlessly touring. That was the whole point.

Frequently asked questions

Who was Mr. Chi Pig?

Ken Chinn, SNFU’s legendary frontman — one of punk’s most distinctive personalities — who led the Edmonton hardcore institution until his death in 2019.

What are SNFU’s best albums?

...And No One Else Wanted to Play (1985) and Better Than a Stick in the Eye (1988) are the canonical early records.

Where is SNFU from?

Edmonton, Alberta — the band toured North America relentlessly from 1981 onward.
