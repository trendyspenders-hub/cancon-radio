“Bandages”: Hot Hot Heat's Twitchy Masterpiece, and the Island Band That Went Global

Victoria, British Columbia is known for many things; launching an international dance-punk band is not traditionally one of them. Hot Hot Heat — twitchy, wired, and unfailingly melodic — changed that in the early 2000s, and “Bandages” was the single that carried them from island clubs to the world's charts.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

The song

“Bandages” is two and a half minutes of controlled panic: Steve Bays' keyboards stab in angular shapes, the rhythm section lurches and snaps, and the famously titular chorus lands like a first-aid kit hurled across a room. The single arrived first on Sub Pop in 2002 and was re-recorded for the major-label debut Make Up the Breakdown (2002/2003), where it became an international chart record and a video staple. The lyric is characteristically oblique — romantic damage rendered as urgent triage — and the delivery is pure nervous energy, a band playing as if the song might get away from them.

The moment

Hot Hot Heat landed at the exact crest of the post-punk revival — the era of the Strokes, Yeah Yeah Yeahs, and Interpol — but with a distinctly Canadian brightness and humor. “Bandages” charted in the UK and Canada, MuchMusic and MTV played the video endlessly, and the band spent years on the international treadmill: festivals, TV, and tours that made them one of the era's most visible Canadian exports. Elevator (2005) brought “Middle of Nowhere,” their biggest single, and the Junos noticed; Victoria, improbably, had a band in the global conversation.

The afterlife

The band's later records moved toward pop polish and, eventually, an amicable wind-down, with the members moving into production and other projects. But “Bandages” has kept its voltage: it resurfaces constantly on 2000s playlists, in video-game and TV syncs, and as a reminder that Canada's contribution to the dance-punk moment wasn't peripheral — it was, for a couple of glorious years, right in the center of the mosh pit.

Frequently asked questions

Who sings “Bandages”?

Hot Hot Heat, a dance-punk band from Victoria, BC, fronted by singer-keyboardist Steve Bays.

When did “Bandages” come out?

First as a 2002 Sub Pop single, then re-recorded for Make Up the Breakdown (2002/2003), which brought international chart success.

What was Hot Hot Heat’s biggest hit?

“Bandages” internationally; in Canada, “Middle of Nowhere” (2005) also charted strongly.
