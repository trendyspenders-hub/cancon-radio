Osheaga at 20: Montreal's Island Experiment

When Osheaga launched in 2006 on Montreal's Parc Jean-Drapeau (the Expo 67 island), the bet looked risky: an indie-rock-focused festival in a market that already had the jazz festival's gravity and the FEQ's scale. Twenty years later, Osheaga is a fashion-weekend institution (the festival as style destination — the "Osheaga look" as a Montreal export), the city's summer anchor, and one of Canada's defining festival brands.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

Chapter one: the indie bet (2006–2012)

The early Osheagas built the brand on taste: the indie-rock golden age — the Strokes-era headliners, the Arctic Monkeys, the Montreal wave itself (see our Montreal features) — and the island setting (Parc Jean-Drapeau's green spaces and skyline views) gave it a visual identity. The critics' darling phase: the festival that found bands before radio did.

Chapter two: the mainstream turn (2013–2020)

The mid-period Osheagas broadened: the headliner drift toward pop and hip-hop (the bigger marquee names joining the indie core) without losing the identity (the undercard's credibility — the discovery stages). The festival became the Montreal summer weekend: the social calendar anchor (the fashion, the parties, the island).

Chapter three: the institution (2020s)

The pandemic interruption and the return confirmed Osheaga's status: a top-tier North American festival (see our features), a Montreal institution (see our city features), and the proof that an indie bet (the 2006 risk) could become a mainstay (the 2026 reality). The island holds. The fashion followed. The music never left.

Why it matters

Osheaga matters because it proved Canadian festivals could build brands — taste-led, city-anchored, internationally relevant — and because its two-decade arc (indie bet to institution) mirrors the country's own indie-era journey. The island experiment: successful. The fashion followed. The music never left.

Frequently asked questions

What is Osheaga?

A major summer music festival on Montreal's Parc Jean-Drapeau (launched 2006) — indie-rock roots, multi-genre evolution, one of Canada's defining festival brands.

Where is Osheaga held?

Parc Jean-Drapeau, the Expo 67 island in Montreal — the skyline-and-green-space setting is its signature.

Is Osheaga still indie-focused?

The undercards remain discovery-oriented; the headliners broadened to pop and hip-hop as the festival scaled.
