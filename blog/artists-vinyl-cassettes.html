Frequently asked questions

Why do artists release vinyl in the streaming era?

Margins — a $40 LP nets far more per fan than thousands of streams — plus the art-object appeal.

Why cassettes?

Cheap, immediate, lo-fi, DIY — the punk format for the physical revival's fringes.

Does physical actually sustain careers?

Yes — physical sales and merch margins are the mid-tier artist's tour-funding engine (see our income feature).
