“Stereo”: The Watchmen's Living Room Anthem

There is a particular species of Canadian rock song — unhurried, sincere, built for dorm rooms and long drives — and “Stereo” by the Watchmen is one of its purest specimens. Released in 1992 from the Winnipeg band's debut McLaren Furnace Room, it became an across-the-dial radio hit and the song a generation of Canadians filed under home.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

The song

“Stereo” opens with a jangly guitar figure and Danny Greaves' warm, conversational voice, and unfolds at a walking pace — a song about connection and escape through music, its title image doing double duty as object and metaphor. The band plays with the unflashy empathy that makes a song feel like a friend. It was the kind of single that worked on every format: rock radio, campus stations, and the adult-alternative stations that would define 90s Canadian radio. McLaren Furnace Room went gold, and the Watchmen were suddenly one of the country's most-played new bands.

The run

The follow-ups confirmed it was no fluke: In the Trees (1994) delivered “Boneyard Tree” and “All Uncovered,” Brand New Day (1996) kept the machine humming, and the band toured Canada with the workhorse ethic the era demanded — this was the decade of the Canadian club-and-theatre circuit, and the Watchmen were its house band. Greaves' songwriting — plain-spoken, melodic, and genuinely kind — made them a band people claimed personally. They never chased America; they built Canada, city by city, which is why their songs still trigger instant recognition north of the border and polite shrugs south of it.

The legacy

“Stereo” has never left Canadian radio, and its title has become shorthand for the entire early-90s Canadian rock moment — Sloan, the Watchmen, and their melodic ilk filling the country's airwaves before the alternative boom tilted everything louder. The band still plays; the song still lands; and every time it comes on in a Canadian grocery store or a prairie radio station, somebody mouths the words. That's what living-room anthems are for.

Frequently asked questions

Who sings “Stereo”?

The Watchmen, a Winnipeg rock band fronted by Danny Greaves, from their 1992 debut McLaren Furnace Room.

What were the Watchmen’s other hits?

“Boneyard Tree,” “All Uncovered,” and “Incarnate” were major Canadian radio records of the mid-90s.

Are the Watchmen still together?

The band continues to perform and record, with “Stereo” an inevitable and welcome setlist anchor.
