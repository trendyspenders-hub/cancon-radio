Long & McQuade: The Institution Behind Every Canadian Band

Ask a Canadian musician where they got their first real instrument, and the answer, statistically, is Long & McQuade — the instrument retail empire (founded Toronto, 1956) that grew from a single store into the national institution: dozens of locations across the country, the legendary rental program (the reason every Canadian band exists), and the quiet, load-bearing role of being the place where the country's music actually gets equipped.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

Chapter one: the rental miracle

Long & McQuade's founding genius was the rental program: for a modest monthly fee, a kid could take home a real instrument — a trumpet, a guitar, a drum kit — and try being a musician before anyone had to buy anything. The program's effects are incalculable: it removed the financial wall between wanting to play and playing — the wall that, in most countries, filters out everyone but the committed and the wealthy. Canada's generations of musicians got their start with a rental tag on the headstock and a practice room in the basement.

Chapter two: the service and the staff

The institution's second pillar: the staff expertise — the Long & McQuade sales floor as where Canada's musicians got their education: the amp recommendation, the repair referral, the "try this one instead" — the institutional knowledge of the country's gear, dispensed across the counter by working musicians making rent between tours. The stores' role in the ecosystem is social as much as commercial: the counter as scene hub, the bulletin board as gig listing, the staff as the scene's informal faculty.

Chapter three: the quiet empire

A store empire is an unlikely cultural monument, but Long & McQuade is exactly that: the infrastructure of the country's musicianship, the first door every player walks through, the repair shop that saves the tour, and the rental program that keeps feeding the scene its next generation. Canadian music's mythology celebrates the stages and the studios; the supply chain deserves its statue. The kid renting a bass today is the headliner of 2036. The counter's already seen the future walk in. It rents by the month.

Frequently asked questions

What is Long & McQuade?

Canada's national musical-instrument retailer (founded in Toronto, 1956) — famous for its rental program and dozens of stores across the country.

Why is Long & McQuade important to Canadian music?

Its instrument rental program removed the financial barrier to becoming a musician — the access point for generations of Canadian players.

Where was the first Long & McQuade?

Toronto — growing from a single store into the national institution.
