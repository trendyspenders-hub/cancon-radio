The Montreal Jazz Festival at 45: The World's Biggest

Every summer, downtown Montreal becomes the center of the jazz world: the Festival International de Jazz de Montréal — the Guinness-certified largest jazz festival on Earth (the two-million-attendee scale), with a dozen outdoor stages (the free concerts filling the streets) and the indoor theaters hosting the music's legends. At 45-plus years, it's not just a festival; it's Montreal's civic religion.

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

Chapter one: the founding (1980)

The festival began with Montreal's deep jazz lineage (see our Oscar Peterson feature — the Little Burgundy tradition) and a bet: that Montreal could host the world's jazz community. The early editions (the Miles? no — the era's jazz giants played) established the model: the indoor-outdoor duality (the ticketed theater shows and the free street stages), the Quartier des Spectacles as the festival district, and the public dimension (the whole city invited).

Chapter two: the scaling (1990–2010)

The growth decades made it the world's biggest (the attendance milestones, the Guinness records) and broadened the definition (the "jazz festival" as all-music festival: the funk, the world music, the pop crossovers). The indoor venues hosted the legends (the tradition of every jazz great playing Montreal); the outdoor stages made it public — the two-million attendance built on accessibility (the free stages as civic gift).

Chapter three: the institution (2010s–present)

The modern festival balances the jazz tradition (the Peterson lineage honored, the indoor theater concerts) and the breadth (the pop headliners, the global sounds) — the "jazz festival" as Montreal's all-genre summer party. The controversies (the purists vs. the populists) are managed with the diplomacy of a 45-year institution. The festival holds its ground: the world's biggest, the city's proudest, and the proof that jazz — the music this whole site treats as Canada's secret inheritance — deserves the world's largest stage.

Why it matters

It matters because it's the scale argument for jazz: the world's biggest jazz festival in the city that raised the greatest jazz pianist who ever lived — the tradition's past and present, validated by two million attendees every July.

Frequently asked questions

Is Montreal’s jazz festival the biggest in the world?

Yes — certified by Guinness as the largest jazz festival on Earth.

Is the Montreal Jazz Festival free?

The outdoor stages are free; the indoor theater concerts are ticketed.

When did the Montreal Jazz Festival start?

1980, founded by Montreal concert promoters.
