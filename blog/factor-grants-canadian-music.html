Frequently asked questions

What is FACTOR?

The Foundation Assisting Canadian Talent on Recordings — the primary public-private funder of Canadian music recordings, marketing, and touring.

Who else funds Canadian music?

The SOCAN Foundation (songwriters) and the provincial arts councils (regional ecosystems).

Do grants matter to the indie economy?

Yes — the Canadian indie economy exists because the grants exist; the records that couldn't be financed commercially get made because the public invests.
