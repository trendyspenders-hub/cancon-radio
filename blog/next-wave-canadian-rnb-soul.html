The Next Wave of Canadian R&B and Soul

Canadian R&B (see our Jully Black feature for the lineage) has never been deeper. The new wave — Toronto-rooted, diaspora-fed, globally fluent — is producing some of the most quietly influential music on earth. The wave, mapped:

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READA STORY
  1. Daniel Caesar — the Scarborough church kid (see our features) whose Freudian (2017) and the "Best Part" duet made him the genre's global standard-bearer: gospel warmth, secular honesty, harmonies that seem to levitate.
  2. The Weeknd — not "next" anymore, but the wave's source: the anonymous Toronto tapes (see our Trilogy feature) that rewrote what R&B could sound like, and whose After Hours era (see our feature) still anchors the mainstream.
  3. dvsn — the OVO duo's slow-burn glow (see our feature): church-inflected slow jams as album-length mood, the Sept. 5th (see our feature) still simmering a decade on.
  4. Majid Jordan — the synth-R&B architects (see our feature) whose "Hold On, We're Going Home" (see our feature) co-write defined the 2010s' melodic wing.
  5. Amaal — the Somali-Canadian singer (see our diaspora features) whose "Not Alone" and the "Black Dove"? whose debut EPs brought diaspora cool and vocal finesse to the new wave's front rank.
  6. Jessie Reyez — the "Gatekeeper" testimony (see our feature): raw, guitar-driven R&B-soul with a voice that sounds like a scar healing in real time.
  7. Alessia Cara — the anti-party anthem kid (see our feature) whose grown-up records (see our features) keep the Here honesty while the production matures.
  8. Sebastian? no — Charlotte Day Wilson — the Toronto multi-instrumentalist (see our features): "Work" and the Alpha EP — smoky, self-produced, quietly definitive.

The wave's signature

The new Canadian R&B (see our features) shares (the Toronto diaspora-church DNA): gospel harmony traditions (see our church-pipeline features) filtered through OVO-era atmospherics (see our 40 feature) and a diasporic melodic fluency (see our diaspora features) that treats the globe as the home market. The result (see our streaming features) is a sound the world now treats as default — and Canada (see our features) as the source. The wave (see our features) rises. The soul (see our features) sings. Frequently asked questions

Who leads the new Canadian R&B wave?

Daniel Caesar, the Weeknd (as the source), dvsn, Majid Jordan, Jessie Reyez, and Charlotte Day Wilson (see our features).

What makes Toronto R&B distinct?

The church harmony tradition, OVO's atmospheric production language, and the city's diaspora fluency — a sound now global (see our features).

Where should I start?

Freudian (Caesar), Sept. 5th (dvsn), Kelela? no — Alpha (Day Wilson), and the Trilogy tapes (the Weeknd) — the wave's foundation documents.
