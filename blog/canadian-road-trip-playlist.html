Your Ultimate Canadian Road Trip Playlist, Province by Province

The Trans-Canada (see our atlas feature) is long — 7,800 kilometers — and every kilometer deserves the right song. The province-by-province perfect playlist (see our features):

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READA STORY

The provinces, set to music

BC — "Home for a Rest" (Spirit of the West): the getting-going anthem (see our feature) — technically about coming home, functionally the departure song. Add "California Dreamin'"? no — American. Add "Go-Go Boots"? no. Add "Big Yellow Taxi"? — American. Add "The House of the Rising Sun"? — no. Add "Blame Brett"? too new. Add "Fallen Leaves"? — Billy Talent? unverified. Keep "Home for a Rest" as the BC opener and "Four Strong Winds" as the leaving-BC song.

Alberta — "Four Strong Winds" (Ian & Sylvia): the wind itself (see our feature). Add "Pass Me the..." no. Add "Sundown"? — Ontario. Add "Calgary"?" no. Add "Big League" (Tom Cochrane): the prairie dream and its cost (see our feature).

Saskatchewan — "Wheat Kings" (the Hip): the prairie night (see our feature). Add "Running Back to Saskatoon" (the Guess Who): the returning song (see our feature).

Manitoba — "One Great City!" (the Weakerthans): Winnipeg (see our feature) — hated and loved simultaneously. Add "Stand in the Rain"? no. Add "The Sensual World"? no. Keep it pure.

Ontario — "Life Is a Highway" (Tom Cochrane): the road itself (see our feature). Add "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald" for the northern leg (see our feature) and "Bobcaygeon" for the cottage country (see our feature).

Quebec — "Mon pays" (Vigneault): the winter — even in July (see our feature). Add "C'est pas parce qu'on est jeune"? unverified. Add "Hallelujah"? — Cohen for the Montreal sunset (see our feature).

New Brunswick — "Lunatic Fringe"? — no. "Fare Thee Well Love" (the Rankins): the Acadian heart (see our feature). Add "Pleine lune dans un ciel"? unverified. Keep the Rankins.

PEI — "Snowbird" (Anne Murray): the island's warmest voice (see our feature). Add "Bud the Spud" (Stompin' Tom): the harvest haul (see our feature).

Nova Scotia — "Barrett's Privateers" (Stan Rogers): the sea (see our feature). Add "The Mary Ellen Carter" for the hope (see our feature).

Newfoundland — "Rant and Roar" (Great Big Sea): the island itself (see our feature). Add "The Northwest Passage" (Stan Rogers): the arctic if you're ferry-bound (see our features).

The territories — "Northwest Passage" (Stan Rogers): the North (see our features). Add the Jerry Cans (see our features) for the living tradition.

Frequently asked questions

What’s the best Canadian road trip song?

“Life Is a Highway” is the road itself; “Four Strong Winds” is the leaving; “Home for a Rest” is the return.

What order should I play the provinces?

West to east (BC → NL) for the full TCH experience — the songs land in geographical sequence.
