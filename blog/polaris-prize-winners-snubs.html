The Polaris Music Prize: Best Winners and Biggest Snubs

Since 2006, the Polaris Music Prize (the juried, album-based, sales-blind award) has been Canadian music's conscience (see our Juno-vs-Polaris feature): the prize that ignores the charts and asks only is this album art? The winners form a canon of the century; the snubs (see our features) are the arguments that never end. Both, ranked:

BY CANCON RADIO3 MIN READA STORY

The winners, ranked

  1. **Arcade Fire, The Suburbs (2011)** — the popular winner that was also the right one (see our feature): the sprawl-album that defined the decade.
  2. **K'naan, Troubadour (2010)** — the global conscience: "Wavin' Flag" (see our feature) as proof the prize could crown reach as well as art.
  3. **Feist, Metals (2011)** — the quiet masterpiece (see our feature): Polaris's patience as a value.
  4. **Backxwash, God Has Nothing to Do With This (2020)** — the expander: horror-rap, trans identity, Montreal (see our feature) — the prize's scope, permanently widened.
  5. **Godspeed You! Black Emperor, 'Allelujah! (2013)** — the underground's coronation (see our Constellation feature): the anti-industry industry prize, won by the anti-industry band.
  6. **Kaytranada, 99.9% (2016)** — the dance prize (see our feature): electronic music's prestige moment.
  7. Beyoncé? no — Haviah Mighty, 13th Floor (2019)** — the arrival (see our feature): the new generation's claim, staked.
  8. The honorable winners — And the Conductor Wore? no — the full list (Patrick Watson's Close to Paradise (2007) (see our feature), Caribou's Andorra (2008) (see our feature), Fucked Up's The Chemistry of Common Life (2009) (see our feature), Tanya Tagaq's Animism (2014) (see our features)) — the canon, complete.

The snubs, ranked by grievance

  1. Drake and the Weeknd, every year — the streaming era's elephant (see our streaming feature): the two biggest Canadian artists of the century have never won (see our features) — the prize's art-over-commerce stance, absolute — and the debate, eternal.
  2. Jagged Little Pill (1995 — no Polaris) — the pre-Polaris masterpiece (see our Alanis feature): the prize didn't exist; the snub is history's.
  3. The Hip, never winning — the national album (see our features): Fully Completely (see our features) shortlisted in spirit; the band the jurors respected but never crowned.
  4. **The annual what-about (the shortlist's near-misses)** — the debate (see our debates feature): every year's should-have (the annual arguments are the prize's fuel).

Frequently asked questions

What is the Polaris Music Prize?

A juried Canadian award (founded 2006) for the best Canadian album, based on artistic merit alone — sales and popularity excluded.

Has Drake ever won the Polaris?

No — nor has the Weeknd; the prize's art-over-commerce stance has passed over the streaming era's biggest stars (see our features).

Who are the most important Polaris winners?

Arcade Fire (The Suburbs), Feist (Metals), Kaytranada (99.9%), and Backxwash (see our features) anchor the canon.
