Skratch Bastid: The DJ's DJ

Skratch Bastid (Paul Murphy) — the Halifax-raised, Toronto-based turntablist — is the DJ's DJ: the three-time Scribble Jam champion (the world's most prestigious DJ battle), the mixer whose routines are studied by students of the craft, and the producer whose party-rocking sets bridge the underground and the mainstream. The story of Canada's greatest living turntablist:

BY CANCON RADIO2 MIN READAN ORAL HISTORY

Chapter one: the Halifax apprenticeship

Murphy came up through Halifax's DJ scene (see our Halifax features) — the all-ages parties, the mixtapes, the battles. He won his first Scribble Jam (Cincinnati) in 2001, then defended the title twice (2002, 2003) — an unprecedented three-peat that announced a new standard in turntable musicianship. The routines — the technical precision, the humor, the musicality — elevated the battle DJ from technician to artist.

Chapter two: the mixer and producer

The transition from battle DJ to working musician: Skratch Bastid became the DJ of choice for Canadian hip-hop tours (see our hip-hop features), the remixer for Canadian acts (see our collaborations feature), and the producer whose original tracks (the Bubblin' series, the Spring Up EPs) brought the party-rocking aesthetic to record. The Back to the Basics mixtape series became a canonical document of Canadian DJ culture — the education and the party in one package.

Chapter three: the keeper of the craft

Skratch Bastid's legacy is the craft itself: he teaches (the workshops, the masterclasses), he mentors (the next generation of Canadian turntablists), and he keeps the DJ-as-musician tradition alive in an era of controllers and sync buttons. The three-time champion still battles (the Red Bull 3Style, the exhibitions), still tours (the festival main stages), and still represents the idea that turntablism is music — composed, performed, and judged on the merits. The records spin. The cuts land. The DJ's DJ holds the standard.

Why he matters

He matters because he proved the DJ could be an artist — not a technician, not a human iPod, but a musician whose instrument happens to be two turntables and a mixer. Three Scribble Jam titles. Decades of craft. The standard-bearer.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Skratch Bastid?

Paul Murphy — a Canadian turntablist, three-time Scribble Jam champion, producer, and DJ culture ambassador.

What is Scribble Jam?

The world's most prestigious DJ battle (held in Cincinnati), where Skratch Bastid won three consecutive titles (2001–2003).

Is turntablism still alive in Canada?

Yes — through practitioners like Skratch Bastid, who teaches, mentors, and performs the craft internationally.
