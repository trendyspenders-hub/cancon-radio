Frequently asked questions

What albums are best for driving across Canada?

Harvest, Moving Pictures, Fully Completely, and The Suburbs anchor the list — terrain-matched to the provinces.

How do I sequence the albums?

Match the record to the terrain: prairie albums for the prairies, mountain music for the mountains.

Roughly 7,800 kilometers — about 80+ hours of driving, so bring a deep playlist.
