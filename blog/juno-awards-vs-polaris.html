Juno Awards vs. Polaris: Two Visions of Canadian Excellence

Canada has two big music prizes (see our Polaris feature), and they disagree about almost everything: the Juno Awards (the industry (see our industry features) — the industry's sales-and-broadcast mainstream) and the Polaris Music Prize (the juried, artistic, sales-blind conscience). Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between the Junos and the Polaris?

The Junos are the industry-voted, sales-and-broadcast-celebrating mainstream awards; the Polaris is a juried, sales-blind prize for artistic merit.

Which matters more?

They measure different things — the Junos, popular impact; the Polaris, artistic achievement — and the culture needs both lenses.

Has any album won both?

Yes — several (see our features) have won the Polaris and received major Juno recognition; the two prizes converge on genuine consensus classics.
