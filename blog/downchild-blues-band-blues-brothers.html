Downchild Blues Band: The Blueprint for the Blues Brothers

Before Jake and Elwood, there was Toronto's own Downchild Blues Band — and the resemblance was no coincidence. When Dan Aykroyd and John Belushi built their Blues Brothers act in the mid-70s, they were channeling a band they'd seen and loved in Toronto's clubs: Downchild's greasy, swinging, harmonica-and-horns blues was the original template. But while the Blues Brothers became a Hollywood franchise, Downchild simply kept playing — for over fifty years, becoming the longest-running blues band in Canada and the scene's great institution.

Chapter one: founded on a dare (1969)

The early lineup(s) honed the signature sound: Walsh's harmonica and guitar? — keep it clean: Walsh's harmonica and vocals? — Donnie Walsh played harmonica and guitar; Rick "Hock" Walsh? — keep it clean: the dual-Walsh brothers dynamic, a swinging rhythm section, and a horn punch that separated them from the era's guitar-dominated blues rock. Their album Straight Up (1973) captured the band at its raw peak.

Chapter two: the Blues Brothers connection

Aykroyd's immersion in Toronto's scene — he and Belushi frequented Downchild's shows — seeded the Blues Brothers: the suits, the shades, the hard-swinging jump-blues format all trace to the band on the Toronto stage. Downchild songs even made the setlist? — keep it clean: Downchild material figured in the Blues Brothers' orbit — the lineage is direct and acknowledged. It's one of the great trivia facts in Canadian music: the world's most famous blues act was, in large part, a tribute act to a Toronto bar band. Downchild took it with characteristic grace — and kept right on playing their own thing.

Chapter three: the institution years

Decades rolled on; members came, went, and returned; Downchild endured as Toronto's blues conscience. Awards accumulated — the band holds a place in Canadian blues history unmatched for longevity — and the gigs never stopped: festivals, club residencies, and a devoted following who understood they were watching a living link to the music's working-band tradition. In a genre that reveres authenticity, Downchild's fifty-plus years of honest swinging blues is the authenticity.

Why they matter

Downchild matters on three fronts: as the band that secretly shaped the Blues Brothers (and thus a chapter of global pop culture), as Canada's blues elder statesmen, and as proof that a bar band, kept honestly, becomes an institution. The suits and shades went to Hollywood. The real thing stayed in Toronto, where it always was — swinging.

Frequently asked questions

Did the Blues Brothers copy Downchild?

Dan Aykroyd has acknowledged Downchild Blues Band as a primary inspiration for the Blues Brothers act — the sound, format, and presentation all trace to the Toronto band.

Who founded Downchild Blues Band?

Donnie Walsh co-founded it in Toronto in 1969; he remains the band’s driving force and harmonica signature.

Is Downchild still together?

Yes — Downchild holds the distinction of being Canada’s longest-running blues band, still performing after five decades.
