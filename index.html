WHAT “CANCON” MEANS

One rule built
this station.

Since 1971, Canadian radio has lived by one rule: roughly a third of everything it plays must be Canadian. “CanCon” is that promise — music written, performed or recorded here, kept on the dial by design, not by accident. Every station on this site meets the test.
