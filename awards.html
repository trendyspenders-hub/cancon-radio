COVERAGE & SOURCES

What this archive contains

Nomination records as published: artist, category, ceremony, year and result (winner or nominee). Totals differ between ceremonies because coverage differs — not necessarily because data is missing.

Missing years and gaps

“Year not recorded” means the consulted source didn't name the ceremony: the entry stays visible, separate from dated years. A year absent from a list means not yet in this archive — not that no ceremony happened.

Artists, albums, songs, videos

Categories distinguish artist, album, song, music-video and industry honours. Joint credits are listed under each named recipient.

Status of a recent ceremony

Nominees announced before results appear as “Nominee”; “Winner” is applied only once a result is published and verified.
