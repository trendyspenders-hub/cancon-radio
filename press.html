BOILERPLATE

CanCon Radio is the all-Canadian broadcast: one continuous radio station streaming the country's music from the 1920s to this week, tuned by era, mood, genre and province. Every play happens on the artist's own official channel, so every listen counts for the artist. The platform pairs the player with a sourced directory of Canadian acts, an archive of national chart history from RPM to the Canadian Hot 100 plus campus-radio charts, award databases spanning the JUNOs, ADISQ, the ECMAs and the Polaris Prize, Music Canada certifications, and daily-refreshed concert listings. No account is required to listen; accounts exist only for saving.
